# The Two Tits

Inn on the western side of the market square in [[Amberhold]].

In [[Session 37]], [[Deanira]] observed patrons gathering there, pairing off, and disappearing into the basement. Some wore phallic pins, while one woman carried a staff topped by an explicitly sexual wooden knot. Deanira suspects a possible connection to [[Daddy Dildo Daggins]]'s order or his collection of fertility statues.

In [[Session 38]], Deanira confirmed that the gathering was the [[Union of Many]], an adult religious community devoted to multiple deities of love and fertility. The group meets in the basement about once a week, and Daggins was a member known as Brother Daggins.
