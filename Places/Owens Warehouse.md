# Owens Warehouse

[[Richard Owens]]'s warehouse on the riverfront docks of [[Amberhold]]. Its cellar holds [[Peter Owens]]'s hidden laboratory ([[Session 39]]). It's the warehouse that the stranded ship in [[Session 36]] couldn't unload into.

## Layout ([[Session 39]])
- **Arrival.** Touching the orb on the [[Owens Family]] crest in the mausoleum crypt teleports you into a dark cellar: stone floor, with the underside of a wooden floor for a ceiling.
- **Crate labyrinth.** Crates of cloth, with delivery notes addressed to Lord Richard Owens's warehouse, are stacked high and laid out on purpose as a maze. Five glowing spheres turn visible, shoot at intruders, then float back among the boxes; they attacked several times.
- **Laboratory.** Hanging lights, four cages built into the walls, two operating tables with sheets over bodies, and a desk of notes. See [[Peter's Experiments]].
- **Exit.** A door out onto the riverfront. From outside it looks like part of the wall. The party saw the city's lights and felt sure they were still in Amberhold.
