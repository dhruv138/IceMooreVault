# Temple of Mistra

Temple on or near the campus of [[Mistra's Academy]] in [[Amberhold]]. [[Samuel Patel]] serves there. In [[Session 37]], its clerics cured [[WISC]] and [[Furgus]] of lycanthropy and diagnosed [[Deanira]]'s minor curse.
