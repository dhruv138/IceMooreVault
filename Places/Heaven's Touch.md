# Heaven's Touch

New brothel in [[Amberhold]] competing with the [[Velvet Glove Brothel]]. Known for magically/uncannily matching clients with preferences.

In [[Session 38]], [[Theo'din Corvis|Theo]] detected an enchantment aura settled over people inside, including [[Zelda]]. When he attempted to cast *dispel magic*, staff removed him because spellcasting was prohibited and asked the party to leave. The source, effect, and intent of the enchantment remain unknown.
