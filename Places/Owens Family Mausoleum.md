# Owens Family Mausoleum

Family mausoleum of the Owens family, in a graveyard to the west outside [[Amberhold]].

- [[Session 38]]: [[Peter Owens]] has been seen visiting more often since his parents died. As the party approached in the rain, a cloaked, hooded figure entered the structure and vanished. See [[Owens Family Deaths]].

## Recent developments
- [[Session 39]]: Inscription over the door: "OWENS — WHAT THE SEA GIVES, THE SEA KEEPS". Holds the new coffins of Lord Richard and Lady Margaret Owens.
- [[Session 39]]: Bartholomew Owens's coffin slides aside on hidden rollers onto a spiral stair. Below is an old brick tunnel, older than the sewers, leading to a round chamber with drains on every side, where the [[Missing Sanitation Workers]] were chained.
- [[Session 39]]: The [[Man in the Grey Mask]] watched the party from the graveyard wall.
