# Owens Family Mausoleum

Family mausoleum of the Owens family, in a graveyard to the west outside [[Amberhold]].

- [[Session 38]]: [[Peter Owens]] has been seen visiting more often since his parents died. As the party approached in the rain, a cloaked, hooded figure entered the structure and vanished. See [[Owens Family Deaths]].

## Recent developments
- [[Session 39]]: Lit inside, with four sarcophagi. Stepping in triggered a magical "hook behind the navel" that teleported [[Riffle]] and [[Deanira]] into sealed sarcophagi filling with poison gas. [[Furgus]] felt the tug but didn't move, "as if something is preventing him". [[WISC]] lifted the lids to free them.
- [[Session 39]]: One sarcophagus is wood painted to look like stone. Inside, a ladder leads down to a **hidden crypt**, also lit, with six coffins: Harold and Margaret Owens, Nicholas and Elizabeth Brightvine, and Lord Richard and Lady Gezibel Owens. The last two were empty. There was no smell of decay.
- [[Session 39]]: The [[Owens Family]] crest is painted on the crypt wall. Touching the orb in the mermaid's hand teleports you to the [[Owens Warehouse]].
