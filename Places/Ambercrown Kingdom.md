# Ambercrown Kingdom

Kingdom ruled by King Xavier, who took the throne shortly before its war with the [[Dragon League]] about 15–20 years ago. The war lasted only a few months and the Dragon League lost ([[Session 36]]). This year the King's birthday coincides with the Harvest Moon festival, making for a bigger celebration in [[Amberhold]] ([[Session 24]]).

## Recent developments
- [[Session 40]]: Per [[Talir Rengavi]], many Lords accept the current King, but many miss his father's weak rule, when they went largely unchecked. Lord [[Erik Bufford]] is among those pushing for more power. [[Mistra's Academy]] is a third, unofficial power. A dead Lord ([[Peter Owens]]) is therefore delicate.
- [[Session 41]]: Dockworkers were told a guarded ship's cargo is for the King's birthday. See [[Albatross Mystery]].
