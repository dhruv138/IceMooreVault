# Missing Red Robe

One of the 12 old-pattern red ceremonial robes kept in [[Storeroom B]] of the [[Library of Deneir]] is missing ([[Session 39]]). The [[Red Robes Ledger Page]], found on a [[Skeletal Hand]] acolyte, had the line circled with "11" written beside it.

## Why it matters
- [[Daggins Murder]]: a witness saw someone resembling [[Riffle]] in red vestments enter Daggins's house.
- [[Talir's Stolen Documents]]: [[Siobhan]] saw a kenku in red robes, "maybe a bit taller" than Riffle, enter Talir's office.
- Someone with Library access, or the Skeletal Hand, appears to be impersonating Riffle in a stolen robe. That is the party's working theory, not established.

**Status:** unresolved.
