# Blond Skeletal Hand Recruiter

A blond young man who appears to recruit for the [[Skeletal Hand]] in [[Amberhold]].

## Evidence
- The cultist questioned with Speak with Dead in the cave where [[Christolf Nightfall]] was killed said a young blond man recruited him in Amberhold.
- [[Cam Jergins]] copied his Skeletal Hand tattoo from a small, blond young "kid" ([[Session 36]]).
- [[Session 37]]: A job-board flyer appears as a request for help moving boxes to Academy-associated people, but as a private scholarship offer to everyone else. It says to go to a particular tavern, order a beer, an ale and a wine at once, and wait. A blond young man approached the party, said those not admitted to the Academy would be hired for quests, and left. [[Furgus]] saw a Skeletal Hand tattoo under his sleeve. The party believes he used Disguise Self on leaving, and a black-haired woman they didn't recognise followed him out.
- [[Session 38]]: [[Sabrina]] described a young blond man calling himself [[Andrew]] who saw a Velvet Glove worker named Angela. The party wonders if he's the same person.

## Party plans
- [[Riffle]] is considering ordering the drinks again after several days to see whether they are approached.

**Status:** unresolved.
