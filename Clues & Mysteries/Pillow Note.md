# Pillow Note

On the night [[Peter Owens]] died ([[Session 40]]), [[Riffle]] found a note on their pillow reading: **"What secrets is Talir keeping from you?"**

[[Deanira]] and Riffle stayed up listing signs that [[Talir Rengavi]] **is** keeping secrets:
- He had a secret safe Riffle didn't know about (see [[Talir's Stolen Documents]]).
- He is involved in the politics of the city and region.
- Riffle still doesn't know what is going on between him and [[Bianca Onyxcrest]].
- He had a surprise contact in the [[Royal Guard]].

## Open questions
- Who left the note, and how did they get into Riffle's room?
- Is it connected to whoever stole from Talir's safe while impersonating Riffle?

**Status:** unresolved.
