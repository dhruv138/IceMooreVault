# Floating Island Mystery

[[Val]] searched for a floating island whose position constantly changes. Attempts to map it cause the mark to disappear.

Val believed ruins on the island could explain:
- the origin of the world
- why the stars change

Val disappeared while searching for it.

[[Deanira]] continues to pursue this mystery.

## Session 37 reports

Sailors in [[Amberhold]] gave Deanira two new accounts:

- Near the Barren Toll, witnesses saw lights that seemed to be entering port even though they were days from port. Nothing was there at sunrise.
- Two days out from Wellview Helm, witnesses saw an island in the distance that had vanished by the next day.

The reports came from widely separated locations, supporting the possibility that the island moves or appears unpredictably.
