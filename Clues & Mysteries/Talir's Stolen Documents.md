# Talir's Stolen Documents

At the end of [[Session 38]], [[Talir Rengavi]] sent [[Riffle]] a furious Sending message. He demanded that Riffle return documents stolen from his safe that afternoon, saying Archivist [[Siobhan]] saw Riffle enter his office.

Riffle did visit Talir that day, before helping [[Karyn]] tidy her office. The notes don't say whether Riffle was ever in Talir's office alone or went near the safe. This is the second time a witness has placed someone resembling Riffle at a theft. Compare the framing in the [[Daggins Murder]].

## Open questions
- ~~What documents were taken?~~ The Gala vault's warding plans ([[Session 39]]).
- Who did Siobhan see, and can she be trusted?
- Is this the same person or group who framed Riffle for Daggins's murder?

**Status:** unresolved.

## Recent developments
- [[Session 39]]: **What was stolen:** the plans for the warding glyphs on the display vault where the [[Mystery Stone|stone]] will be shown at the [[Academy Gala]]. Whoever has them knows exactly how the stone is protected.
- [[Session 39]]: **Riffle cleared.** Riffle was tidying [[Karyn]]'s office at the time, and Karyn confirmed it. [[Talir Rengavi|Talir]] apologised.
- [[Session 39]]: [[Siobhan]] still says she saw a kenku in RED robes enter the office and leave by the east stair, but admits it was "maybe a bit taller" than Riffle. One old red robe is gone from [[Storeroom B]]; see [[Missing Red Robe]].
