# Man in the Grey Mask

Tall, cloaked figure in a grey mask.

## What the party knows ([[Session 39]])
- The captives beneath the [[Owens Family Mausoleum]] were taken to him one at a time. [[Hollis]] never came back.
- [[Gertrude Mossbottom|Gert]] heard him say he needed "fresh ones, not old ones" for "the night of the stone".
- He watched the party from the graveyard wall as they left, then walked off into the rain. Nobody got a good look.

## Open questions
- Is he the [[Master of Death]]? The party wonders, but nothing connects them yet.
- Is "the night of the stone" the [[Academy Gala]], where the [[Mystery Stone]] will be shown? That's an inference.

**Status:** unresolved.
