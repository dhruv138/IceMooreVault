# Peter's Experiments

What [[Peter Owens]] was doing in his laboratory under the [[Owens Warehouse]] ([[Session 39]]).

## What the party found
- **Two living captives** in two of the four cages, slumped over. They are breathing, but their eyes are open and vacant, and they have soiled themselves. [[Riffle]] judged that what was done to them is **supernatural, not medical or necromantic**.
- **Two operating tables** with sheets drawn over bodies. Under one, [[Deanira]] saw part of an incredibly muscular arm with large nails.
- **A desk** full of scrap paper covered in arcane equations, with anatomy books and physiology papers about various animals in the drawers.
- **A blacked-out page** where only the words "brass shield and the sapphire of the plains" remain.
- **"Need female phase"**, underlined three times.
- **A list of names**, all crossed out, followed by "**Deanira? [[Coda's Landing]]**". Deanira's name and hometown were next.
- **Teleport traps.** When Deanira lifted the sheet, she felt the same tug behind her navel that had pulled her into a sarcophagus at the mausoleum, but resisted it this time.
- **Greater zombies.** Peter had raised his own parents as greater zombies ([[Richard Owens]], [[Gezibel Owens]]).

## Open questions
- What is Peter trying to make, and what is the "female phase"?
- Why Deanira, and why Coda's Landing?
- What are the "brass shield" and the "sapphire of the plains"?
- Who are the two captives? [suspicion] They could be among the [[Missing Sanitation Workers]], but nothing in the notes says so.
- Did Peter kill his parents, or only raise them after they died? See [[Owens Family Deaths]].
- [suspicion] Is this connected to the [[Skeletal Hand]]? A greater zombie also served [[Christolf Nightfall]] ([[Nastil]]), and the recruiter was a youth named Peter ([[Blond Skeletal Hand Recruiter]]). Nothing found in the lab names them.

**Status:** unresolved.
