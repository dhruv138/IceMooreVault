# Union of Many

## Summary

A religious community devoted to multiple deities of fertility and love, including Shialliah, Lathander, and Melora. Members emphasize open love, strong relationships across their community and divine traditions, loving life, and living fully.

## Locations

- [[The Two Tits]] — members gather in the basement about once a week to worship.

## Party Relationship

[[Deanira]] met [[Atheela]] and discussed [[Daddy Dildo Daggins|Brother Daggins]], who belonged to the order.

## Party Knowledge

- The order was devastated by Brother Daggins's death.
- Daggins's collection of fertility statues represented the order's many deities.
- Before worship, members cast *zone of truth* and confirm that everyone present is at least 18.

### [[Session 38]]

Deanira entered the group's meeting beneath The Two Tits, learned its name and beliefs, and showed Atheela the [[Silver Dildo]] Daggins had given her as collateral.
