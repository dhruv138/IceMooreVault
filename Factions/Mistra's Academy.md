# Mistra's Academy

Major magical academy in [[Amberhold]]. Connected to [[Bianca Onyxcrest]], [[Hambrick Onyxcrest]], and the historical [[Skeletal Hand]].

## Recent developments
- [[Session 37]]: Campus includes the [[Temple of Mistra]] and a Student Health Clinic. Students range widely in age and background.
- [[Session 38]]: Preparing the [[Academy Gala]] and the [[Gauntlet]]. Graduate student [[Peter Owens]] is on leave after his parents' deaths.
- [[Session 39]]: Graduate student [[Peter Owens]] was captured after raising his parents as greater zombies. The Academy's job board request for a live mimic is on the party's to-do list ([[Acquire a Live Mimic]]).
- [[Session 40]]: [[Talir Rengavi]] calls the Academy a third, unofficial power alongside the Crown and the Lords: generally neutral, but some Lords think it concentrates too much magic. He fears Peter's death could look like an Academy-linked move against the Lords.
