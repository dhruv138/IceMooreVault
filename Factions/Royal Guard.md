# Royal Guard

The King's guard and garrison in [[Amberhold]] (see [[Ambercrown Kingdom]]).

- [[Session 40]]: [[Talir Rengavi]] has a contact in the royal garrison. The cover story for [[Peter Owens]]'s death is that he died resisting a royal arrest. Next morning, many royal guards were posted around the [[Owens Warehouse]].
- [[Session 41]]: Royal guards approached the party in the evening and asked them to come along. Talir, told by Sending, replied "proceed."
