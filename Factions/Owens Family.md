# Owens Family

Wealthy merchant family of [[Amberhold]], with a ship and a [[Owens Warehouse|warehouse]] on the riverfront docks ([[Session 36]], [[Session 39]]).

**Crest:** a ship whose figurehead is a mermaid holding an orb in her outstretched hand. The crest painted in the family's hidden crypt is magical: touching the orb teleports you to the [[Owens Warehouse]] ([[Session 39]]).

## Members
- [[Richard Owens|Lord Richard Owens]] and [[Gezibel Owens|Lady Gezibel Owens]]: died recently; raised by their son as greater zombies, and destroyed by the party ([[Session 39]]).
- [[Peter Owens]]: their son, captured by the party ([[Session 39]]).
- Harold Owens and Margaret Owens: buried in the family crypt ([[Session 39]]). How they're related to Richard isn't stated.
- Nicholas Brightvine and Elizabeth Brightvine: also buried in the Owens crypt ([[Session 39]]). Their connection to the family isn't stated.

See [[Owens Family Mausoleum]], [[Owens Family Deaths]].
