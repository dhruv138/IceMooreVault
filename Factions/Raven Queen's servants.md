# Raven Queen's servants

Religious network opposing necromancy. [[Theo'din Corvis]] serves the Raven Queen and investigates northern necromantic activity.

## Recent developments
- [[Session 37]]: The **Order of the Black Feathers** holds that the dead should stay dead. Calling people back from the Queen's realm through divine intervention may be acceptable, but death should be respected as a process. Followers should intervene against dark magic that steals souls from the beyond or desecrates the bodies left behind. [[Theo'din Corvis]] recalled these tenets while communing at the [[Temple of the Raven Queen]].
