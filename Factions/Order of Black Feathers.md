# Order of Black Feathers

An order serving the [[Raven Queen]] whose tenets [[Theo'din Corvis]] recalled during [[Session 37]]. The order teaches that:

- The dead should remain dead.
- Death must be respected as a natural process.
- Return from death through divine intervention may be acceptable.
- Members should oppose dark magic that steals souls, improperly raises the dead, or desecrates corpses and bodily vessels.

Theo concluded that the party's opposition to necromantic forces gives them common cause with the order.
