# Raven Queen

[[Theo'din Corvis]] serves the Raven Queen and describes her as strongly opposed to necromancy.

Theo's personal history involves a bargain his mother made with the Raven Queen to save her life, at the cost of one of her twins.

In [[Session 37]], Theo communed with her in a magically dark and silent chamber. She appeared wearing a white mask, black dress, and raven-feather cloak. The experience recalled the tenets of the [[Order of Black Feathers]] and reinforced his opposition to magic that steals souls or desecrates the dead.
