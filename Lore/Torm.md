# Torm

Lawful good god associated with duty, loyalty, courage, and self-discipline. In [[Session 37]], [[Riffle]] learned that a religious sect claiming to worship Torm had been breaking into and damaging businesses that sell alcohol in [[Amberhold]]. Their exact identity and motives remain unknown.
