# Session 7 — 1 June 2025

We leave the [[Black Dragon Inn]] at 11 am to head back towards [[Truemetal Halls]]. As we travel along the mountainous canyon [[Tryborne Pass]], we hear a cacophony of ravens around a curve in the distance. [[Furgus]] sneaks ahead and looks around the curve. He sees bashed carts and Cuban fedora hats surrounded by ravens picking at the bodies of the Cubans. We approach carefully and investigate the method in which the animals and Cubans were slain. They are covered in arrows and the occasional slashes.
[[Furgus]] notices that the arrows are larger than the ones you’d typically see. As we approach one of the carts to investigate it, the ravens attack. After a few rounds, the ravens are scared off by 3 approaching dire wolves, who are lured to us by the smell of blood. They attack. After defeating the dire wolves, we rummage through the carts and find the oxen butchered with all of the choice cuts taken. On one of the bodies shielding a dead teenaged boy is a bloodstained note (on the following page).
We move on out of the canyon and find ourselves into a forest with a fog rolling in as the evening falls. It’s strange that the fog is rolling in at this time of day, and something about this feels very unnatural. Along the edge of the forest, [[Deanira]] sees a ghostly silhouette of the dead Cuban. [[Oswald]] from the note. He beckons for us to follow, and [[Deanira]] prances after him. He leads her 100 yards off the trail, and we come across a building. The house looks beautiful from one angle and decrepit from another. When it’s decrepit, the sign above it says the “[[First Star]],” and when it’s pristine, the sign reads “[[Unfinished Business]].” As soon as we cross the threshold, the environment changes. Where it looked rotten and derelict before, it transforms into a lively tavern. 3 individuals are sitting and a bartender is cleaning [[Glasses]]. He hollers out to us, “Ah, we have three live ones!” We are welcomed
in to [[Unfinished Business]], the Business for wayfaring souls that aren’t ready to pass on. The bartender introduces himself as [[Anton]]. He was killed in a bar fight the first night he opened his bar. Now he caters to wandering souls. They never know where or when the bar will appear to mortals. The three other people are [[Oswald]], an older half orc dressed in plate, and a young half elf in the equivalent of his early 20s. [[Deanira]] approaches [[Oswald]] to speak to him. Dhruv talks to the half elf and [[WISC]] talks to the half orc in plate. [[Oswald]] says [[Mary Salter]] is in [[Lakeshire]], his hometown. He asks [[Deanira]] to send her word (write) of his and their son’s passing. He said they were ambushed by huge bald humanoids with tattoos firing arrows. They then repelled down and finished them off with axes and swords. He asks for us to avenge him and his son, then use a courier to send word to Mary in [[Lakeshire]]. Their goods included salted fish, cigars, and fine rum.
NOTE: the dead have no sense of time. We can wait to do these quests. We may not even necessarily be the proper level for these quests yet.
Refer to the Quests page for details.
We are offered lodging for the night. When we wake up in the morning, we find ourselves on the forest floor, the tavern vanishing overnight. We continue on our way to [[Truemetal Halls]], and come across a berg, a single space that is heavily fortified and food is stored where a town will band together in the winter for protection. It’s the time of the year where the bergs are being prepared for when winter comes.
We drop off [[Elzock Emberstone]] at the [[Ring of Gold]] to return him to his family.
We’re not let into the neighbourhood, so we’re promised payment and head to [[The Warhammer]]. We sit at the bar and tell [[Gunther Rockhammer]] of our travels. He pays us and tells us to stick around because there may be someone in the city who wants to talk to us. He gives us a room on the house and we level up.
A new shop has opened up in [[Truemetal Halls]] called [[The Shooting Star]], buys and sells magical items - Rupert Bubblegum
Backstory
[[Deanira]] was born in a coastal village where the tides were strange and the stars never matched the charts. She didn’t have parents in particular, the culture of the tribe was that the children were raised by the whole community, not just by one or two. She was very close to a kind man named [[Val]], who was a handsome and gruff satyr in his late 40s/early 50s. She would sit with him and look over his shoulder as a small child and watch him draw exquisite maps. He taught her some cartography skills over the years. He would vanish over the horizon now and then, to chart what was beyond the line, especially looking for this floating island whose position eternally changed and no map could hold. If you charted it on a map, the marking on the map would vanish as well. Some of the villagers thought he was mad, but [[Deanira]] thought he could see what no one else could. He was convinced that there were ruins on the island that would explain the origin of the world and why the stars change. One day he never came back. It’s taken everything in [[Deanira]] to hold herself back from sailing off to find him and his island. Everyone insisted that she drop the subject or the island would drive her mad, like it had to someone each generation. Now she’s honing her map making skills by travelling the Icemoor mountains and charting ruins, so that one day she may become skilled enough to find that island, and maybe find him too.
