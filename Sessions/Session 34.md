# Session 34 — 27 July 2026

Looking at the Stone Giant were-bears, we see fresh brands on each person’s cheek in runes that translate to “kin slayer”. They have tattoos but don’t seem significant for our purposes. We decide to use Speak With Dead to get some information from them. They said that they received their were-bear curse when they were bitten by an unknown creature. They say that they are from the [[Moaning Gorge]] far to the north in a volcanic valley in the mountains. We ask about the brands on their cheeks and they say that on the full moon their anger was unleashed beyond their control and killed their kin. Lycanthropes can transform at will, but during a full moon they transform regardless. Once you get bit, there is a ticking clock to that first full moon. Up until that first full moon it is possible to cure it. After that it’s too late and only Divine Intervention, Wish Spell, and killing the Prime Lycanthrope that turned you. You can get lycanthropy by being bitten by another lycanthrope, it can be self-inflicted by a very rare and dangerous ritual, or “lycanthrope prime” which are far stronger, immortal, and are are turned by being cursed by extraplanar being or something of the like.
[[Theo'din Corvis]] and [[Furgus]] collect vials of were-bear blood. We camp overnight just past the tree-line a little ways away from the stone giant camp.
[[Deanira]] takes the first watch, then [[Theo'din Corvis]] takes the next watch. While he is sitting, he sees the silhouettes of 5 small figures against the fire. He approaches and sees 5 [[Cornhusk Dolls]], now including him. This is his first time seeing them and now he’s freaked.
In the morning, [[Furgus]] and [[WISC]] check their wounds, remembering that they were bitten during the battle. The wounds aren’t healing. [[Deanira]] also got bit, but her wounds have healed fine. The full moon was a few days prior, we have about a month until it’s too late.
We get back to [[Venburg]] and check on Hanz and [[Philip]] and speak with [[Klaus]] about them doomed to transform into were-bears. While we fuck around, [[Riffle]] casts Sending to [[Hana Goldfirm]] and asks for the nearest holy spring and updates. She says she’ll do some research and for
[[Riffle]] to contact her again in a few days. [[Riffle]] Sends a message to [[Talir Rengavi]] and asks if [[Bianca Onyxcrest]] is able to help with the were-bear situation. He responds and says he’s sure [[Bianca Onyxcrest]] could help but that [[Riffle]] should ask her directly. He says if worse comes to worst, kill the afflicted party members. [[Deanira]] kindly but honestly explains the situation to [[Philip]].
Later that night [[Riffle]] hears back from [[Hana Goldfirm]]. She was able to find 3 holy pools in the area ([[Kriptin Forest]] in the [[Emerald Glade]], [[Amberhold]] being looked after by the sisters of Lethander, and one in an island south of Admiral’s Point under the care of the religious order of Sune). We know that [[The Bastard]]’s Band was heading was heading to the [[Krypton Forest]] in the [[Emerald Glade]].
Ingredients for Wolfsbane Potion:
Wolfsbane, stabilizing agent (holy water, bellandonna, etc.), powdered moonstone or moon seed (swamps or bogs), blood of a like lycanthrope, and purified water or water from a blessed body of water.
We run through our options. Firstly, we will try to craft the wolfsbane potion. As a backup, as the Gauntlet at [[Amberhold]] is being in a little under a month, we decide that taking [[Philip]] with us to [[Amberhold]] (to which [[Talir Rengavi]] will provide magical transportation) and attempt to access the blessed water site.
