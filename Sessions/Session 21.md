# Session 21 — 8 December 2025

[[Riffle]] asks if any of us have a necklace of beads. [[Deanira]] offers her mala beads and [[Riffle]] takes them off to the corner and starts closing their eyes and counting them. None of us have ever seen [[Riffle]] do anything remotely religious in the time that we have known them.
After our short rest we leave the room we rested in and decide to head down the sloping hallway to explore further. As we make our way down,
we begin to trudge through water. We come upon a door that is larger and more ornate than the others we’ve encountered. The doors open and reveal a shrine with a large stone statue of a dragon. The eyes are huge rubies. Along the walls are various statues of humanoids wearing cloaks and hoods, seemingly in prayer. The water is know up to [[Deanira]]’s thighs. [[WISC]] knows that because we’re in a swamp and the fortress has sunk, this is a low point in the water table that is causing the water to seep in from the surrounding area. In front of the large statue seems to be a baptismal font that remains above the water. [[Riffle]] had a book called “The Rise and Fall of the [[Dragon League]]” and remembers that although the [[Dragon League]] worshipped Tiamat and was therefore able to command dragons themselves, this seems to be an area of reverence of worship of a dragon themselves. The cults of the [[Dragon League]] also never established themselves on this continent. To our knowledge, there is no overlap between these groups. [[Riffle]], on [[WISC]]’s back, climbs up onto the statue to investigate the ruby eyes.
They check for traps and get the impression that there would be magical consequences for removing the rubies. [[WISC]] looks down into the baptismal font and the hole is shaped like a dragon’s maw with the hole of the throat going down far beyond what we can see. [[Deanira]] drops a gold piece down and hears it clink a ways down and land in a pile of metallic objects. [[Deanira]] does notice that there are dark brown stains of old dried blood in the basin. [[Deanira]] pricks her finger with her mithril dagger and spends a full 30 seconds wincing and milking her finger to get a drop of blood. [[WISC]] watches flabbergasted and says, “[[Deanira]], we’ve killed people, this is what bothers you?” A drop of blood falls into the basin. Something may have happened, but we can’t tell. [[Riffle]] is still contemplating pulling these ruby eyes out of the dragon. [[Riffle]] climbs around the back of the dragon’s head in case the rubies are protected by a cone-shaped AOE. [[WISC]] feels a grip on his calf underwater and life force leeches out of him. We roll initiative. [[Riffle]] is able to see an undead hand gripping [[WISC]] from the murky water.
While fighting the undead priest and his summoned hoard of bones, [[Riffle]] pops out the ruby eyes one by one and stashes them. We defeat them and then make our way further down the hall and the water level falls. We come upon another door and hear a sort of gurgling sound on the other side. This seems to be the dead end, the final room. We get the impression that we have walked the perimeter of a keep and are now at the base of a tower that goes far above us. The portions that remained above ground and did not sink was over time harvested and repurposed for other things, but the underground sunken structures that have not collapsed remain intact. [[Furgus]] knows undead to be on the other side of this door.
We open the door and a massive stench of decay and bloat hits us. In the room we see a huge bloated ghoul. We roll initiative. After killing the drowned ghoul and drowned assassin, we find a key around the ghoul’s neck. We search the room and see a locked vault. Inside the vault, we find a total of 2,000 gp, a silver shield with the design of a dragon in a mosaic of gemstones, a carved wooden mask shaped like a dragon’s visage painted green with flecks of gold (even though this mask is 800 years old, it is still pristine, transmutation. [[Mask of the Green Dragon]]), a set of studded leather armour (abjuration, [[Armour of Cold Resistance]]), 3 potions (2 potions of Greater Healing, one Potion of Climbing), a dagger (evocation, the [[Dagger of Venom]]), and a mace (evocation, +1 mace). We find a roll of silvery tape (transmutation, [[Duck Tape]]), a foot long rod with a button on it (transmutation, The [[Rod of Fishing]]), and a gold ring with animals (horse, dog, cat, transmutation magic, [[Ring of Gaseous Forms]]) engraved into it. We find a map case (unopened, necromantic magic) and a map of [[Axeholm]]. [[Deanira]] and [[Riffle]] open the map case and find inside a [[Scroll of Revivify]].
Spell scroll mechanics: If it’s not on your spell list but you are still able to cast that level of spell, you need to roll. It needs to be of the spell type you can cast (Cleric, Wizard, etc.)
The following day, we pack up and make our way towards the Basilisk nests. As we’re walking, we hit a patch of land, that becomes squishy, like we’re walking on a water bed. [[Deanira]] immediately jumps and dives to the left. When she lands, she immediately sinks underwater.
She passes a strength check to pull herself out of the mess of decaying vegetation. [[Deanira]] and [[WISC]] know this to be a natural phenomenon called a floating bog. [[WISC]] takes the time to fish and we make lunch. He catches 3 fish.
We’re walking and [[Deanira]] does a double take and sees a cabin that wasn’t there before. [[Deanira]] sneaks up to the cabin but the windows are shuttered. The chimney has smoke gently billowing out of it. [[WISC]] smells his favourite food on the smoke, roasted reindeer. [[Furgus]] smells pea and ham stew. [[Deanira]] smells smoke (rolled low). We approach the cabin and a fucking creepy arm wraps around the side of the building and opens the door until it’s ajar. “Come in” a creepy voice says. She’s an over proportioned old woman with very very long arms. We ask if she has seen any basilisks. She introduces herself as [[Agatha]]. “[[Agatha]] knows and sees a lot”. An empty bird cage hanging from the ceiling cackles and says “[[Agatha]] knows all! [[Agatha]] knows all!” We ask about the voice and she says “Ick! Show yourself!” An imp appears. We step inside and [[Riffle]] immediately starts stealing shit. She slaps them with a long arm in reprimand. We ask what she wants in return.
[[Deanira]] offers her a small jar of her toenail (hoof) clippings. She takes it and tells us where the Basilisks can be found. Travel West for another 6 hours and we will discover their nesting grounds. “You know you’re getting close when we see the statues of the previous hunters in the fields.” She asks for a piece of [[WISC]]’s fate in return for more information. [[WISC]] wishes to know about the corn husk dolls. He takes the deal. She takes the [[Cornhusk Dolls]] and licks it with an extraordinarily long tongue. “Ah, this is a fae creation. A pixie! Looks like you have a tricky tricky pixie around you”. We ask about [[Axeholm]]. We offer her the unbreakable bottle with the flame skull in it. She accepts. She peers into her crystal ball and tells us of [[Axeholm]].
“Ah, [[Axeholm]]. Buried deep behind the Coalcaster glacier. But how do you find it? [[Zyuir]]. From the Drow language. The Drow outpost of [[Zyuir]].
Find that, and you’ll find the key to getting to [[Axeholm]].” We recognize this word from the translation of the journal that [[Daddy Dildo Daggins]] worked on.
She offers us a porridge that tastes like everyone’s favourite dish. [[Deanira]] tastes her favourite fish dish from Coda’s Landing, her hometown. It is delicious. She hands us a card with “[[Agatha]]” written on it. Just a drop of blood on it will tell her that we want to talk. If she wants, she will show up.
We arrive at the basilisk nest and spend the night scouting it out. [[Furgus]] sees somewhere between 4 and 6 snouts protruding above the water here and there. They seem to be more active during the day.
