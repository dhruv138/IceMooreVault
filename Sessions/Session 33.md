# Session 33 — 20 July 2026

[[WISC]] prepares a succulent breakfast of ham. People are dumbfounded and shocked at watching [[WISC]] conjure up a ham using only an old wizened staff. As we eat, we hear a long low hunting horn sound through the valley. [[WISC]] and [[Furgus]] know the warning sign, someone needs help. We run to the edge of the woods and see two hunters staggering out and trying to hold each other up. They are covered in blood, one bitten in the shoulder by a creature. One of the hunters, [[Philip]], collapses from exhaustion. We question the other hunter Hanz, and he says that it was a huge bear attack. [[Deanira]] furrows her brow, thinking it’s strange that local hunters who are skilled and know the region would accidentally stumble into the den of a bear. They give us direction to the area South of Hawk’s Nest (north of where we are in [[Venberg]] now), a rock outcropping about 10 hours travel. They had run throughout the night, not stopping so that [[Philip]] could receive help. We are hesitant to travel to go kill the bear just because some townsfolk ventured into the wrong area, but as [[Klaus]] and some of the townspeople explain, sometimes their hogs run wild out that way to fatten up before the winter, and they’re worried for them. We decide to head to check it out.
On the way, [[Riffle]] asks [[Theo'din Corvis]] about his travels and story. He talks about pigeons. He does share that he heard tale of a group raiding the homes of the elderly in the dead of night and burning goblin villages. He said that he got exiled from [[Wren Nalore]] as well for attending the funeral of an important person in the town and cast Speak With Dead and asked some “inappropriate questions”.
We arrive at Hawk’s Nest and notice large depressions in the ground where rock has clearly been removed, as though someone has moved the stones. In the distance [[Riffle]]’s owl sees a large bear lounging in the plain. This bear is HUGE. It reminds [[Riffle]] of when they went to a circus in [[Amberhold]] as a child and saw a Dire Bear, twice as large as a normal bear. This one is EVEN BIGGER. We fight. They’re both Stone
Giant were-bears! We kill the first two and then a third Stone Giant were-bear comes out. We kill that one too.
Loot: we find a large mammoth fur pelt, a granite stone carving of a 6 ft bear that is incomplete, a giant bone club, giant carving tools, and a javelin that makes our fur stand on end ([[Javelin of Lightning]]), and a set of magical musical pipes. [[Deanira]] plays them and a bunch of rats come running out of the woods and surround her, watching as she plays. It is The [[Pipes of the Sewers]].
