# Session 38 — 24 September 2026

## Summary

[[Deanira]] learned that the gathering beneath [[The Two Tits]] was the [[Union of Many]], a consensual adult religious community to which [[Daddy Dildo Daggins|Brother Daggins]] belonged. The party shopped for the Academy Gala, investigated competition between the [[Velvet Glove Brothel]] and [[Heaven's Touch]], and found apparent enchantment magic affecting people at Heaven's Touch. They also investigated graduate student [[Peter Owens]], arriving at his family mausoleum just after a cloaked figure entered it. At the same moment, [[Talir Rengavi]] accused [[Riffle]] of stealing documents from his safe after [[Archivist Siobhan]] claimed to have seen Riffle enter his office.

## Locations Visited

- [[The Two Tits]] — Deanira entered the basement and met members of the Union of Many.
- A high-end Amberhold dress shop — Deanira purchased Gala attire.
- [[Velvet Glove Brothel]] — Riffle met [[Sabrina]] outside for a drink and information.
- [[Heaven's Touch]] — the party detected a possible enchantment affecting workers and patrons.
- [[Mistra's Academy]] — the party asked about Peter Owens while Riffle checked on Gala and Gauntlet preparations.
- [[Owen Family Mausoleum]] — a cloaked figure entered the crypt shortly before the party arrived.

## NPCs Encountered or Discussed

- [[Atheela]] — half-elf member of the Union of Many who explained the order and remembered Brother Daggins.
- Jeffrey — underage visitor refused entry to the Union's worship gathering after a *zone of truth* age check.
- [[Sabrina]] — reported that a young blond man calling himself Andrew recently visited a Velvet Glove worker named Angela.
- [[Zelda]] — former Velvet Glove worker now at Heaven's Touch; appeared to be enchanted.
- [[Peter Owens]] — Academy graduate student on leave after both parents died; reportedly visiting the family mausoleum frequently.
- [[Talir Rengavi]] — accused Riffle of stealing documents from his safe.
- [[Karyn]] — Bianca's overworked assistant; Riffle helped her and glimpsed Gauntlet details.
- [[Archivist Siobhan]] — allegedly saw Riffle enter Talir's office.

## Factions Involved

- [[Union of Many]] — followers of several love and fertility deities who emphasize open love, community, and living fully.
- [[Mistra's Academy]] — preparing the Gala and five-round Gauntlet.
- [[Library of Deneir]] — Talir's missing documents and the accusation against Riffle create a new internal crisis.
- [[Skeletal Hand]] — the party suspects that “Andrew” may be the existing [[Blond-Haired Youth|blond recruiter]], but this is not confirmed.

## Quest Progress

### [[Daggins Murder]]

The Union of Many confirmed that Daggins was a member known as Brother Daggins. His collection of fertility statues reflected the order's many love and fertility deities. This explains the gathering at The Two Tits but does not identify his killer.

### [[Gauntlet]]

Riffle learned that four teams will participate independently. The five themed rounds are Beasts, Celestials, Fiends, Elementals, and Fey.

## Major Events

- Deanira confirmed that the basement gathering at The Two Tits was a Union of Many worship meeting.
- Deanira purchased a gold-and-blue feathered ballgown and a collar of fine jewels for the Gala.
- Sabrina reported a young blond visitor using the name Andrew.
- Theo detected an enchantment aura at Heaven's Touch and attempted to cast *dispel magic* on Zelda; staff removed him and asked the party to leave because spellcasting was prohibited.
- The party learned that Peter Owens's parents had both died and that he had been visiting their mausoleum despite reportedly not being close to them.
- A hooded figure entered the Owen family crypt and disappeared from view.
- Talir sent Riffle an angry message demanding the return of documents allegedly stolen from his safe.

## Information Learned

- The Union of Many meets at The Two Tits about once a week and uses *zone of truth* to verify that participants are adults.
- Daggins belonged to the Union and collected fertility statues associated with its diverse deities.
- Heaven's Touch occupies a converted waterfront warehouse and appears comparable in quality to the Velvet Glove.
- An enchantment aura appears to affect people inside Heaven's Touch, including Zelda.
- Peter Owens is an Academy graduate student currently on leave. Both parents recently died; rumors suggest their cook may have poisoned them.
- Peter lives in an apartment rather than on campus and has recently spent time at the family mausoleum.
- The Gauntlet has four teams and five themed rounds: Beasts, Celestials, Fiends, Elementals, and Fey.

## Decisions Made

- The party investigated Heaven's Touch after speaking with Sabrina.
- The party went to the Owen family mausoleum to investigate Peter's behavior and his parents' deaths.

## Items Acquired or Revealed

- Deanira purchased a gold-and-blue feathered ballgown and a collar of fine jewels.
- Deanira showed Atheela the [[Silver Dildo]] that Daggins had entrusted to her as collateral.

- Determine why Zelda and other workers moved to Heaven's Touch.

- Determine who stole documents from Talir's safe and why Siobhan identified Riffle.
