# Session 38 — 24 September 2026

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Deanira meets [[Daddy Dildo Daggins]]'s fertility order, the [[Union of Many]], and Theo gets the party ejected from enchanted [[Heaven's Touch]]. Rumours surround [[Peter Owens]] and his family's deaths. At his family mausoleum, [[Talir Rengavi]] accuses Riffle of stealing from his safe.


[[Deanira|Deaníra]] walks down to the basement to see how many people are down there. She knocks on the door and opens it to see about a dozen people of all kinds. The Half Elf woman with the vagina staff asks if she can help [[Deanira]]. [[Deanira]] asks if they know someone with the name of [[Daddy Dildo Daggins|Daggins]]. They do! They were devastated to hear about [[Daddy Dildo Daggins|Brother Daggins]]'s death. She explains that their order is called [[Union of Many|The union of Many]]. They are followers of many different deities: Shialliah, Lethander, Melorah, and any other deities of fertility and love. Their emphasis is on open love and building strong relations amongst their community and deity lines. They are about loving life and living life to the fullest. [[Deanira]] says that she is delighted and that it makes so much sense as to why [[Daddy Dildo Daggins|Daggins]] was passionate about collecting fertility statues from many different cultures and old civilizations representing many different fertility/love gods and goddesses. She introduces herself as [[Atheela]]. She says that they come to [[The Two Tits|the Two Tits]] about once a week to "worship". [[Deanira|Deaníra]] excitedly shows [[Atheela]] the silver dildo that he entrusted to her. She says that she takes good care of it every night. As [[Deanira|Deaníra]] leaves, she hears, "as always, before we begin, we must cast the zone of truth. Is everyone here over the age of 18?" A young pimply kid named Jeffrey says, "no..." "Jeffrey, as we discussed many times before, come back when you're 18."
The following day we go shopping for the [[Academy Gala|Gala]]! [[Deanira]] is on the hunt for a gown, jewellery, and face paint. We go to a high end dress shop and [[Deanira]] gets a gold and blue feathered ballgown and a collar of fine jewels.
After shopping, [[Riffle]] wants to go speak to [[Sabrina]], the bartender at the [[Velvet Glove Brothel|Velvet Glove]]. [[Sabrina]] had agreed to meet [[Riffle]] outside of the brothel. for a drink and to talk about the competition between the [[Velvet Glove Brothel|Velvet Glove]] and [[Heaven's Touch]]. [[Sabrina]] spoke about a young blonde man who gave his name [[Andrew]] and saw one of the workers at the [[Velvet Glove Brothel|Velvet Glove]] named Angela. We are wondering if this is the [[Skeletal Hand]] fellow...
We head to the [[Heaven's Touch]]. It is down by the water front and appears to be a warehouse that was converted into a brothel recently. It is beautiful inside, comparable to the [[Velvet Glove Brothel|velvet Glove]]. We are greeted and guided into a lounge and offered a free drink, and given time to get to know the various talent. [[Theo'din Corvis|Theo]] notices that there is an aura or enchantment that seems settled on people in here. [[Riffle]] recognizes [[Zelda]], a former worker from the [[Velvet Glove Brothel|Velvet Glove]]. She also appears to be enchanted. She says that she needed a change of scenery and it just felt so nice here. She moved workplaces just a few weeks ago. [[Theo'din Corvis|Theo]] shakes her hand and tries to cast Dispel Magic. [[Theo'din Corvis|Theo]] is escorted out because the area is a no magic casting area. We are asked to accompany him out. [[Riffle]] is upset because [[Theo'din Corvis|Theo]] got us kicked out before they could even enter the brothel. They Dimension Door 500 ft away and then go for a walk to cool
down.
We head to the [[Mistra's Academy|Academy]] to see about a graduate student named [[Peter Owens|Peter]]. Owen, the son of the warehouse owner, the one the ship is stuck in port because of. His parents both just died, so he his taking a leave of absence from the school while things are sorted out. He's been spotted at the [[Owens Family Mausoleum|family mausoleum]] more lately, which some of the students find a little strange since he wasn't very close to them. There's rumours going around regarding their deaths, perhaps the cook poisoned them. They say that [[Peter Owens|Peter]] is strange and gives off icky weird vibes. He doesn't live on campus, instead he has an apartment.
Meanwhile [[Riffle]] visits [[Talir Rengavi|Talir]] and then a staff member named [[Karyn]] ([[Bianca Onyxcrest|Bianca]]'s assistant). Both (separately) are very stressed and overworked preparing the [[Academy Gala|Gala]] and the [[Gauntlet]]. [[Riffle]] gets [[Karyn]] a sandwich and coffee and then helps to tidy up her office. [[Riffle]] gets a glimpse of some of the [[Gauntlet]] details and sees that there are a total of four teams (not competing against each other). There are also 5 themed rounds: Beasts, Celestials, Fiends, Elementals, and Fey.
We all make our way to meet up at the graveyard to the West outside of the city to check out the mausoleum. As we walk, rain begins to fall. As the mausoleum come into view we see a cloaked hooded figure enter the structure and vanish.
Right after the figure disappears into the crypt, [[Riffle]] receives a
Sending message in their head from [[Talir Rengavi|Talir]], sounding angrier than he has ever been before. He demands that [[Riffle]] return the documents they supposedly stole from his safe that afternoon, saying Archivist [[Siobhan]] saw [[Riffle]] enter [[Talir Rengavi|Talir]]'s office.
