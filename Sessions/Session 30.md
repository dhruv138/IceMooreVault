# Session 30 — 3 June 2026

We decide to take a long rest and then gather information from the corpses through Speak With Dead. Then we will follow the trail of footprints to find and scout the extraction point. Overnight, [[Riffle]] sends a message to [[Hana Goldfirm]] updating her on what we found in the tomb and that some remaining [[Skeletal Hand]] members are heading south. [[Riffle]] approaches one of the dead [[Skeletal Hand]] members and casts Speak with Dead.
“Who is the [[Master of Death]]?” “I do not know”
“What were you planning on doing with [[Corthos]]’s body?” “Reanimating it.”
“Where were you going to take [[Corthos]]?”
“I know we were going south. I don’t know the final destination.” “How did you join the Skeletal Hands?”
“I wanted to learn magic but couldn’t afford the academy. I was offered a different path to learn.”
“A [[Blond-Haired Youth|blonde-haired youth by the name of Peter]] at an inn in [[Amberhold]].”
We head out and follow the tracks south-southeast. The tracks follow the tree-line for a while, and then eventually pass above a homestead in the low lands. We see a pack of wolves drawn to the homestead but staying on the outskirts, like they are weary or afraid. It reminds [[Riffle]] and [[Deanira]] of canines that want meat roasting on a spit, but they are afraid of the fire. We don’t see any smoke from the chimney or any obvious signs of habitation or abandonment. The grass isn’t overgrown and there are no animals out. We sneak down to check out the homestead and while the wolves see us, they keep their distance from us. We look through a window of the house first, but they are shuttered. [[WISC]] opens the front door, which is unlocked. Inside, we find three things that can only be described as “meat sacks”.
They are three Orcs, having had all their bones removed without any exterior cuts or harm. Oscar and Marybell and their toddler Ivan, who resided in [[Venberg]]. It seems like the home has been untended for only a few days, but the bodies are not decomposing at all. We find the same thing in the barn with the livestock. [[Riffle]] knows that this reeks of necromancy. This is not any type of necromancy that anyone would have learned of at the academy. We continue on the trail following the tracks until night falls. We make camp and [[Riffle]] casts sending to [[Talir Rengavi]], telling him about how we found [[Skeletal Hand]] Faction members from the academy and that the remaining members are headed south. They may be recruiting new members in [[Amberhold]]. [[Talir Rengavi]] responds that it is very interesting, he needs time to think, and to not tell anyone about this yet. The night is eerily quiet but it passes without anything happening. [[Deanira]] does notice that this area is completely devoid of wildlife, and [[Furgus]] knows this is very unusual for this region. Not even rabbits, bird, foxes, squirrels, nothing. In the morning, [[Riffle]] summons a familiar in the form of a [[Tiny]] owl. They send it off to scout the region as we travel to look for any wildlife.
Right before lunch, the owl spots that the trail ends in a little curl de sac or alcove in the woods which contains three crates. There are signs that people were here, but it is still late morning on the day they were supposed to meet here at the behest of the [[Master of Death]]. We go down to check out the crates. No sound is coming from them. A blast of necrotic energy comes out when we open a crate, and it hits [[Deanira]] harder than the others. There is nothing else inside the crate except the remnants of a glyph on the bottom. In the second crate [[Deanira]] rocks it back and forth and hears something inside. She tips it over away from her and the lid comes off, spilling bones onto the ground. They are both humanoid and animal bones, and begin to come together to form a large skeletal juggernaut. We roll initiative. We kill the skeletal juggernaut and then [[Riffle]] examines the glyph and recognizes it from the ceiling of Kristoff’s undertaker lair that threw
out thunder damage at us previously. The final crate gets pummelled by [[Riffle]]’s spiritual weapon and bursts open with a wave of necrotic energy. [[Riffle]] reads the letter from the [[Master of Death]] to Kristoff.
They get the impression the rendezvous hadn’t happened yet. We put the crates back together and hide in the trees to wait for the arrival of the [[Skeletal Hand]] members. The day passes and night falls with no arrivals. We set up a fireless camp and keep watch. At around 0100, a flash of light suddenly bursts in the middle of the clearing. A cloaked humanoid figure is standing there. “Kristoff,” they whisper. [[Riffle]] wakes us quietly. The voice is masculine but not familiar. We stay silent. [[Riffle]] sends their owl familiar to try to get a glimpse of the face of this hooded person. [[Riffle]] sees through its eyes, and glimpses an extremely annoyed face, concentrating on something and muttering under his breath. He is casting sending. He appears to be human/ Elven lineage, in his late 40s early 50s with slight greying along his temples. He is clean shaven but with slight sideburns and green eyes. After a minute, we hear him say “that damn idiot, did those four trip him up again? I should have turned him into a zombie when I had the chance.” He casts a rip in reality and walks through it, vanishing.
