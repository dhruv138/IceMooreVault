# Session 20 — 22 November 2025

[[WISC]] ties rope to a piton and throws it up to the edge of the hole.
[[Deanira]] grabs and secures it. There is movement on the other side of the door. We prepare for combat. We find a flame skull and undead skeletons, we fight them, [[Riffle]] finds a leather document case with a lock on it. But importantly, we discover that [[WISC]] has had the intention to use the Yeti cooler and fishing supplies to go fishing so that we can open a fish stick stand at the market at [[Truemetal Halls]], because we couldn’t find fish sticks to eat at the last market. We destroy the flame skull, but as the ashes will reanimate in one hour, we place the ashes in an unbreakable bottle, wrap it with fabric, and place it into the Yeti cooler. We take a short rest and then check the bottle. We hear glass rattling around inside the cooler. We don’t open it. [[Riffle]] picks the lock on the leather case. Inside are documents belonging to the Dragon Cult, including blackmail materials from several hundred years ago. We also find a map of [[Axeholm]] itself. It appears to be inside the mountain itself… somewhere. Shenanigans also ensue where [[WISC]] summons a giant elk from the [[Grey Bag of Tricks]] and it completely squishes us in the hallway. None of us can move so we have to mercy kill it.
