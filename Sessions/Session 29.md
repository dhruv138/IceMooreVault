# Session 29 — 15 April 2026

We use a key and unlock the cells to release the prisoners. We summon some ham to feed them, but it takes a long time to rouse them. [[Riffle]] finds a bag of dried dates on one of the men who attacked us and traces them back to [[Amberhold]]. They don’t grow anywhere around here, but are a common snack sold at the docks in [[Amberhold]]. Investigating the Dwarves in the other cell, the sulfuric acrid smell isn’t strong on their clothing, but it is not the same scent that we encountered on the poison flesh golem. They appear to be healthy and not poisoned. [[WISC]] has discovered a magical tapestry and it begins to weave itself. He looks at it and sees his greatest feat represented in the cloth. The hag’s hut is in the background, the hag crying behind him, and him standing proudly and triumphantly in the foreground. It is the [[Tapestry of Feats]].
The [[Venburg]] people finally awaken. We learn that they arose here all different ways. [[Wolf]] was out hunting and suddenly fell unconscious and found himself here. [[Ambria]] was ambushed while tending to her flock in the night. [[Zeke]] was up at [[Truemetal Halls]] and got a contract to act as a guide because he knew the general area of this tomb. He came with the Dwarven party we were looking for. Some of the Dwarves were captured, some were killed. He said that those that survived in his party were taken down the hallway we have not yet gone down. They were originally a party of 10, 4 of the Dwarves survived in addition to him. The remaining 5 were killed. The bodies of the guys who fought us were not the ones who ambushed them. It was a party of more of them, for a total of around 20. He said that there also used to be a lot more stuff in the chamber. [[Deanira]] looks around and it definitely seems like there was more people here at one time. Slowly people had moved on. The surviving Dwarves were taken this morning. The Dwarves in the other cell are not members of his party. We are thinking that perhaps members of the group left with their horses and
donkeys and left behind the hoofprints that we saw. [[Zeke]] said that the captors got past the minotaurs by holding up some sort of object (amulet maybe?) that made the minotaurs stand against the wall and leave them alone. The cages look new, like they were fabricated to fit into natural alcoves in the tomb. The doors to this chamber look similarly recent and have been designed to be barricaded by placing a plank across the door.
[[Riffle]] goes to the cage of the Dwarves and they think [[Riffle]] is the angel of death. [[Deanira]] stands behind [[Riffle]], ink black wearing her skull. We hear [[WISC]] call, “You’re not dying, they’re just weird!” They were working the lime kiln and were snatched. They mined quick lime, and when it interacts with moisture it explodes. It is highly volatile and the fumes are very poisonous. Due to this interaction with moisture, they need to cover up head to toe so that it doesn’t interact with sweat.
The Dwarves tell us that the captors were moving stuff out of the chamber and were discussing “work below” and an “extraction point”. We let them go and head deeper into the tomb.
The walls are lined with Minotaur skulls, like catacombs. While the floors are stone, we can see from the footprints and drag marks in the dirt and dust that it has been recently travelled. We come upon a door and on the other side is an enormous natural cavern. At the other end is three doors, above each one is a 20ft statue of a Goliath. [[Riffle]] approaches the rightmost door and lettering appears. It says in Giant, “In the name of the mother” (statue is a Goliath woman holding a baby goat and a shepherd’s hook with flowers growing at her feet. Theleaia, the god of agriculture and fertility). The middle door says “In the name of the warrior” (a Goliath warrior roaring with two battle axes). The leftmost says “In the name of the hunter” (a Goliath with a bow and quiver of arrows). [[Riffle]] opens the door of the mother. We split up and each go down a door ([[WISC]] goes down the mother door with [[Riffle]]).
[[Deanira]] goes through the hunter door, and [[Furgus]] goes through the warrior door. The hallways all end up converging into one after only 5 feet or so. Behind us, the doors have closed (not locked). The passage
ends in another door. The hallway is decorated in runes similar to the ones on the door (Giant language). [[Furgus]] translates for us and tells us that it shares the story of the adventurer to whom this tomb belongs. We enter through a door at the end and enter the final chamber. A massive Goliath warrior is standing in the centre of the tomb, dripping in honey, holding a halberd. He is surrounded by his spirit guardians. The two pillars closest to us that we can see there is a Dwarf tied to each, clearly dead. A robed figure also lies dead at the foot of each.
[[Christolf Nightfall]] (the undertaker from [[Truemetal Halls]]) steps out and attacks us along with the Goliath adventurer. When we kill Kristoff, the papers on the desk behind him burn up. Only two are able to be recovered. (Attached) Also on the desk we find basic alchemist’s supplies. [[Corthos]] (the Goliath) has a [[Dread Helm]] and a halberd (The [[Blade of the Ancestors]]), along with two snake shaped gold and jewel encrusted arm rings. [[Christolf Nightfall]] has some gold and a +1 arcane grimoire.
