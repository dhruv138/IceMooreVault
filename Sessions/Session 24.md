# Session 24 — 9 February 2026

We all take a seat in an adjoining room on couches surrounding a massive black stone. The black stone was our little river stone. It was under a shrinking spell. This is its true form. The wizard introduces us to [[Bianca Onyxcrest]], a colleague of his. They have an idea of what our smooth black stone could be.
The Lords of the Elements:
About 3000 years ago, different elementals were wreaking havoc on Ligos. A group of mages formed the [[Elementalist Syndicate]] in order to discover how to better control these elementals that were roaming the land. They ended up binding them together into larger elementals, with the understanding that the fewer there were, the easier they would be to control. Eventually they got too strong and broke free of their captives. Two were confirmed to be defeated. The Lord of Earth’s body was hallowed out and then occupied by the Dwawrves. The city is marked on the map in the far north, Derragh (Dwarven for Titan Fall) The Lord of the Air is believed to be alive and at large, possibly residing in the upper atmosphere. The Lord of Water took to the sea. It was believed to have been killed in battle with an ancient dragon turtle, but this was never confirmed. The Lord of Fire was defeated in the west in the volcanic region now home to the [[Fire Dwarves]]. He was defeated by a party of adventurers made up of magic wielders and melee fighters. The magic wielders froze it and turned it to obsidian, and the Lord was shattered into many pieces. The smooth river stone is one of those pieces. The archivists want to take the stone to the library to research and protect it. They offer us payment, but [[Riffle]] insists that it is worth far more than 3,000 gp. After discussing among our party, we decide that we want it to go to the Library, as the Academy is not reliable with following up or being thoroughly trusted. We develop a counter offer of giving it to [[Talir Rengavi]] for 2,000 gp. [[Talir Rengavi]] accepts the offer.
We go to [[The Warhammer]] with [[Hana Goldfirm]] and [[Talir Rengavi]] so [[Riffle]] can catch up with them and introduce us. [[Riffle]] updates them on their adventures. [[Talir Rengavi]] tells us that every year a festival is held on the Harvest Moon, and this year it just so happens to align with the King’s birthday, leading to a larger than usual celebration in [[Amberhold]] in 3 months. He tells us that there is an event planned by the Academy called The Gauntlet, including a series of encounters with many beasts and creatures that the Academy mages will be conjuring. They are looking for a party to defeat these monsters in the trials. They think it would be a good chance to heighten the prestige of the Library and humble the Academy. He offers for the Library to sponsor us. With nary a squeak, a giant spectral elk forms out of [[WISC]]’s fart then fades into the wind. After this, [[Deanira]] asks if it’s possible to arrange for some magical transportation for us to get to [[Amberhold]]. Otherwise it is a month’s travel. [[Talir Rengavi]] says that he will see what favours he can call in, and will be in touch with [[Riffle]]. [[Hana Goldfirm]] asks to speak to [[Riffle]] alone, and they skulk off while [[Deanira]] hands out cards to do fortune telling for the party. During this a 17-19 year old Dwarven male ([[Hector]]) comes to our table and introduces himself as a huge fan. He buys us a round of Dwarven Ale and asks us our story. We spend time giving anecdotes and a little bit of adventuring advice. He wants to be an adventurer one day. He works at [[Gunther Rockhammer]]’s Tannery (another [[Gunther Rockhammer]], not the one who runs [[The Warhammer]]) which is on the same level of the city that the Undertaker necromancer we defeated was on. He says the Undertaker’s place is still sealed off and guarded by the town guards.
We finish out the night with tarot readings and [[Deanira]] purposefully interprets [[WISC]]’s spread as negative and that he’s harbouring a huge secret that is slowly destroying himself and those he loves. (Due to the green dragon mask that makes [[Deanira]] suspicious of [[WISC]]). In the middle of the night, [[WISC]], [[Deanira]], and [[Furgus]] wake up horribly ill.
The ale we drank from the Dwarven teen was POISONED. [[Riffle]] hears us in the middle of the night vomiting and runs to heal us. They ask us what the fuck happened, we were only left alone for 4 hours.
When we feel well enough to stand, we go down to the bar and tell [[Gunther Rockhammer]] we were poisoned. We describe [[Hector]] and tell him the story. He doesn’t have much sympathy. [[Riffle]] examines the steins we had, and recognizes the poison called Midnight’s Tears. It is a poison that only affects a creature at the stroke of midnight. It smells slightly of vanilla. The three of us spend the rest of the day in bed. [[Riffle]] sees [[Hana Goldfirm]] and [[Talir Rengavi]] off. [[Olga]] for [[The Warhammer]] comes in several hours later. [[WISC]] talks to [[Olga]] and meanwhile we go to the Alchemist’s shop to deliver the shipment from [[Wren Nalore]] and the Basilisk eggs. We ask him about the poison Midnight’s Tears. He tells us that no one legit in the city makes it. It is incredibly dangerous and is a favourite tool of assassins. He does have 3 antitoxins on hand for sale. We buy them, though we know the antitoxin only works if you take it before midnight (when symptoms start). He does tell us that he sold one antitoxin to a woman the previous day.
Back at [[The Warhammer]], [[WISC]] tells [[Olga]] about the poisoning as he orders some porridge. She didn’t see anything but he shares his description. [[Olga]] tells us that she’s continued to check on [[Daddy Dildo Daggins]] as we asked. She last saw him two days ago and he was very excited and had something to tell us. We then get back together and go to check in on [[Daddy Dildo Daggins]]. We knock on his door but there is no answer. We go in, and we find him dead in a pool of blood in his study with a knife in his back.
His fingers are bloodstained and he started to write, ‘RI’ and the beginning of what looks like an ‘F’ and several black feathers surround him on the floor.
