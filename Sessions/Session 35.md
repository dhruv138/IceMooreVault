# Session 35 — 17 August 2026

So… [[Philip]] is coming to [[Amberhold]] with us. And [[Riffle]] refuses to ask [[Bianca Onyxcrest]] for help. In three weeks The Gauntlet will be held in [[Amberhold]], in which we will compete on behalf of the Library. The next full moon is in a month.
In [[Venburg]], the following day is the festival. [[Riffle]] receives a ping from [[Talir Rengavi]], and no one at the Academy knows where [[Venburg]] is, so he hired a local who knows the region who will arrive in 2 days (day after the festival). In the meantime, [[Theo'din Corvis]] asks [[Furgus]] if he is morally okay with eating rabbit. A moral argument ensues, without much of a consensus. [[Riffle]] asks [[Jane Pedimore]] that if she sees another golden-eyed bugbear, if she can get the name before she slaughters him. [[Jane Pedimore]] agrees.
The morning of the festival, we awaken to the smell of slow roasting pig. In the evening, an effigy of Melora will be lit in honour of a good harvest the following year. [[Theo'din Corvis]] is spending his morning painting small wooden figurines, and [[Riffle]] approaches, wanting to discuss something. They point out that [[Theo'din Corvis]] has asked many personal questions of [[Riffle]], so [[Riffle]] offers to answer more questions if he would like. [[Theo'din Corvis]] asks [[Riffle]] if they would eat a raven. [[Riffle]] says yes, because they are not a raven. “Would you eat a kenku?” “No, is cannibalism”. [[Theo'din Corvis]] gets stressed and worried that this is his one chance to ask questions and that [[Riffle]] won’t be in the mood in the future.
[[Theo'din Corvis]] wants to make a list of towns our Kenku friend would be bored and would not want to steal in. While it’s Question Time, [[Furgus]] asks [[Riffle]] if they would kill us in our sleep. [[Riffle]] responds that no, of course not, we would be awake for that.
Among the festival games are archery, grease stick pull, weight throw, ear pull, and blanket toss. [[Riffle]] and WIS do the ear pull. There is a braided leather band that connects [[Riffle]]’s head feathers to [[WISC]]’s ears. It’s a contested con check. We take turns doing the grease stick pull, archery, and blanket toss. For the blanket toss, there is a bell that is hung 30 feet in the air. You use a blanket like a trampoline to launch someone into the air to touch the bell. We win first try!
[[Riffle]] seeks out [[Uthal]] and finds him drinking and singing with some others. [[Riffle]] says they need to talk and pulls him to another area where they can talk in private. [[WISC]] gave [[Riffle]] a letter and a hundred gold for [[Oma]]. [[Riffle]] heads [[Uthal]] the letter in case [[WISC]] doesn’t return. They warn [[Uthal]] to NOT give [[Oma]] the letter unless [[Riffle]] specifically contacts him saying it’s time. They entrust [[Uthal]] with the gold for [[Oma]] as well. The caveat: [[WISC]] can’t read or write, unless it’s Dwarvish. The joke is that the letter is likely written in Dwarvish, which [[Oma]] cannot read.
[[Furgus]] spends time with his family vaguely saying goodbye to his niece and nephew, in case he doesn’t return.
As the sun sets, the townspeople head south of the palisades to the effigy of Melora. [[Klaus]] sets it alight and people spend time watching and counting the number of sparks flying off of the figure. It is said that each spark represents a child, goat, pig, etc. that will be born in the following year.
[[Riffle]] leaves when the effigy is lit and is drinking by themselves at the furthest part of town, religiously avoiding eye contact with the effigy. As the festival begins to die down, [[Deanira]] seeks out [[Riffle]]. She grabs a drink and asks if everything is okay. She learns that [[Riffle]] doesn’t like fire. Drunken [[Riffle]] tells her that 5 to 6 years ago, [[Riffle]] accompanied [[Talir Rengavi]] and a bunch of librarians to a “stupid” magic symposium at [[Western Point]]. On the way back to [[Amberhold]] they were attacked by bandits. Sometime during the scuffle a fire began, which entrapped [[Talir Rengavi]] and greviously wounded him. [[Riffle]] asked Danir for aid, and Danir granted it. He went into the fire and saved [[Talir Rengavi]], who was fully healed by [[Riffle]]’s new healing abilities. They show a patch of their shoulder and back where the feathers are matte and growing askew. “The burn was the price I paid to become Cleric.”
Out of nowhere, [[Furgus]] receives a sharp pull on his ear, and [[Uthal]] freaks out, realises that [[Riffle]] had given false advice to [[Uthal]] that [[Furgus]] likes his ears pulled. Realising this is bullshit, [[Uthal]] runs away super fast.
The following morning, a gnome bedecked in steampunk approaches us and tells us that he is here as our transportation guide. The entire town comes out and gathers to watch: they have never seen anything like this before. We decide to leave our horses here at [[Venburg]] to be cared for. The gnome fiddle with a small contraption and a portal opens. We go through one by one. We seem to be tumbling through space for a long time and yet no time at all. We land on a dark wood floor, [[Furgus]] landed poorly and tumbles onto a well built man. “Thank you ma’am, may I have another?” This man is spread eagled tied to a bed blindfolding with only skimpy underwear. He has a tattoo of crashing waves with lightning on a pectoral. A female Orc walks in wearing a leather biker’s cap, a chainmail bikini, and a riding crop. According to [[Riffle]], we have arrived inside the [[Velvet Glove Brothel]]. We are a littleeee off our mark. [[Riffle]] knows the bartender [[Sabrina]]. We go downstairs and [[Riffle]] gives a Tiefling female a big hug. [[Sabrina]] tells [[Riffle]] that things are going okay, but there is a new brothel in town taking a lot of the business, called Heaven’s Touch. This brothel apparently has an uncanny ability to find the perfect person to match to the client’s preferences and needs. [[Theo'din Corvis]] spends time giving [[Sabrina]] ideas for special theme nights to help boost business. We each order a drink (except for [[Philip]], who is wide eyes and shocked). [[Riffle]] wants to meet [[Sabrina]] later off premises to discuss tattoos.
Outside the brothel, we see a stand for [[Sending Stones]] at a discounted price for Unlimited Sending within [[Amberhold]] on their Party Plan by the company Horizon. 150 gp each.
We step into the city and are surrounded by the shock and awe of the large grandeur of [[Amberhold]]. [[Riffle]] is home.
