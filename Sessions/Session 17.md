# Session 17 — 16 October 2025

The officers begin to sweep the house and seal off the scene. The front gate will be under guard as they do their investigation. We head back to [[The Warhammer]] and speak to [[Gunther Rockhammer]], but he doesn’t know much. We begin to talk about someone exploiting this mine and taking advantage of that opportunity, and [[Riffle]] is curious if there has been an increased supply of Mithril coming into the city lately.
[[Riffle]] remembers that at the last Market Day (day after Dagin’s accident), the Mithril merchants were upset that the price of the special coal had doubled for no apparent reason. We speak to some of the Mithril merchants, and they say that the cost of Mithril is fairly regulated and quite stable. Anthracite is the only coal that can burn hot enough to work with Mithril and Adamantine. The merchants and smiths say that the price of Anthracite is usually very stable as well. It is mined in [[Kohlemine]], to the East, by the [[Black Hand Dwarves]]. In order to make this price hike sustainable, the [[Black Hand Dwarves]] would have to have another buyer, factoring in the decreased demand from [[Truemetal Halls]] (due to the price). We keep this information in the back of our minds.
We swing by the wizard’s shop to see if he’s made any progress on looking into the stone. The wizard isn’t in, so we speak to the apprentice who doesn’t have any updates. We go back to [[The Warhammer]] for the night and then decide to head out to retry the Apothecary quest in the morning. The next day we pack up our belongings and our black horse Dante, and [[Furgus]] notices that in his bag is [[Daddy Dildo Daggins]]’s [[Silver Pie Cutter]] and gold coins that weren’t there before. (None of our characters know that [[Riffle]] is placing these things in his bag for fun). [[Furgus]] doesn’t have a conscience and wants [[Furgus]] to sell it to raise money for his family at next month’s festival we are going to in his hometown. [[Furgus]] doesn’t want to sell it, since it’s [[Daddy Dildo Daggins]]’s. We hear a knock on the door of our room at the
Warhammer. It’s the bar maiden we asked to keep an eye on [[Daddy Dildo Daggins]] and make sure he’s still eating and functioning. He asked her to give this transcription of the journal to us. This is from the journal of a Cleric [[Daddy Dildo Daggins]] came a cross a while ago. He thinks this information is relevant or important. The entries mention a water genasi who claimed to have gone to [[Axeholm]] as part of a group but was the only survivor.
According to his ramblings, his companions were killed and feasted on by undead that roamed the streets of the city. (Entry pictured on following page)
[[Deanira]] takes the first night watch while her companions sleep. We are not far from our previous campsite where the [[Cornhusk Dolls]] were. She meets a Pixie who introduces himself as [[Wetel]]. In the name of playing tricks on people, he asks if [[Deanira]] would like to take a bag. One bag allows [[Wetel]] to put corn husk dolls into the bag and [[Deanira]] can pull them out of her bag to continue playing the trick on her companions.
[[Deanira]] will play the trick on her companions far away from this location as well. We take our [[Cornhusk Dolls]] and pocket them this time. On Day 3 we approach the area where we encountered the Flesh Golem.
Like before, we explore the area and once again don’t find anything. We continue on to the berg of [[Darham Hold]] (where the Tiefling was from). It appears heavily fortified with new parapets and active patrols. A banner shows an axe with ice on the blade. We approach the guard and ask them if there is anything in particular they are trying to protect the people from. The guards says that the Lords of [[Eisenberg]] (the domain of the [[Iron Dwarves]], like [[Truemetal Halls]]) have failed to protect the land, so they are taking it upon themselves to protect the town. He says that Countess Opal Darham now holds domain over this city, as the [[Eisenberg]] Lords forfeited this right when they failed to protect the land. He tells us of a lady who was coming to pay her rent and was attacked by a dead beast (the flesh golem) but was luckily saved by Adventurers (us, unbeknownst to the guard). This incident was the straw that broke the camel’s back. The guard doesn’t tell us of any more recent tales. After a couple more of hours of travel, we hear horses coming towards us (not at
too fast a clip). There are 6 riders total, one is holding a shield with the same seal of an ice axe that we saw at Durham Hold. They tell us that they just cleared a camp of outlaws/bandits. We continue on as we have a few more days of travel before arriving at [[Wren Nalore]]. One morning, [[Riffle]] sees a path in the woods and smells a fire off in the distance. We follow the trail and find the charred remains of a small village. As we look around, we find goblin bodies. They have slash marks and clean cuts, clearly made by blades. We also find a broken shield with an ice axe brand on it. Bowls seem to have been gathered and discarded. They appear to be golden and jeweled, but at closer inspection they are wooden but have been painted to appear gold. These goblins appear to have been worshipping Merdiadar, a deity of patience, tolerance, meditation, and arts 3 crafts. As we’re talking, we hear a faint crying. We find a young 1 year old goblin child in the bushes. [[Riffle]] picks up the baby and begins to mimic lullabies that they have heard sung. We use the [[Staff of Ham]] to summon ham and feed the child. We are two days from [[Wren Nalore]], and we will keep the child safe until arriving at the city. We put the goblins to rest and [[Riffle]] performs last rites in their best imitation of the rites of Merdiadar.
[[Cult of the Destroyer]]
[[Cult of the Destroyer]]
rose to power under Cidis’s name, taxing the population, collecting riches and treasures for the dragon. A dam was built in the gorge to power processing the gems and treasures for Cidis. About 800 years ago, Cidis disappeared and no one knows why. The [[Cult of the Destroyer]] was overthrown and the dam was broken. The deciduous forest terrain was forever changed and became a bog, and the remains of the region were sunken into the ground. As we make our way on, we see the city of [[Wren Nalore]] appear through the mist. It is a series of islands connected by bridges.
