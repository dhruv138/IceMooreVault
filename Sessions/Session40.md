# Session 40 — 25 Oct 2026

## Interrogating "Peter"

- "Peter" is bound and gagged with WISC's super smelly socks.
- Theo casts **Message** to try to communicate with the people in the cages. They don't seem to receive or register it at all. *The lights are on but nobody's home.*
- Theo casts **Zone of Truth** and we interrogate him.
  - He says he is **Lord Peter Owens**.
  - He suggests it was his **parents** who were doing something to the people in the cages and who created this area.
  - Theo threatens him with waterboarding he learned during his time at the Conjuring Initiates Association (CIA).
- **Key admissions (under Zone of Truth):**
  - He **didn't break into Talir's office**, has **never framed Riffle** for anything, and has **never framed a kenku** for anything.
  - Riffle asks when, where, why, and how he got the tattoo.
  - We confirm he's **working for the Master of Death**, and the Master is a **he**.
  - Peter **finds recruits** for the Master and **does various experiments**.
  - WISC asks whether the people in the cages are recruits or experiments: they're **test subjects to create bigger and better minions** for the Master of Death.
  - He smiles like he's smarter than everyone else — perhaps even the Master of Death.
  - As far as he knows, **the Master of Death has told no one his true name**.

## Peter's Death

- Furgus intimidates him into telling us everything. Right as he's about to speak, his **tattoo glows green** and a **Disintegrate** spell turns him to ash.
- Theo stores some of the ashes in a condom.
- Furgus recalls a **glyph that glowed in the mausoleum** when Deanira and Riffle first stepped into the tomb and were teleported into the sarcophagi. He feels a similar glyph was in the tattoo, triggered by something specific — *possibly speaking the Master of Death's name?*

## Talir Arrives

- We hear a creak in the floor above. Furgus goes up in the dark to investigate but sees nothing. Something tall and invisible bumps into him and trips over him — it's **Talir**.
- Furgus guides him downstairs, both invisible. Talir drops invisibility and Riffle gives the full "you must be wondering how we got here" spiel.

### The political situation (per Talir)
- **A Lord is dead.** Even though he was doing shady stuff, Peter was still a Lord — this is a delicate problem.
- Many Lords are fine with the current King, but many remember the bliss of a **weak king (the King's father)** when they ran relatively unchecked.
- **The Academy** is a third, unofficial power — a large concentration of magic in one place. Generally neutral, but some Lords feel it's too much power in one place.
- Talir worries the Lords could see this as **a move against the Lords by a party associated with the Academy**.
- **Stolen from Talir's secret safe:** his personal documents, notes on the acquisition teams, and reports (including Riffle's).
- **We'll definitely need a cover-up.**
- The delicate balance of power is one of the reasons for **the Gauntlet**:
  - It was a project of the **late headmaster Gavin Goslin** to flex the Academy's power.
  - Talir wasn't a fan, preferring a subtler approach.
  - The headmaster died, but the train was already moving down the tracks.
- With the death of the **Owens heir**, an inheritance will fall into the vacuum.
- **Cover story:** Talir will contact someone he knows in the **royal garrison** and bring him in — it becomes a royal operation in which Peter *perished while resisting royal arrest*.

### The alarm
- Riffle asks if their alarm went off when Talir's office was robbed.
- There's an alarm coded specifically to Riffle for entering Talir's office. **An alarm did go off, but it wasn't Riffle's** — even though the **Archivist (Shivon, she)** saw "Riffle" enter.

### Other concerns
- Riffle is worried about the **timing**: the Master of Death animating **Corthos**, recruiting to the **skeletal hand**, and creating bigger and badder minions — all very close to the Gauntlet and concentrated in **Amberhold** (they were going to bring Corthos to Amberhold).
- **Erik Bufford of Bufford's Landing** (north of Amberhold) is one of the Lords leading the movement to gather more power.
