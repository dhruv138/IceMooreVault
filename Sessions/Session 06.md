# Session 6 — 2 May 2025

[[WISC]] pulls the three of us into a huddle. When we were in the temple to Abathor, when we went to sleep, he woke up in a dream of a grand hall and Abathor was there. He told him we’d wake up and encounter 20+ armed soldiers. He said he’d protect us if [[WISC]] agreed to give out a handful of coins to people back in [[Truemetal Halls]]. Then [[Furgus]] says he had a very similar dream where Abathor gave the same info, but even though [[Furgus]] didn’t agree to do a favour, he had to sign a contract with Abathor with his blood anyway. [[WISC]] didn’t have to do this.
One of the Bugbears we freed from slavery is named Crez. He comes to us and says that he’s been looking around and thinks he knows where we are. In the distance, he points out columns of clouds rising into the sky. He says, “if I’m not mistaken, those are the hot springs outside of the [[Black Dragon Inn]]. The inn owner there offers training and supplies. We could rest and resupply and then make our way back. Canborg road will take us to [[Truemetal Halls]]. The road is a major road that runs from [[Lakeshire]] to [[Truemetal Halls]]. As we part ways, Crez apologizes for not having anything to give to thank us. He says that if we ever find ourselves in the hamlet of Briar Run, we will have their hospitality.
We make our way to the [[Black Dragon Inn]]. The Inn itself is a beautiful Tudor style building. On the building are black dragon wings unfurled. They look remarkably preserved. As we approach the door, it opens for us. We don’t see any horses or carts outside. We enter and see well weathered tables. Chairs surrounding a table pull out for us. We see a sign by the bar that reads, “first drink’s free with a story”. [[WISC]] asks, “didn’t Cruz say that a woman named [[Hilda]] runs this place?” With that, we hear a voice from the back calling, “go ahead and take a seat!” When we sit, menus float to us.
A Halfling with curly gray hair floats out on a Tenzer’s Floating Disk. Both of her legs are missing from the knees down. She comes over and asks for a story in exchange for a round of drinks. We tell her the story of the possessed goat. We show her the stone and she is very interested. She pulls out her spellbook and casts Identify. She says, “interesting. Nothing comes up when I cast Identify.” We ask about the dragon wings. “He got my legs, but I got his wings.”
We spend our time in the hot springs where steam mephits cater to us along with more unseen servants. We ask [[Hilda]] if she recognizes the dagger symbol of Abathor. She does not specialize in religion. We go back to the bar and as the day grows later, we see the bar filling up with various species including Cubans. A Dwarven woman recognizes [[Elzock Emberstone]] and comes over. They go off and chat. He later lets us know that they’re “business acquaintances”. She has done business with the family in the past.
[[Deanira]] spends her evening playing a rapid and dexterous game of cards. [[WISC]] gambles at a game of “In Between”. We wake up the next morning at 11am, having been thrown off by weeks in the darkness and drinks from the night before.
