# Session 8 — 18 June 2025

Upon our return to the city, we visit a new magic shop that has just opened and is still in the process of unpacking, called [[The Shooting Star]]. The proprietor, a gnome with light pink fuzzy hair and the deepest voice we’ve ever heard, Mr. Bubblegum, greets us. He isn’t able to identify what our stone is. We see a few items we’d like to buy eventually.
We sell our dire [[Wolf]] pelts to a leather shop, and she recommends that we attend the market to get a tanning setup, a skinning knife, and salt to cure the pelts. They will then be worth 10 gold each in a decent condition.
We then go to the War Hammer and the owner [[Gunther Rockhammer]] calls us over. He gives us a letter that came for us. It’s from the desk of [[Earl Emberstone]]. Thanking us for the safe return of his son, inviting us to the [[Ring of Gold]] to collect our reward.
We head to the [[Ring of Gold]] where we are presented with gifts. [[WISC]] receives a spectacular bill hook with a Raindrop Damascus pattern called “[[Reaper]],” forged by the [[Iron Dwarves]]. [[Furgus]] receives a quiver made of a quilt of chromatic dragon skins, embedded with magical properties. [[Deanira]] receives linens embedded with runes of gold. They are the thunderous handwraps, once worn by a monk dedicated to the god Kord.
When we return to the War Hammer, [[Gunther Rockhammer]] tells us that per our request our belongings have been moved to Room 10. We don’t recall requesting this. We go to the room and see a nicer room with cozier looking bar stools, and noticeably no bed. Out of the shadows, we see a Dwarf step out. He removes his mask and introduces himself as [[Lord Ivan Deepshaft]]. He says he’s a member of the [[Court]] and understands that we recently came across some things that would be beneficial for them to know, like what we discovered in the mineshaft. We share what we learned, but
[[Deanira]] doesn’t offer the map, and we don’t mention Abathor. We share that the Duergar are using the knockers to mine mithral for the army they are raising. When we mention [[Elzock Emberstone]]’s capture, we see the slightest flicker of a smirk cross his face. [[Deanira]] gives him her “mithril” dagger pendant and we tell him that the Duergar believe they are doing this for a higher power and the Knockers appear to also worship this figure. As we prepare to leave, Lord Ivar tells us to not share what we discovered in the mines, in order to avoid a panic and allow the [[Court]] to resolve it quietly.
We go back downstairs and for the first time, [[Gunther Rockhammer]] motions for us to sit down at the booth with him. He says that it seems we have helped out several high powers in the city today. He hands us a leather pouch and informs us that the city will be paying for our room and board as long as we’re here. We each receive a room here at the War Hammer. [[Deanira]] takes the pouch and he tells us to turn it inside out on the bed. We ask him what he knows about the [[Murderhorn]] region. He says it’s very mountainous, and a half elf and his son came through a few months ago wanting to ski the region (our ghost friends who asked us to find their bodies and put them to rest). A lot of people go up but don’t come back, especially due to the weather, which make maps and charts quite useless, as mountain passes close from the wind and snow.
We go up to the rooms and [[Deanira]] turns the sack inside out on her bed. Three sacks fall out along with 3 greater healing potions. In each sack is 300 gold. We received this for providing the city [[Court]] with information about the mines.
