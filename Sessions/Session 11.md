# Session 11 — 6 August 2025

We are fired upon by 5 goliaths. During the fight, one of the archers turns on the other warriors and says he’ll join us and offer information on the camp. He wants vengeance. We see that he’s covered in bruises in various stages of healing, and he’s telling us the truth. [[WISC]] and [[Furgus]] disarm him and we talk. He says his name is [[Uthal]]. He tosses [[Deanira]] some nice bandages. He tells us that there are another dozen goliaths in the region, scattered between different raiding parties and the camp. [[Ilkan]] is the new leader. [[Uthal]]’s father used to be the tribal leader. They weren’t raiders, but were nomads and herders. [[Ilkan]] came back one day, smarter. He kept coming up with new ideas like contraptions to improve their chances and better ways to make it through the [[Triborn Pass]]. [[Ilkan]] killed [[Uthal]]’s father and took over leadership of the tribe. They now have a Druid named [[Alkan]] who is often sent out as a hawk or owl to scout. We remember that the previous night we heard the hoot of an owl. That’s how they found us. [[Ilkan]] keeps [[Uthal]] alive and in servitude as an insult to his father’s name. A little less than a day’s travel up the mountain is the camp.
He says that a little while back they found a caravan with fine rum and cigars, so at night many of them drink, which could be to our advantage. After [[Uthal]] kills [[Ilkan]], he wants to return to being a nomad and working for people as a herder.
With regards to strategy, we plan to attack at night, when the Goliaths can’t see well and are likely drunken (mechanically the poisoned condition). We are considering having [[Uthal]] go to the camp and tell them they need help with loot so that, so that they peel off from the main camp and we pick them off at a choke point we create at the bottom of the path.
We head to the camp. When we arrive, it’s twilight and the residents are beginning to light the fires and open casks of rum. [[Riffle]] sends their familiar to scout and get a lay of the land. 3 big guys, 3 archers, 1 sleeping Druid, and 1 leader. 4 tents are
also there and perhaps occupied. The Druid is close to the boss, and the boss is not drinking.
The Plan:
We plan to take a long rest now, wake up around 0400 when it’s still dark, when they’re drunk, asleep, and can’t see in the dark. As we make our way up into the camp, we will systematically take out the braziers. We will first take out the two closest to the log so we have the cover of darkness. We’re hoping that Rifle can command the two guards to flee, since we can’t one shot them.
