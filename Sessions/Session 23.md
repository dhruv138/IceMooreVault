# Session 23 — 5 February 2026

So we’re exiled from [[Wren Nalore]]… [[WISC]] heads towards town to visit the guard tower to see what exactly we are in trouble for (we know it’s to do with [[Riffle]]’s nonsense). He offers to settle things in a gentlemanly way and asks for the details of our charges. They go to get the Captain Jack Offman. He explains that it’s in relation to harassing one of the elderly ladies in the town. We were tried in absence and found guilty, but we can come to the town hall to stand trial in person. Through [[WISC]]’s discussions (and bribe) with the Captain of the guard, we will receive all of our goods we left (Dante our horse, and our donkey - which is just on loan for this quest) and can receive our goods from the [[Living Bloom]] to deliver to the apothecary in [[Truemetal Halls]]. They deliver our horse and donkey loaded with the goods. We go through and make sure that everything is there. We head away from the town to set up camp for the night, but [[Riffle]] sneaks off to thaumaturgy the guard tower, making it appear to be set on fire, the windows fly open, and loud raven shrieks echo through the tower. They hear, “to arms! To arms! There is skullduggery about!” A guard does see [[Riffle]], and shoots an arrow right past their head. As they weave back to the camp through the woods, [[Riffle]] runs into a troll pulling bird nests out of trees and eating the eggs. Thankfully the troll doesn’t see them. They keep getting lost and don’t end up returning until morning. In the middle of the night, seeing that [[Riffle]] hasn’t returned from wherever they have sauntered off to, [[WISC]] searches the immediately surrounding woods for any sign of [[Riffle]]’s trail. In the morning, [[Riffle]] saunters up to camp like a teenager doing the walk of shame. [[Deanira]] asks “where were you last night? We were worried.” [[Riffle]] responds, “am okay.” [[WISC]] says, “It’s less about if you’re okay; are OTHER people okay?! Do we need to GO?” [[Furgus]] adds, “and how fast do we need to go?” [[Riffle]] assures us no one was hurt, but [[Deanira]] packs up very quickly nonetheless.
As we pass near the camp where we found the goblin child, we hear a whistle and see [[Tiny]], the Goliath Cleric from [[The Bastard]]’s Band.
He says that they are making their way South and that his party is staying in the raided goblin village. He recommends we join them, as there are brigands about. We join. We ask [[The Bastard]] where they are heading, and he says The [[Emerald Glade]]. We can tell he’s much more subdued and not as jovial as he usually is. We see that [[Tiny]] (Goliath Cleric) and [[Glasses]] (Gnome Wizard) are here with [[The Bastard]], but [[The Poet]] is missing. We help set up camp and conjure a smoked country ham from The [[Staff of Ham]]. [[WISC]] portions the food, and [[Deanira]] tends to the horse and donkey. [[Furgus]] asks if they’re okay, as we have noticed they are acting more solemn and serious. He says it’s been a hard few weeks and that he’ll tell us more tonight. While they look healthy, it looks like they’ve been travelling hard and fast, and haven’t taken the time to repair their clothes, have a hot meal, rest, and recover between quests. We only see 3 bedrolls set up for them.
We all sit down to share a meal and [[Deanira]] asks if [[The Poet]] is here.
[[The Bastard]] says, “yeah, he’s with us,” and plays with a necklace around his neck with a vial containing some hairs. They were contracted to go down and deal with the Duergar in the mines that we discovered. They were able to disrupt the activities of the Duergar, but [[The Bastard]] asks, “did you guys see that area with the mushrooms?” We did not go that one route. The Duergar were distilling the mushroom extract to create a type of paralytic. [[The Poet]] got attacked and didn’t make it, but they were able to grab some of his hairs. They are heading down to the [[Emerald Glade]] in Emerald Grove to see if the Druids down there can help.
We all get ready for bed and we set up the [[Cornhusk Dolls]] and the plush water buffalo, but nothing happens to them.
In the morning, [[The Bastard]]’s Band are gone. [[Deanira]] hears a voice in her head belonging to the Wizard’s assistant in [[Truemetal Halls]], “The Master would like to see you. Are you available now?” We say yes, but we are still a week’s travel away. A portal appears before us, large enough to fit our horse and donkey as well. We go through. We find ourselves in the Wizard’s shop, and the assistant escorts our animals
away. We see four individuals before us. [[Hambrick Onyxcrest]], a female half-elf wearing robes from Mistra’s Academy of magic. [[Talir Rengavi]] Rengavi the tallest human we’ve ever seen ([[Riffle]]’s father figure and the head of the Library) and a small gnome with a pixie cut [[Hana Goldfirm]] (Rifffle’s best friend from the Library). [[Riffle]]’s Library is an offshoot of Mistra’s Academy. Within the normal library, there is the Department of Aquisitions, which consists of two teams of two, one of which is [[Hana Goldfirm]], [[Riffle]]’s researcher.
