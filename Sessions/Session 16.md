# Session 16 — 9 October 2025

Baron Tyrack
Baron Tyrack
Kaeenbane. [[Deanira]] asks about the cultural customs. The burials vary with the family and their spiritual wishes. Most Dwarves worship Moroden, but not always. There are other options besides cremation in [[The Forge]], but when Winter hits, ground burials are not possible due to the freezing of the earth. As far as preparing the body, occasionally a wake is held if the family wishes. The body will always arrive to [[The Forge]] covered in a shroud. It is an ancient tradition that the priest never look upon the body. If the family is too poor to fund the funeral or pay for the costs associated with the preparation and disposal of the body, there are options where the city can help with contracting undertakers or junior priests. [[Elzock Emberstone]] tells us that he’s been frequenting a pub down in the miner’s area called [[Kicking Ass]].
[[Riffle]] has long since disappeared. While we have been talking, they have been seeking out the priest who performed the rites. They follow the priests to the side room where they all make their way to. One of the younger priests notices them and they talk. [[Riffle]] introduces
Themself as a priest of Denier. [[Riffle]] asks about the Dwarf who fell in the market and died named Dagin Tillmore There is a ledger in the office and [[Christolf Nightfall]] is the undertaker that took care of this body. The cremation of this body was paid for by the city. [[Riffle]] looks for other names associated with [[Christolf Nightfall]]. They memorise and commit it to memory in Kenku fashion. They take note that the office has a standard lock on it as they leave. While making their way back to us, they copy three names down on a scroll to show us. [[WISC]] and [[Deanira]] read the names Okrede Earthview, Sven Surefoot, and Dur. All of the names were associated with [[Christolf Nightfall]] and were funded by the city.
[[WISC]] wants to find out more information from one of the priests, a female Dwarven priest named [[Vistra]], under the guise of looking for work as an undertaker. [[Riffle]] is interested in breaking into the priest office to steal the ledger for copymaking, and [[Deanira]] wants to map out [[The Forge]] and look for any offices, side rooms, hidden doorways, and especially a place where the bodies are kept or prepared when a family is not involved.
We head to where the priestess [[Vistra]] told [[WISC]] that [[Christolf Nightfall]] has his office, in a poorer, more accessible part of the city. [[Riffle]] goes into the shop first. They introduce themself as a priest of Denier and that they have travelled here from [[Amberhold]] to find Dagin Tillmore. They walk in and see a tall human male with a deep voice, this is not [[Christolf Nightfall]]. When [[Riffle]] asks for Nightfall, the man tells him that he’s occupied in “the workshop”. This man introduces himself as [[Nastil]], the assistant undertaker. [[Christolf Nightfall]] is the Master undertaker. This guy [[Nastil]] is incredibly thick, he doesn’t know shit and has zero curiosity. [[Riffle]] gets frustrated and screams in the guy’s voice “Master Nightfall, Master Nightfall, you are needed upstairs IMMEDIATELY. It’s an EMERGENCY.” [[WISC]] and [[Deanira]] barge into the shop. This assistant [[Nastil]] is very, very pale. The three of us notice that his chest does not move with breaths. As [[WISC]] talks to [[Nastil]], [[Riffle]] uses the opportunity to sneak to the door behind him that leads to the stairs to the workshop.
[[Riffle]] tries to pick the lock but is not successful. The assistant notices and we roll initiative. In the middle of the fight, [[Riffle]] charms [[Nastil]] and tells him to take us downstairs to see Nightfall. The charm is successful. [[Nastil]] rips the door off the hinges and leads us down the stairs. Inside the workshop, there are several whole dead bodies.
[[Christolf Nightfall]] is not in this space, so [[Nastil]] knocks on another door and says, “[[Christolf Nightfall]], you have visitors.” The suggestion drops now that his task has been completed. We reenter combat and [[Deanira]] is downed a few times by critical hits. We kill the undead creature, but the raven familiar from upstairs flies down and hits a rune on the ceiling above us. We are hit by a vast amount of cold damage and hear silence on the other side of the door. It takes us 5 minutes to break through the door. On the other side we see some desks, alchemical supplies, bookshelves, an open chest, and a faintly smoky air. We smell the scent of brimstone. The room is devoid of any people. [[Riffle]] finds a letter that he attempted to burn.
We do discover another room with a caged door containing roughly carved wooden arms and legs. [[Deanira]] realises that these appendages could replace the missing limbs under a burial shroud. Guards have now arrived and we explain the situation. The captain of the guard takes over and orders his guards to head to [[Christolf Nightfall]]’s house. We don’t tell them about the golem we encountered. [[Riffle]] is concerned about this being covered up to some extent, especially if the city is involved on some level. We can check in on the progress of this case by buying any of the officers a drink. What we learn from the note is that there was a fabled Mithril mine that was so poisonous that even Dwarves who are naturally poison resistant cannot go into the mine. As a result, it was sealed off. (As a note, the poison is naturally occurring, not magical).
