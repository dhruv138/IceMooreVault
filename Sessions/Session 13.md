# Session 13 — 20 August 2025

Now that the fight is over, we take a good look through the camp.
The Druid’s tent tells us that this was her permanent residence. [[Deanira]] finds a [[Music Box]] in her tent. She recognises the song Meredith’s Lullaby from the human lands, known in taverns as the corrupted version Meredith’s Tits. She pockets the [[Music Box]]. We decide to take a long rest. We assume the Druid ran off to the others (a party of 4 remains) who have likely prepared an ambush on the road 2 miles away. We keep a watch overnight in case of attack. At some point in the middle of the night, [[Uthal]] disappears. We make our way to the road where we know a crane they constructed lays.
We burn it down along with leftover crates from a raid. Our job done, we decide to take the path back that leads past The [[Black Dragon Inn]].
While at the Inn, we learn more about [[Riffle]]. They work for the [[Library of Deneir]] in [[Amberhold]], an organisation that collects information ranging from books and knowledge to artefacts and secrets. They are a clandestine agent tasked to collect this knowledge and hidden information. On Dante, [[Riffle]] found some letters, one of which speaking of The [[Dunnings Auction House]] and an upcoming auction the first Monday after the Beaver Moon. The auction will include a quill of Deneir, and [[Riffle]] being a Cleric of Deneir, the god of scribes/knowing, they are very very interested.
They are also interested in acquiring the other items. The catch is that the auction house is in [[Western Point]], over a month’s journey away. Thankfully it’s around June right now and the Beaver Moon isn’t until Autumn (November), so we have time to plan. [[Deanira]] is looking forward to the opportunity to chart down South.
When we return to [[Truemetal Halls]], we go to [[The Warhammer]]. [[Gunther Rockhammer]] calls over [[Furgus]] and hands him a letter. [[Furgus]] tells us after reading it that he thinks [[Gunther Rockhammer]] might be racist.
Apparently [[Furgus]] and [[WISC]] are being invited back to the farm to participate in the Summer Festival with [[Furgus]]’s family. It’s a community work week turned celebration that is equivalent to a county faire. It takes place in about a month’s time. [[Furgus]] is indifferent to going, so leaves it open to the rest of us.
