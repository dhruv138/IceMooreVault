# Session 19 — 6 November 2025

We wake up in our individual rooms in the Bog’s Rest and each find corn husk dolls resembling us once again sitting outside our respective doors. [[WISC]] learns that someone in town named Old Edith makes corn husk dolls locally (this is common in every town). While eating breakfast down in the pub, [[Deanira]] tells her companions she had a vision at the temple. They go upstairs and smoke the [[Dream Weed]] to share her memory. She shares her backstory as well. After this, [[Riffle]] goes up to [[WISC]] and looks uncomfortably close into one of his eyes. [[Riffle]] eventually shares that they saw the same rare eye colour in a bugbear who was part of that raider group from Durham Hold.
[[WISC]]’s father left before [[WISC]] was a baby, and [[WISC]] says “he’s a deadbeat”. It is possible that this other bugbear is a younger half-brother of [[WISC]]’s.
We learn that today in town there is a water buffalo race. We head to the stadium. [[Deanira]] and [[WISC]] are interested in betting, and [[Riffle]] wanders off to do some [illegal] “shopping”. The water buffalo race is a mix between horse racing and water skiing, where the riders hang onto reins trailing behind the beast and “ski” on the mud track. [[WISC]] and [[Deanira]] make their bets. [[Deanira]] loses hers and [[WISC]] wins one. [[Riffle]] does some pickpocketing and put 2 gp into [[Furgus]]’s pocket. [[Deanira]] buys a water buffalo beer stein. [[WISC]] buys [[Riffle]] a water buffalo race t-shirt and for himself buys a stuffed water buffalo.
We go to see Old Edith about the corn husk dolls. They’re not hers, and we get kicked out of her shop/home because [[Riffle]] begins going through her things.
We set out on our journey to hunt for basilisk eggs. As we make our way through the swampy forest hours later, we suddenly hear a creaking below us. [[Deanira]] jumps out of the way before the rest fall through a wooden floor that breaks from the prolonged weather damage. They land on a stone floor 15 feet below and as they get up, they look right into the face of a green dragon. After their initial fear, they notice
that it’s a stone sculpture. They find themselves in a room with rusted spears and a single door. They hear movement on the other side.
