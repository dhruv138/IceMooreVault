# Session 39 — 14 October 2026

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Inside the [[Owens Family Mausoleum]], teleport traps drop [[Riffle]] and [[Deanira]] into poison-filled sarcophagi until [[WISC]] frees them. A hidden crypt shows that Lord Richard and Lady Gezibel Owens's coffins are empty, and the family crest teleports the party into a booby-trapped crate maze under the [[Owens Warehouse]]. In a laboratory with caged, vacant-eyed captives and notes reading "Need female phase" and "Deanira? Coda's Landing", [[Peter Owens]] sets his zombified parents on the party. They destroy the greater zombies and capture Peter, whose spellbook includes *polymorph*.

## Session Notes

### The Mausoleum
With [[Riffle]] and [[Furgus]] being invisible, [[Deanira]] and the rest of the party sneak to the door to see if it is locked behind [[Peter Owens|Owen's]]. She opens it easily and carefully and sees that it is lit within. Within the stone mausoleum are 4 sarcophagi.

[[Riffle]] somehow sneaks in front of [[Deanira]] without being seen, and as they both enter they feel a magical hook pull them behind the navel and vanish into darkness. [[Furgus]], [[WISC]], and [[Theo'din Corvis|Theo]] see them disappear. [[Furgus]] feels the same tug but doesn't move: the glyph couldn't find another sarcophagus to put him into. The glyph is still intact.

[[Deanira]] and [[Riffle]] take a breath in and choke on the poison gas that begins to fill the dark space. They do see the roughness of stone around them but can't make out anything else. [[WISC]] lifts off the lid of the first sarcophagus and [[Furgus]] readies an attack. [[Riffle]] yells and pounds on the stone; we are stuck in the sarcophagi. [[Deanira]] screams, rolls onto her back, and begins kicking the lid with her hooves. [[WISC]] rescues [[Riffle]] and [[Deanira]].

### The Hidden Crypt
[[WISC]] removes the lid of another sarcophagus and finds that it's wood disguised as stone. Inside the tomb is a ladder leading down. [[Furgus]] goes down first and the room is similarly lit. There are 6 stately coffins and a crest painted on the wall. The [[Owens Family|crest]] has a ship with a mermaid holding an orb in her outstretched hand as the figurehead.

The coffins are labeled:
- **Harold Owens** & **Margaret Owens**
- **Nicholas Brightvine** & **Elizabeth Brightvine**
- **[[Richard Owens|Lord Richard Owens]]** & **[[Gezibel Owens|Lady Gezibel Owens]]** ([[Peter Owens|Peter]]'s parents)

[[Furgus]] notices that there is no smell of putrefaction and it's strange... We open [[Richard Owens|Richard]] and [[Gezibel Owens|Gezibel]]'s coffins and they are both empty. [[Furgus]] walks over to the crest on the wall. He touches the orb and vanishes.

### The Warehouse Labyrinth
We follow suit and find ourselves in another dark room, this time with a group of crates stacked high. We initially wonder if we're on a ship, but the ground is still and made of stone, and the ceiling is the underside of a wooden floor. The crates are filled with various cloths and we find a note of delivery to the [[Owens Warehouse|warehouse]] of **[[Richard Owens|Lord Richard Owens]]**.

[[Riffle]] sneaks to the top of the crates and realizes that the crates are laid out intentionally like a maze. We make our way through the labyrinth. 5 glowing spheres drop invisibility and shoot at [[Deanira]], [[Theo'din Corvis|Theo]], and [[WISC]] and then float back into the boxes. We get through the labyrinth, dealing with the orbs several more times.

### The Laboratory
We enter a new room with hanging lights, cages built into the walls, and two objects that seem like tables covered with sheets. If we didn't know better, they'd look like operating tables with sheets drawn over bodies. Two of the four cages have a slumped-over body in them, possibly still alive... they are breathing at least. Their eyes are open and vacant and they have soiled themselves.

[[Riffle]] immediately beelines for the desk across the room and finds it full of scrap paper with arcane equations. Anatomy books and physiology papers about various animals fill the drawers.
- A paper is blacked out with only the words **"[[Peter's Experiments|brass shield and the sapphire of the plains]]"** remaining.
- **"Need female phase"** underlined 3 times.
- Several names are crossed out before **"[[Deanira]]? [[Coda's Landing]]"** is written.

[[Deanira]] heads over to one of the tables, lifts the corner, and sees part of an incredibly muscular arm with large nails. She feels a tug behind her navel but resists it this time.

### Confrontation with Peter
[[Peter Owens|Peter]] emerges and calls on his [[Gezibel Owens|mother]] and [[Richard Owens|father]] to take care of us. [[Furgus]] shoots [[Peter Owens|Peter]] with an [[Arrow of the Harpy]], charming him into a stupour, and [[Deanira]] uses the [[Immovable Rod|immovable rod]] to impale [[Richard Owens|Richard]] and fix him to the spot. We finish off the greater zombies and tie up [[Peter Owens|Peter]]. We find a [[Peter's Spellbook|spell book]] on [[Peter Owens|Peter]] and it includes **polymorph** but not **disguise self** or **alter self**.

### Aftermath
We step outside and find ourselves at a riverfront on the docks. Interestingly, the door to the [[Owens Warehouse|warehouse]] appears as a wall from the outside. We aren't completely sure we're in [[Amberhold]]. [[Deanira]] looks around behind the warehouse and sees the lights of a city, getting the feeling that they are still in the [[Amberhold]].

[[Riffle]] goes to check on the people in the cages and gets the sense that this is not medical or necromantic but [[Peter's Experiments|supernatural]]. [[Theo'din Corvis|Theo]] offers to send his raven to deliver a letter from [[Riffle]] to [[Talir Rengavi|Talir]]. For the moment, [[Peter Owens|Peter]] is tied and restrained, gagged with WISC's super smelly socks.
