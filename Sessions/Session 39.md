# Session 39 — 8 October 2026

> [!summary] TL;DR
> The party finds six of the seven [[Missing Sanitation Workers|missing sanitation workers]] chained beneath the [[Owens Family Mausoleum]], destroys two ghouls and captures a [[Skeletal Hand Acolyte|Skeletal Hand acolyte]] carrying a Library ledger page about the old red robes. [[Riffle]] is cleared of the theft from [[Talir Rengavi|Talir]], whose stolen documents turn out to be the warding plans for the stone's display at the [[Academy Gala|Gala]], and one red robe is missing from [[Storeroom B]]. A [[Man in the Grey Mask|man in a grey mask]] watches from the graveyard, and [[Angela]] vanishes from the Velvet Glove.

Standing in the rain outside the [[Owens Family Mausoleum|mausoleum]], [[Riffle]] sends a message back to [[Talir Rengavi|Talir]]: "Was not me. Was not in office. Will explain. Please do not burn bell tower." [[Talir Rengavi|Talir]] doesn't answer. [[Riffle]] is visibly rattled but insists we finish what we came here to do first.
The [[Owens Family Mausoleum|mausoleum]] belongs to the [[Owens Family Deaths|Owens family]]. Carved above the door: "OWENS — WHAT THE SEA GIVES, THE SEA KEEPS". Inside are two new stone coffins, [[Owens Family Deaths|Lord Richard Owens]] and [[Owens Family Deaths|Lady Margaret Owens]], with fresh white lilies on both. [[Theo'din Corvis|Theo]] feels a cold prickle along his arms, the kind the [[Raven Queen's servants]] are taught to recognise. Someone has been doing necromancy in here recently, but not on the bodies; [[Riffle]] checks and both corpses are still very much dead (and smell like it).
[[Furgus]] notices scuff marks on the floor in front of an older coffin belonging to a great-grandfather, Bartholomew Owens. [[WISC]] pushes it and it slides aside on hidden rollers. Underneath is a spiral stair going down. We hear dripping water and smell the sewers. [[Deanira]] realises this must connect to the sewers where the [[Missing Sanitation Workers|sanitation workers went missing]].
At the bottom is an old brick tunnel, much older than the sewers above it. [[Deanira]] maps as we go. We find a sanitation worker's lantern and an orange city work vest with the name "PIM" stitched on it. Further on, the tunnel opens into a round chamber with old drains on every side. Six people are chained to the walls. Four of them are alive. Two are not, and they get up when we walk in.
We roll initiative. The two dead workers have been turned into ghouls, and a robed figure on a ledge above starts chanting. [[Furgus]] shoots him in the shoulder and his hood falls back: it's a young man with dark hair and a [[Skeletal Hand]] tattoo on his wrist, NOT [[Blond Skeletal Hand Recruiter|the blond guy]]. [[Deanira]] plays the [[Pipes of the Sewers]] and about forty rats pour out of the drains and swarm the ghouls, which buys [[WISC]] time to cut the chains. [[Theo'din Corvis|Theo]] calls on the [[Raven Queen]] and both ghouls crumble to ash. The [[Skeletal Hand Acolyte|acolyte]] tries to run but [[WISC]] throws the [[Javelin of Lightning]] at him and he goes down.
The four survivors are [[Gertrude Mossbottom|Gertrude "Gert" Mossbottom]] (the sanitation foreman, a very angry halfling), [[Pim Lark]] (the young human whose vest we found), and two of the city searchers, Odo and Fenwick. That makes six of the seven missing people. [[Gertrude Mossbottom|Gert]] says the seventh, a searcher named [[Hollis]], was taken to "the [[Man in the Grey Mask|man in the grey mask]]" two days ago and never came back. She says the masked man talked about needing "fresh ones, not old ones" for "the night of the stone".
The [[Skeletal Hand Acolyte|acolyte]] is alive but unconscious. On him we find: a [[Black Iron Key|black iron key]], a vial of grave dust, 14 gold, and a folded page of [[Library of Deneir|Library]] letterhead. [[Riffle]] recognises it immediately as a page from the [[Library of Deneir|Library]]'s storeroom ledger. It lists "Ceremonial robes (red, old pattern), 12, [[Storeroom B]], sub-basement". Someone had circled the line and written "11" next to it.
We bring the survivors and the prisoner up through the [[Owens Family Mausoleum|mausoleum]]. On the way out [[Philip]] spots someone watching us from the graveyard wall, a tall cloaked figure in a grey mask. When [[Theo'din Corvis|Theo]] steps toward him, he turns and walks into the rain and is gone. Nobody gets a good look.
The city watch takes the [[Skeletal Hand Acolyte|acolyte]]. The captain, [[Imelda Thorne|Captain Imelda Thorne]], thanks us and says the city will pay the 200gp reward once the foreman confirms everything, plus extra for the searchers. [[Gertrude Mossbottom|Gert]] hugs [[WISC]] around the knees and won't let go for a while.
The next morning [[Riffle]] goes to see [[Talir Rengavi|Talir]] in person, with [[Hana Goldfirm|Hana]]. [[Talir Rengavi|Talir]] is cold at first. [[Riffle]] explains that they were in [[Karyn]]'s office tidying up when [[Talir's Stolen Documents|the documents]] were taken, and [[Karyn]] backs this up when [[Hana Goldfirm|Hana]] fetches her. [[Siobhan]] is called in. She is certain she saw a kenku in RED robes go into [[Talir Rengavi|Talir]]'s office and leave by the east stair, but when [[Riffle]] stands next to her she admits it was "maybe a bit taller". [[Talir Rengavi|Talir]] apologises, awkwardly. He finally tells us what was stolen: the plans for the warding glyphs on the display vault where [[Mystery Stone|the stone]] will be shown at the [[Academy Gala|Gala]]. Whoever has them knows exactly how the stone is protected.
[[Riffle]] shows [[Talir Rengavi|Talir]] the ledger page. [[Talir Rengavi|Talir]] goes pale. He goes down to [[Storeroom B]] with us and there are only [[Missing Red Robe|eleven red robes]] in the crate.
Meanwhile, [[Deanira]] goes back to [[The Two Tits|the Two Tits]] to see [[Atheela]]. [[Atheela]] says the [[Union of Many]] has heard that a fertility statue from the old [[Dragon League]] continent is going to be sold at the [[Dunnings Auction House|Dunnings auction]] in [[Western Point]]. [[Deanira]] is now VERY interested in the [[Southern Auction|southern auction]].
[[WISC]] and [[Furgus]] go to the [[Mistra's Academy|Academy]] practice yard to watch the other [[Gauntlet]] teams train. One team is from the [[Mistra's Academy|Academy]] itself, [[Team Ember]], led by a very serious tiefling evoker named [[Corvina Ashgrove]]. Another is a team of halfling adventurers called the [[The Copper Kettles|Copper Kettles]], led by [[Marigold Thistlewick]], who challenges [[Furgus]] to an archery contest. [[Furgus]] wins by one point. [[Marigold Thistlewick|Marigold]] says they're friends now.
[[Philip]] spends the day at the [[Cock and Pig]] with [[Evelyn Keep]] and the sailors. One of them, a sailor named [[Bertram]], says his crew saw [[Floating Island Mystery|an island with a broken white tower]] on it two nights out from the city, and the next morning there was nothing but sea. [[Philip]] writes it down for [[Deanira]].
On the way back to [[The Three Candles]], [[WISC]] and [[Theo'din Corvis|Theo]] pass a wine shop where four men in white tabards are smashing bottles. They are the [[Sect of Torm|followers of Torm]]. Their leader, [[Aldric Venn|Brother Aldric Venn]], tells us drink "dulls the discipline the gods require" and that the city will be "clean before the [[Academy Gala|Gala]]". [[Theo'din Corvis|Theo]] asks why the [[Academy Gala|Gala]] matters to them and [[Aldric Venn|Aldric]] just smiles and walks off.
That night [[Theo'din Corvis|Theo]] finds a new [[Cornhusk Dolls|cornhusk doll]] on his pillow. It looks like [[Philip]].
Late that night [[Riffle]] gets a Sending from [[Sabrina]]: "[[Angela]] didn't come to work. Her room at the [[Velvet Glove Brothel|Velvet Glove]] is empty. She left everything."
