# Session 37 — 12 September 2026

## Summary

The party spent the day investigating several threads in [[Amberhold]]. [[WISC]] and [[Furgus]] were cured of lycanthropy at the [[Temple of Mistra]], while [[Deanira]] learned that the [[Mask of the Green Dragon]] had placed a minor curse on her. The party accepted a job to find missing sanitation workers, made contact with a probable [[Skeletal Hand]] recruiter, gathered new reports of the mysterious moving island, and uncovered leads relating to both [[Daggins Murder]] and strange activity beneath [[The Two Tits]]. [[Theo'din Corvis|Theo]] also communed with the [[Raven Queen]] and reaffirmed the mission of the [[Order of Black Feathers]].

## Locations Visited

- [[Temple of Mistra]] — [[Samuel Patel]] diagnosed Deanira's curse and arranged the party's treatment.
- [[Student Health Clinic]] — a healer removed the Mask of the Green Dragon from Deanira and ended its influence.
- An Amberhold cartography shop — Deanira bought four maps of other continents and asked about the unchartable island.
- An Amberhold tavern — the party followed the coded scholarship flyer and met a suspected Skeletal Hand recruiter.
- Amberhold docks — Deanira questioned sailors about mysterious islands and lights.
- Temple of the [[Raven Queen]] — Theo communed with the Matron.
- [[The Two Tits]] — Deanira observed a possible secret fertility cult meeting in the basement.

## NPCs Encountered

- [[Samuel Patel]] — cleric at the Temple of Mistra who identified Deanira's minor curse.
- A Student Health Clinic healer — removed the Mask from Deanira.
- An Amberhold city representative — hired the party to find three missing sanitation workers and four missing searchers.
- [[Blond-Haired Youth]] — responded to the coded scholarship order, discussed Academy admission and paid quests, and concealed a Skeletal Hand tattoo.
- An unidentified black-haired woman — followed the blond recruiter when he left under a possible *disguise self* spell.
- Sailors at the docks — supplied two reports connected to the moving-island mystery.
- The head priest of the Raven Queen's temple — directed Theo to commune with the Matron.
- [[Ester]] — told Riffle that the Library's robes changed from red to purple roughly 50–60 years ago.

## Quest Progress

### [[Missing Sanitation Workers]]

The party accepted a 200 gp city job to enter the sewers and find three sanitation workers who vanished several days ago. Four people sent to search for them have also disappeared.

### [[Floating Island Mystery]]

Deanira obtained two geographically distant sailor reports:

- Near the Barren Toll, lights appeared to be entering port even though the witnesses were days from port; nothing remained at sunrise.
- Two days from Wellview Helm, sailors saw an island in the distance that was gone the next day.

The widely separated locations support the idea that the island moves or manifests unpredictably.

### [[Skeletal Hand]]

The party answered a magically concealed flyer promising deeply discounted Academy scholarships. At the named tavern they ordered a beer, an ale, and wine together. The [[Blond-Haired Youth]] approached them, said Academy admission was not guaranteed, and offered quest work to unsuccessful applicants. [[Furgus]] glimpsed a Skeletal Hand tattoo beneath his sleeve. He apparently used *disguise self* while departing, followed by an unidentified black-haired woman.

The [[Blond-Haired Youth]] may be the same person whose tattoo [[Cam Jergins]] copied and the same recruiter described by a dead Skeletal Hand member after the confrontation with [[Christolf Nightfall]].

### [[Daggins Murder]]

Riffle learned from [[Ester]] that the Library changed its robes from red to purple 50–60 years ago because a former headmaster preferred the new colour. Some people may have retained old red robes. This provides a possible source for the red robes used by the person who framed Riffle.

### [[Cornhusk Dolls]]

Theo left one of the dolls in the Raven Queen's temple after communing with the Matron.
