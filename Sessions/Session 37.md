# Session 37 — 12 September 2026

%% card:start %%
**Session:** 37 · Played 12 September 2026 (real-world date of the game night, not an in-world date) · **Previous:** [[Session 36]] · **Next:** [[Session 38]]
%% card:end %%

> [!summary] TL;DR
> The were-bear curse is cured and the [[Mask of the Green Dragon]]'s curse is lifted from Deanira. A coded scholarship flyer leads to a blond [[Skeletal Hand]] recruiter, and sailors report vanishing islands. Theo communes with the [[Raven Queen]].


[[Deanira]] wakes up early and the smell of fresh pastries waft upstairs.
She gets a mixed berry turnover on her way to the temple to see the Cleric
that [[Riffle|riffle]] screamed about in the middle of the night. She gets a few
pastries for the others too. Walking through the campus we see people of
widely varying ages and backgrounds. At the [[Temple of Mistra]] we
introduce ourselves and he introduces himself as [[Samuel Patel|samuel Patel]]. He tells
[[Deanira]] that she has a minor curse on her and sends her to the Student
Health Clinic once the others have their lycanthropy cured. The healer
thinks [[Deanira]] is there for an abortion, but eventually she hands the
healer a letter from the Head cleric explaining the situation. She removes
the [[Mask of the Green Dragon]], and suddenly [[Deanira]] feels a lot better
about [[WISC|Wisc]].
[[Deanira]] goes to a cartography shop in the city and purchases 4. maps
of the other continents. She also asks if they have charted anything in
the Feywild or heard anything about a [[Floating Island Mystery|mysterious unchartable island]].
They have not, but they suggest perhaps talking to some of the sailors:
We speak to a city rep who posted on the job board about some missing
persons. She asks us to find [[Missing Sanitation Workers|three sanitation workers]] who vanished in
the sewers a few days ago. They have sent 4 people to go look for them
who also disappeared. We agree to take the job.
Also on the job board we see a flyer that to [[Mistra's Academy|Academy]] associated people
appears to be a flyer for help moving boxes. To the rest of us, we see a flyer
advertising a private benefactor offering scholarships to the [[Mistra's Academy|Academy]] for
vast discounts. It tells us to go to a particular tavern and order a beer, an
ale, and wine all at once and then wait. [[Riffle]] asks [[Talir Rengavi|talir]] if he can read
what it says and [[Talir Rengavi|Talir]] takes this as [[Riffle|riffle]] admitting that they can't
read, and [[Talir Rengavi|Talir]] feels terrible and apologizes for being a "bad" father who
failed them. The misunderstanding is soon cleared up.
on our way to the bar, [[Riffle|riffle]] noticed that many wine/alcohol shops
have obvious security outside their buildings. speaking to a proprietor,
they learn that a [[Sect of Torm|religious sect who worships Torm]], the Lawful Good God
of self Discipline, has been breaking into and destroying various establishments selling alcohol.
At the bar, we order the drinks and sit at a table in the corner. A
young man approaches us and asks why we ordered the drinks we did.
We explain that we are interested in learning magic. He says that they
cannot guarantee that we will be admitted into the [[Mistra's Academy|Academy]], and if we
are not, they would employ us for various quests here and there. [[Furgus]]
sees the hint of the [[Skeletal Hand]] tattoo on his arm covered by a sleeve.
The guy leaves not long after, we get the feeling that he used Disguise
self when he left, and a black haired woman we don't recognize followed
him out.
Based on his description (blond youth), we think that this could be the
guy that the [[Cam Jergins|Gollath sailor]] that [[WISC|wisc]] arm wrestled with saw and copied
his tattoo. We also remember that the guy that we used Speak with Dead
on in the cave where we killed [[Christolf Nightfall|Christoff]] said that he was recruited to the
[[Skeletal Hand]] by a young man with blond hair in [[Amberhold]].

[[Deanira]] speaks to the sailors and gets a fair bit of information:
-Near the Barren Toll, saw lights that looked like they were coming
into port, but they were days away from port. When the sun came up
the next day there was nothing there.
-Two days out from wellview Helm they saw an island in the distance
and the next day it was gone.

The locations are wildly all over the map.

[[Riffle]] asks [[Ester|Esther]] if she remembers when they changed the robes from
red to purple. 50-60years ago the robes were changed because the
Headmaster at the time didn't like them and preferred a different colour.
[[Riffle]] learns that it's possible that some people held onto their old robes.
When [[Riffle]] was framed for killing [[Daddy Dildo Daggins]], the person
sighted was wearing red robes.
[[Theo'din Corvis|Theo]] visits the local [[Temple of the Raven Queen|Temple of the raven Queen]] to check it out. They
see his raven and point slyly to a side door. He waits in there and then
speaks to the Head Priest. He wants to confirm that he's on the right path. 
They suggest that he reach out to the [[Raven Queen]] and ask her
himself (through prayer). He goes into a dark sensory deprivation
(magical Darkness and Silence) room to commune with The Matron.
Eventually he sees a woman appear before him, wearing a white mask, a
black dress, and a cloak of raven feathers. He remembers the tenets of
the [[Raven Queen's servants|order of Black Feathers]]. Their goal is that the dead should stay
dead, and that while it may be acceptable through divine intervention to
call people from the Queen's realm, death should be respected as a
process. You should intervene with any dark magicks that attempt to
violate and steal the souls from the beyond or desecrate their bodies!
vessels. He feels that even though the rest of us are pretty whack, we do
have common goals. As he leaves, [[Theo'din Corvis|Theo]] places one of the [[Cornhusk Dolls|cornhusk dolls]]
in the Temple.
[[Deanira]] returns to the [[The Two Tits]] Inn for dinner and finds it
particularly busy. They seem to be milling around and then
disappearing off one or two at a time, but not through the front door.
One woman comes in holding a staff topped with a wooden knot very
specifically shaped like a vagina. Other people come in with phallic pins:
They pair off and disappear into the basement. ARE THEY
CONNECTED TO DAGGINS' ORDER?! [[Deanira]] wants to continue his
work in collecting "fertility statues" from around the world.
