# Session 25 — 16 February 2026

[[Riffle]] examines the feathers and notices that they are too small to belong to him. They look more like they belong to an actual bird, rather than birdfolk. We hear [[Olga]] announce her entrance that she has brought her daily food for [[Daddy Dildo Daggins]]. [[Deanira]] runs over to [[Olga]] and stops her before she comes into the adjoining room. “[[Olga]], something terrible has happened, we’ve only just seen”. Meanwhile, [[Riffle]] quickly picks up the feathers and smears the blood lettering. [[Furgus]] inspects the knife but there are no distinguishing marks or designs. [[Olga]] runs out and calls for the town guards and [[Furgus]] and [[Deanira]] look around to see what is missing. All of his research and material on [[Axeholm]] is gone. Either he gathered up all of his materials to give to someone, or the killer had enough time to pick through his belongings and gather all of the relevant things. The town guards arrive and we hand over the case.
[[Olga]] heads back to [[The Warhammer]] for a drink, and we decide to head for the Tannery near the necromancer to investigate the whole [[Hector]] thing. We tell [[Gunther Rockhammer]] the owner that we heard from [[Hector]] that the shop has the best leathers around. [[Gunther Rockhammer]] calls out “[[Hector]]” and the kid seems to be exactly the person we saw before. [[Hector]] was apparently playing the game last night called Humans and Households. Last night their characters had to go through “a tax audit”. [[Deanira]] buys a pigskin ball to keep the peace. “Dad it’s not stupid! I’m a level 3 flower arranger!”. We leave, realising that we’re barking up the wrong tree.
Someone must have been pretending to be [[Hector]].
As far as the feathers, there are certainly birds, to the extent that there are birds inside a mall. We head back to [[The Warhammer]] where we see [[Sherlock Gnomes]] waiting for us. He interviews each of us individually. When talking to [[Riffle]], he tells them that there was a witness that saw someone that looked like [[Riffle]] wearing red vestments entering [[Daddy Dildo Daggins]]’s house at midnight. However, [[Riffle]] only wears the colours of Denier, purple and gold, and was taking care of us when we were ill at midnight. [[Sherlock Gnomes]] believes that someone is
trying to impersonate and frame [[Riffle]]. He lets us go and we go shopping. With the family gathering for [[WISC]] and [[Furgus]]’s family coming up in two weeks, we are shopping for things that they may need and always be low on around the time of winter preparation. [[Deanira]] buys 4 Dwarven nesting dolls to bring for the children. [[Furgus]] goes into the fletcher’s shop. In addition to seeing some specialty feathers and buying a few, he also sees some feathers fletched with black feathers that look very much the same as the ones we saw at the crime scene. [[Furgus]] asks about them, and he says that he tried to make some out of Raven feathers, but they were too small and wouldn’t work. He was going to throw them out, but someone came in and wanted to buy just those feathers. [[Furgus]] asks what they looked like. An androgynous human with long blonde hair.
When we arrive back at [[The Warhammer]] after shopping, [[Gunther Rockhammer]] tells us in code that [[Lord Ivan Deepshaft]] is waiting in one of the rooms upstairs to speak to us. He asks us about [[Christolf Nightfall]] who was the undercover necromancer. He informs us that the assistant wasn’t a normal zombie, but a Greater Zombie. He tells us that in order to become a Greater Zombie, the person has to be greater themselves (I.e. not a commoner). He tells us that they sent people to investigate the tombs up here in the North of the heroes who have died. All came back except the one from the tomb of Quithos the Breaker (Goliath hero, centuries ago). He fought against Minotaurs and were able to drive them out. When he died, legend says that they entombed him in honey to preserve his body. Legend also says some Minotaur captives were killed and put in there to protect him and his tomb, along with the treasure buried with him. His tomb is 2 days away from [[WISC]] and [[Furgus]]’s hometown of [[Venberg]] (Venragh on the map). A party of 8 Dwarven soldiers were sent to make sure the tomb was still sealed, but they disappeared. As part of the payment, they are offering to giving us several carts of goods to bring to the people of [[Venberg]] to help in their preparations.
