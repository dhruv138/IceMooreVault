# Session 40 — 25 October 2026

> [!summary] TL;DR
> Under [[Theo'din Corvis|Theo]]'s Zone of Truth, [[Peter Owens]] admits he recruits and experiments for the [[Master of Death]] but denies robbing [[Talir Rengavi|Talir]]'s office. Before he can say more, his tattoo glows green and a Disintegrate spell turns him to ash. [[Talir Rengavi|Talir]] arrives, explains the political danger of a dead Lord, and arranges a [[Royal Guard|royal]] cover story. That night [[Riffle]] finds a note on their pillow asking what secrets Talir is keeping.

## Interrogating "Peter"

- "[[Peter Owens|Peter]]" is bound and gagged with [[WISC]]'s super smelly socks.
- [[Theo'din Corvis|Theo]] casts **Message** to try to communicate with the people in the cages. They don't seem to receive or register it at all. *The lights are on but nobody's home.* (See [[Peter's Experiments]].)
- [[Theo'din Corvis|Theo]] casts **Zone of Truth** and we interrogate him.
  - He says he is **Lord [[Peter Owens]]**.
  - He suggests it was his **parents** ([[Richard Owens]] and [[Gezibel Owens]]) who were doing something to the people in the cages and who created this area.
  - [[Theo'din Corvis|Theo]] threatens him with waterboarding he learned during his time at the Conjuring Initiates Association (CIA).
- **Key admissions (under Zone of Truth):**
  - He **didn't break into [[Talir Rengavi|Talir]]'s office**, has **never framed [[Riffle]]** for anything, and has **never framed a kenku** for anything.
  - [[Riffle]] asks when, where, why, and how he got the tattoo.
  - We confirm he's **working for the [[Master of Death]]**, and the Master is a **he**.
  - [[Peter Owens|Peter]] **finds recruits** for the Master and **does various experiments**.
  - [[WISC]] asks whether the people in the cages are recruits or experiments: they're **test subjects to create bigger and better minions** for the [[Master of Death]].
  - He smiles like he's smarter than everyone else — perhaps even the [[Master of Death]].
  - As far as he knows, **the [[Master of Death]] has told no one his true name**.

## Peter's Death

- [[Furgus]] intimidates him into telling us everything. Right as he's about to speak, his **tattoo glows green** and a **Disintegrate** spell turns him to ash.
- [[Theo'din Corvis|Theo]] stores some of the ashes in a condom.
- [[Furgus]] recalls a **glyph that glowed in the [[Owens Family Mausoleum|mausoleum]]** when [[Deanira]] and [[Riffle]] first stepped into the tomb and were teleported into the sarcophagi. He feels a similar glyph was in the tattoo, triggered by something specific — *possibly speaking the [[Master of Death]]'s name?*

### Loot: Rings found on Peter Owens
Both are transmutation magic.
- **[[Ring of Waterwalking]]** (symbol of waves) — what could [[Peter Owens|Peter]] have been using this for…?
- **[[Pearl of Power]]** (single pearl set into a ring)

## Conji & Talir's Reply

- A soft knocking on the secret door — it's **[[Theo'din Corvis|Theo]]'s raven**, wanting to be let in. It carries a return message and hands it to [[Riffle]].
- [[Theo'din Corvis|Theo]] talks to his raven in his head for the first time ever, apparently. The raven squawks and [[Theo'din Corvis|Theo]] tells [[Riffle]], "you'd understand." [[Riffle]] responds in kind (through mimicry).
- [[Theo'din Corvis|Theo]] introduces his raven as **[[Conji]]**.
- [[Talir Rengavi|Talir]]'s note: he's on his way. Proceed with caution — there could be **kingdom-shaking stakes** at play. Don't do anything stupid.

## Talir Arrives

- We hear a creak in the floor above. [[Furgus]] goes up in the dark to investigate but sees nothing. Something tall and invisible bumps into him and trips over him — it's **[[Talir Rengavi|Talir]]**.
- [[Furgus]] guides him downstairs, both invisible. [[Talir Rengavi|Talir]] drops invisibility and [[Riffle]] gives the full "you must be wondering how we got here" spiel.

### The political situation (per Talir)
- **A Lord is dead.** Even though he was doing shady stuff, [[Peter Owens|Peter]] was still a Lord — this is a delicate problem.
- Many Lords are fine with the current King, but many remember the bliss of a **weak king (the King's father)** when they ran relatively unchecked. (See [[Ambercrown Kingdom]].)
- **The Academy** ([[Mistra's Academy]]) is a third, unofficial power — a large concentration of magic in one place. Generally neutral, but some Lords feel it's too much power in one place.
- [[Talir Rengavi|Talir]] worries the Lords could see this as **a move against the Lords by a party associated with the Academy**.
- **Stolen from [[Talir Rengavi|Talir]]'s secret safe:** his personal documents, notes on the acquisition teams, and reports (including [[Riffle]]'s). See [[Talir's Stolen Documents]].
- **We'll definitely need a cover-up.**
- The delicate balance of power is one of the reasons for **the [[Gauntlet]]**:
  - It was a project of the **late headmaster [[Gavin Gosling|Gavin Goslin]]** to flex the Academy's power.
  - [[Talir Rengavi|Talir]] wasn't a fan, preferring a subtler approach.
  - The headmaster died, but the train was already moving down the tracks.
- With the death of the **[[Owens Family|Owens]] heir**, an inheritance will fall into the vacuum.
- **Cover story:** [[Talir Rengavi|Talir]] will contact someone he knows in the **[[Royal Guard|royal garrison]]** and bring him in — it becomes a royal operation in which [[Peter Owens|Peter]] *perished while resisting royal arrest*.

### The alarm
- [[Riffle]] asks if their alarm went off when [[Talir Rengavi|Talir]]'s office was robbed.
- There's an alarm coded specifically to [[Riffle]] for entering [[Talir Rengavi|Talir]]'s office. **An alarm did go off, but it wasn't [[Riffle]]'s** — even though the **Archivist ([[Siobhan|Shivon]], she)** saw "[[Riffle]]" enter.

### Other concerns
- [[Riffle]] is worried about the **timing**: the [[Master of Death]] animating **[[Corthos]]**, recruiting to the **[[Skeletal Hand|skeletal hand]]**, and creating bigger and badder minions — all very close to the [[Gauntlet]] and concentrated in **[[Amberhold]]** (they were going to bring [[Corthos]] to [[Amberhold]]).
- **[[Erik Bufford]] of [[Bufford's Landing]]** (north of [[Amberhold]]) is one of the Lords leading the movement to gather more power.

## That Night

- We leave the [[Owens Warehouse|warehouse]] and return to our rooms.
- [[Riffle]] finds a note on their pillow: **"What secrets is Talir keeping from you?"** (See [[Pillow Note]].)
- [[Riffle]] gets very concerned about the possibility that [[Talir Rengavi|Talir]] *isn't* keeping secrets and has been entirely truthful, and becomes very distraught.
- [[Deanira]] and [[Riffle]] stay up late on the Party Line discussing signs that [[Talir Rengavi|Talir]] **is** keeping secrets:
  - He had a secret safe [[Riffle]] didn't know about
  - He's involved in the politics of the city/region
  - [[Riffle]] still doesn't know exactly what's going on between him and **[[Bianca Onyxcrest|Bianca]]**
  - He had a surprise secret contact in the [[Royal Guard|royal guard]]
  - etc.

## Next Morning — Three Days Until the Gala

### Fishing
- [[WISC]], [[Furgus]], and [[Deanira]] get up early to go fishing for fish sticks at the pier.
- [[WISC]], [[Furgus]], and [[Theo'din Corvis|Theo]] catch flounder and skates.
- **[[Deanira]] catches 2 salmon and a small barrel of fine cognac!**
- Heading back into the city, we notice a **large presence of [[Royal Guard|royal guards]] around the [[Owens Warehouse]]**.
- **[[Heaven's Touch]]** is about 50 ft from it (converted from a warehouse).
- **Hypothesis:** [[Peter Owens|Peter]] may have used the [[Ring of Waterwalking]] to get from his warehouse to [[Heaven's Touch]].

### Riffle visits Hana
- [[Riffle]] brings [[Hana Goldfirm|Hana]] pastries and coffee and word-vomits all the updates since before the [[Owens Family Mausoleum|mausoleum]].
- They confirm no one has broken into her house and stolen the **copy of the [[Axeholm Map|map of Axeholm]]** [[Riffle]] mailed her as a failsafe.
- They theorize about what the enemy got from [[Talir Rengavi|Talir]]'s safe. [[Riffle]] worries people know about them; [[Hana Goldfirm|Hana]] says information on [[Riffle]] was possibly among the stolen items.
- [[Riffle]] wants to leave to protect everyone, but [[Hana Goldfirm|Hana]] talks them out of it.
- [[Riffle]]: *"Hana, I'm having emotional growth and is not good. Do not like it. Feel like have been pushed into emotional maturity over the past few days. Unrelated, do we know if Gavin Goslin died of natural causes?"*
  - [[Hana Goldfirm|Hana]] thinks so — he was older and it wasn't unexpected. **There was no investigation.** (See [[Gavin Gosling]].)
- [[Riffle]] asks [[Hana Goldfirm|Hana]] to investigate the mention of **[[Deanira]] and [[Coda's Landing]] in [[Peter Owens]]'s notes**.
- During [[Riffle]]'s emotional breakdown, [[Theo'din Corvis|Theo]] messages them: *"Miss you Riffle, hope you're having a good day!"* … *"Want to hang out and go to the brothel?"*
