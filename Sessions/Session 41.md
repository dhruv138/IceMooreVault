# Session 41 — 1 November 2026

> [!summary] TL;DR
> The party shops for the [[Gauntlet]] across [[Amberhold]]: [[Hilda the Blacksmith|Hilda]] agrees to sponsor them and offers cheap custom armour for help with a dangerous smelting project, and [[Furgus]] buys a [[Mystery Key]]. City records show [[Heaven's Touch]] was bought recently by a [[Trent Cole]] and hold no trace of the [[Owens Family|Owens]] properties, while [[Slick Willy Campbell]] tells [[Riffle]] about a guarded ship, the Albatross, said to be for the King's birthday. That evening [[Royal Guard|royal guards]] come for the party, and [[Talir Rengavi|Talir]] tells them to proceed.

## Hilda's Hammer

We begin our shop at **[[Hilda's Hammer]]**, the [[Amberhold]] blacksmith. She ([[Hilda the Blacksmith|Hilda]]) is a Dwarven woman with massive arms, even bigger breasts, and burn scars covering her forearms. She's training an apprentice and we browse her wares.

- [[Theo'din Corvis|Theo]] talks her into **sponsoring us for the [[Gauntlet]]**.
- [[Riffle]] personally intrigues her by asking to order **1500 caltrops**. Given the short timeframe and the sanity of her apprentices, she can supply **100**.
- She offers us a **discount on custom armour** if we help her with a project ([[Hilda's Smelting Project]]). She has been studying the theory of a particular type of metal that needs to be smelted in a special way. The process could cause elemental beings to emerge while she is smelting, and she wants our help keeping them off her back while she works. We agree enthusiastically. **We will do this after the [[Gauntlet]].**

## Zook's Nook

Next we head to the artificer **[[Zook's Nook]]**. [[Furgus]] asks if they would be able to attach the [[Immovable Rod]] to one of his arrows to activate it remotely. The goblin at the counter (**[[Krag]]**) yells for **[[Zook]]**, who then comes out and gets very very close to [[Furgus]] to study him as they talk. He says "interesting..." and then wanders off into the back room.

In the meantime we browse their other wares:

- Clockwork amulet
- Boots of false tracks
- Needle of mending
- Folding boat

[[Theo'din Corvis|Theo]] tries to sell them a very small item that can fill with a substantial amount of liquid without bursting. It's just a condom.

## Odds & Ends

We head to **[[Odds & Ends]]**. **[[Neima]]** offers us:

- An **[[Assquatch]]** (taxidermy deer butt made to look like a humanoid face)
- A **Jenny Hanniver** (a stingray cadaver posed to look like a cryptid)
- A **jackalope** (from the [[Golden Sea]])
- A **wooden artificial leg** (which [[Deanira]] holds up to her legs that bend the opposite way)
- A **[[Mystery Key|mysterious key]]**
- A **hat with a dead vulture** on it
- A **smoking pipe**
- A **raven figurine** (Figurine of Wondrous Power — Silver Raven)

**Purchases:**
- [[Furgus]] buys the **[[Mystery Key]]**.
- [[Deanira]] buys the **[[Assquatch]]** to ward off danger or bad juju during night watches while travelling.

## Varis's Magical Emporium

We next head to **[[Varis's Magical Emporium]]**. **[[Varis]]** is the Elvish husband of [[Hilda the Blacksmith|Hilda]].

[[Deanira]] asks after a **Belt of Hill Giant Strength** and some **Bracers of Defence**. He says that someone from the Academy ([[Mistra's Academy]]) or the Library ([[Library of Deneir]]) recently bought them. He will eventually get more in.

He has:
- A screaming arrow he calls his **"[[Hilda the Blacksmith|Hilda]] Arrow"**
- Potions of healing
- Greater potions of healing
- Ring of evasion
- Arrow of anchoring
- etc.

## The City Office — Furgus

After our shopping, [[Furgus]] goes down to the City Office to look through the records for who owns the warehouse that is now the **[[Heaven's Touch]]**.

- The name **[[Trent Cole]]** comes up. He acquired it very recently, within the past 6 months. The [[Heaven's Touch]] is also this recent.
- There is no business record of anything in between the time of acquisition and the opening of the brothel.
- There is no other record of him owning any other business or property within [[Amberhold]].
- There is also **no record of the [[Owens Family|Owens]]' properties, business, or purchase of Lordship**.

## The Salty Dog — Riffle

[[Riffle]] seeks out **[[Slick Willy Campbell]]**, a common frequenter of many bars. They find him at the **[[Salty Dog]]**. He's been very busy with everyone coming into [[Amberhold]] for the [[Gauntlet]], and the Pelican being stuck in port.

**Willy is a longshoreman** who likes to gossip with everyone, including those involved in merchant/trading business and managing the shipments. He says that there is a **mysterious boat** they were told to stay away from — that there were special people coming to unload it and that no one was to touch it. They say it was for the **King's birthday** (see [[Ambercrown Kingdom]]). The ship is named **the Albatross** ([[Albatross Mystery]]).

## Plans

[[Riffle]] and [[Deanira]] want to scout out the **Arena** before the [[Gauntlet]], but there isn't any word of where it could be. No hint at all. We all then plan to **scout [[Albatross Mystery|the Albatross]] at night**.

## WISC's Secret Errand

[[WISC]] secretly goes back to [[Varis]] and brings his newly acquired **[[Pipe of Remembrance]]**. The pipe typically shows a person's proudest and most heroic deeds. [[WISC]] asks if [[Varis]] can enchant it to instead show the **worst deeds** that someone has done. This way we can know something about the people we meet and are dealing with. [[Varis]] says he will look into it.

## Evening

That evening we cook **fish sticks** :) They give us **+10 to our max HP until our next long rest**.

Some **[[Royal Guard|royal guards]]** approach us and ask us to come with them. [[Riffle]] immediately casts *Sending* to **[[Talir Rengavi|Talir]]** to let him know. [[Talir Rengavi|Talir]] says, **"proceed."**
