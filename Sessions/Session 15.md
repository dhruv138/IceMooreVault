# Session 15 — 20 September 2025

We take a short rest and [[Deanira]] sketches the Dwarven arm with its distinctive scar, in case we want to show anyone the arm. We wake up the Tiefling to learn what she knows about the flesh golem. She was walking to [[Darham Hold]] and all of a sudden the golem erupted from the forest. She was travelling alone to make a rental payment on her land. She said she ran for three miles. [[Darham Hold]] is a half day’s travel down the road. She’s never seen or heard of anything like this before. [[Deanira]] offers her a ride on Dante, since we are headed the same direction and want to check out the woods the golem emerged from. [[Riffle]] knows upon examination that creating a necromancy being like this is extraordinarily expensive. It is unlikely that someone from a smaller town is unlikely to have the tools, resources, knowledge, and finances to pull this off. Someone like that is likely from [[Truemetal Halls]] or [[Eisenberg]], the most prosperous cities behind [[Amberhold]]. [[WISC]] drags the body off the main road so we can return to it on our journey back. [[Deanira]] is walking beside Dante carrying the Tiefling Ea and she is looking for signs of the golem having emerged from the woods. Ea doesn’t remember an exact spot and [[Deanira]] doesn’t find anything that stands out. Up on a hill we see fortifications; this is Durham Hall. A flag hangs from the walls, signifying an axe with ice dripping from the blade.
After dropping off Ea, we decide to hold off on our quest and return to [[Truemetal Halls]] due to the discovery of the arm. When we pass by the body once more, it is still as we left it, though decaying slightly and dead bugs surround it due to the poison. [[Riffle]] is able to identify the type of poison and recognises it as an inhalant and skin irritant, and it is naturally occurring. It is not emitted by Mithril, but we know that underground there can be pockets of poisonous gas (we encountered this in the mines when we found a pit with 2 dead halflings. We got the night vision goggles from this
encounter). Is there a passage to the Underdark in [[The Forge]]? We can tell due to the freshness of the body parts they never got close to being cremated and were stolen and repurposed very quickly. We can tell that this was done by a practiced hand. [[Riffle]] suggests that when we get back we bring this up to the wizard (he would know about necromancy and magic), but first we should explore [[The Forge]].
During a day of travel, we hear whistling approaching us on the wind. We come upon a large figure and a small figure. One is a small goblin with a bowler hat and suspenders, and the other is a huge creature that looks like a cross between a half giant and an ogre also wearing a bowling hat. The goblin is whistling merrily and introduces them as [[Fizz]] and [[Ugly]]’s travelling store. [[Ugly]] unwraps a piece of fabric from inside his bowling hat and spreads it on the ground. It turns into a hole in the ground. [[Fizz]] unwraps a bundle of sticks and shakes them out into the shape of a ladder. [[Riffle]] and [[Deanira]] go down into the interdimensional space and find ourselves in a general store. He has a shovel with Mold Earth, a glass jar filled with “[[Dream Weed]]” - you smoke it with your group and you can transmit memories from your past to others via dreams. He also has two glass vials fused together with a black liquid on one end and a white fluid on the other; you throw it and if people are caught in the flash bang blast, they are blinded for a round upon failing a constitution save. He grabs a small glass vial and summons us upstairs. He asks [[WISC]] to attack the vial with his bill hook and see if it breaks. [[WISC]] hits the [[Unbreakable Vial]] and it reverberates painfully through [[WISC]]’s bill hook. We end up buying almost all of their unique goods. They pack up and walk away with a whistling tune.
Throughout the night we take shifts at watch by the light of the dying fire. [[WISC]] is on watch and gazes into the embers. He sees little figures in the fire, humanoid in shape. He takes a ball bearing from his pack and flicks it at them. It hits one of the figures and it falls over. He keeps his eyes on the fire and wake the rest of us up in the tent. “You hit it and it fell over, isn’t that like physics?” [[Deanira]] rolls over and goes
back to sleep. [[Riffle]] is the only one who wakes up and is curiously inspecting the figures. [[WISC]] is full on hating all of this and is sweating. [[Riffle]] sees four little corn husk dolls, one that looks like each of us. [[Riffle]] takes out their rapier and says “experiment”. They poke the one that looks like [[Furgus]]. [[Furgus]] makes a loud snore from the tent.
[[Riffle]] gently pokes [[WISC]] but [[WISC]] feels nothing. They do the same thing with [[Riffle]]’s with the same lack of sensation. They look around for any footsteps from the dolls or whoever may have deposited them.
There is no indication of that. They start contemplating burning the dolls in the embers. [[WISC]] calls out “whoever left these dolls, just want to let you know that we are about to BURN them because they are CREEPY, so if you don’t want us to destroy your gift, let us know”. [[WISC]] and [[Riffle]] hear a, “hee hee hee!” floating on the wind. [[WISC]] holds his doll over the fire and lights the foot on fire. He begins sweating. Because his arm is held out over the coals. He drops his doll into the fire. It burns, but nothing else happens. [[Riffle]] examines [[Furgus]]’s doll and looks to see if it contains any actual part of [[Furgus]] (hair, whisker, fur, etc.) [[Riffle]] decides to shove their doll into one of the unbreakable vials. In the morning, they update [[Deanira]] and [[Furgus]]. [[Deanira]] decides to leave hers there instead of accepting a potential gift from the fae. We continue on our way to [[Truemetal Halls]] and arrive back at the familiar gates.
