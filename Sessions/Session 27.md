# Session 27 — 9 March 2026

We come upon a field of what seems to be fog. We are able to discover that it is spores from fungus. It will take us a lot of time to circumvent the cloud, so we decide to pull something out of the [[Grey Bag of Tricks]]. [[Deanira]] pulls out a boar and throws it into the cloud of spores. It spawns into existence and takes poison damage. As this extends as far as we can see, we decide to go around it.
In the middle of the night as [[WISC]] is taking watch, he suddenly feels a terrible bite on his back. He turns to see a massive troll with a gigantic snake around its neck that has snuck up on him. We all roll initiative. At the end of the fight, [[Furgus]] wrangles the body of the snake and holds it up for [[Riffle]] to milk it. We go back to sleep and complete a long rest.
In the morning, we hear fiddling. [[Deanira]], being a satyr and Fae, [[Deanira]] REALLY wants to explore it. We follow it to an old hollowed out tree with a fiddle laying inside of it, unmoving, with music coming from it. [[Deanira]] plays her pan flute along to it, and [[WISC]] becomes enchanted by the fiddle. He would have danced incessantly for 10 hours if [[Riffle]] didn’t dispel magic at minute 30. We grab the fiddle and put it into the bag of holding, and [[Deanira]] sustains significant lightning damage in the process.
At the end of the session, we arrive at the location of the ridges and cliffs that the tomb was carved into.
