[[Deanira]] goes into the kitchen and where it was pristine a week ago, it is currently in horrifying condition as [[Daddy Dildo Daggins]] has been so absorbed in his work. We spend some time cleaning the kitchen a bit, finding what’s still edible. [[WISC]] finds some edible mushrooms growing on rotten food and makes an omelette. [[WISC]] rolls a nat 20 on the housework check and gains the chef feat.
After feeding [[Daddy Dildo Daggins]] we leave him to his work and stop by [[The Warhammer]] to tell [[Gunther Rockhammer]] we’re headed out on a quest for the Alchemist, and to request that one of the barmaids check in on [[Daddy Dildo Daggins]] periodically.
The city we are headed to was settled by Elves long ago, named [[Wren Nalore]]. It has some geothermal activity which has created warm water and hot springs. The water is nutritiously rich with very fertile soil. The food and fish there is plentiful, and many valuable Druidic and Alchemical components are grown there. In the swamps surrounding the city lie semi-aquatic basilisks, and the Alchemist says that he will pay extra for any blood or eggs that we can obtain if we so choose. He gives us 10 empty vials in case.
Several days into our journey we approach a curve in the road and hear footsteps running towards us from around the bend. We are in a hardwood forest with the mountains on our right. A 19-20 year old Tieflings runs to us, her face drained of colour, and says, “It’s coming! It’s coming! Help!” She collapses in exhaustion. Approaching us is what seems like a person at first glance, but on closer inspection is a person stitched together, and they look like a corpse. We roll initiative. As we attack the staggering corpse, we see bits of dust, rock, and even some ore knock lose, as though it’s come out of the mines. The creature exudes a poisonous gas that makes us cough and gag when it approaches. We kill it and examine the parts it’s made of. It appears that someone had stitched together limbs from different species in order to specialise the corpse for a specific purpose. One of its arms stands out as interesting, and [[WISC]] feels like it’s familiar, though he can’t say why. [[Furgus]] recognises a sparkling quality to the arm, we saw that same glitter after we fell down that large shoot in the mines. It is mithril ore.
[[Deanira]], [[WISC]], and [[Riffle]] have a flashback to when they first met and that Dwarf fell from the ladder while setting up for the market. He had a very distinct scar on his arm - this belonged to him. We remember that the undertaker came to collect the body and was going to take him to Moroden’s Forge for cremation. We get the impression that the Mithril ore dust is what’s exuding the poison.
