# Session 5 — 18 April 2025

We awaken to the sounds of many Dwarven voices approaching the lake. We’re still in the hallway where we went to sleep, and [[WISC]] wakes up to find a scroll and a pouch of coins next to him. He cracks open the door and while he doesn’t see anyone, he hears angry voices. The one phrase that he can make out is, “find them”. [[WISC]] looks at the scroll and only he sees writing on it. It is a scroll of water breathing. We decide to hide underwater. [[WISC]] can somehow read these runes only he can see, despite being illiterate. He reads aloud the incantation and we head into the dark cool water. We feel a slight current heading down and under the temple. We dive down and follow it. We see blind fish swimming, but no vegetation. As we approach the wall, we see a hole carved into it, just big enough for people. We swim through and it opens into a cavern pool, like an air pocket chamber. As [[Deanira]] surfaces, [[Furgus]] feels the water around him tighten up and grapple him. [[Deanira]] comes back underwater and sees [[Furgus]] struggling and fighting against something invisible. She pierces a spot near [[Furgus]] with a mithril dagger and feels a sort of resistance, like the water is taking the blow. We all attack the entity, [[Deanira]] remembers it to be a water weird. Once we kill it, we continue through the hole on the other side. We surface in the next chamber and see a shore and natural stone steps leading up to a crack in the stone. We shimmy through and find ourselves in a tunnel stretching to our right and left. As we stand there, the water dripping off of us onto the ground flows down the tunnel to our right. We head to the left tunnel that slopes up slightly. We walk for a long time. We eventually settle down after about 8 hours of walking. [[Deanira]] maps out where they’ve been so far, and they are still heading further away from the edge of the map we were given. During the night, we hear subtle sounds of work going on down the tunnel, very far off. The next day, we continue on. Eventually we come to an offshoot. We can either continue down the
main tunnel or explore this offshoot. We continue up the main tunnel for the better part of a day. [[Deanira]] begins to hear more sounds above, though still too distant to make out what they are. We continue on until we can hear more clearly. We hear a curse in Undercommon and a squeal, like someone was hit. We look around for places to hide. We see fissures in the rock and overhangs we could hide in. [[Deanira]] squeezes into a fissure and [[WISC]] climbs onto an overhang. [[Furgus]] tries to climb up onto an overhang but the rock buckles and he falls spread-eagled onto the cave floor and the noise of rock falling around him echoes through the cave. We roll initiative. [[Deanira]] misty steps behind the group and waits to attack from behind. They eventually turn around to go back and see her, and we all attack. One younger attacker runs and gets away. We continue up the tunnel. Not too far up, we see half a dozen people rope-bound in the tunnel. We tell them we’re friends and take off their hoods. We ask them what happened. All of them have been down here a few days. One of the Dwarves ([[Elzock Emberstone]]) says that if we can get them to [[Truemetal Halls]], he can make it worth our while. His family lives in the Golden Circle there (the fancy homes up high in the grand chamber entering [[Truemetal Halls]]). We give them rations and spare arms.
They are farmers, not fighters, but they look more intimidating armed. We walk for the rest of the day, then settle down for the night, planning to finish the journey out tomorrow. The following day we come upon a Y intersection. The one to the right seems more highly trafficked, but to the left we hear the faint sound of birds and a slight breeze of fresh air. We follow this one. We emerge into a large cavern. Looking up, we see the sky, with sunlight streaming down on our faces for the first time in days. Looking around, we see a rope hanging down from the lip of the chamber. It stops 60-70 ft above the ground and the end is frayed. On the ground we see the body of a Halfling and the rest of the rope. We search the body and find a journal on him, written in common, but seemingly in code. The guy was an adventurer like us, trying to find archaeological
locations to unearth wealth. [[Furgus]] has a background in archaeology, and investigates the journal. One word stands out and is written in Dwarvish: [[Axeholm]]. None of us have ever heard the word before. This beautiful area doesn’t offer any new paths, so we turn back to check out the other tunnel. At the end of the tunnel is a fake wall that can be pushed. We push through and emerge into the grass and sunlight.
