# Session 32 — 9 July 2026

[[Klaus]] pulls us aside and tells us that someone new came to the town and was looking for a group with a Bugbear, Herrengon, Kenkku, and a Satyr. He points to a Shadar Kai. He has medium length black hair, and a slight little goatee (think of Russ) and jet black earrings.
[[Deanira]] approaches him and he introduces himself as [[Theo'din Corvis]]. Meanwhile, [[Riffle]] notices a young Halfling woman sees [[WISC]] and then ducks away. [[Riffle]] wanders off to find out where she went. She follows a trail of mud through and up piles of firewood and makes her way onto the roof. She looks frightened at [[Riffle]]. “You’re with him, aren’t you? The bugbear with the golden eyes.” A couple of weeks ago, in Copperhead Hollow (south of [[Eisenberg]]), she was told by a golden eyed bugbear that her family needed to pay tax protection to him. When they didn’t, her brother, father, and grandfather were strung up. [[Riffle]] assures the woman [[Jane Pedimore]] that this is not the same bugbear, despite the resemblance. They agree to get drinks later to discuss [[Eisenberg]]. [[Riffle]] leaves and returns to their meandering. They look for a tent or storeroom with medical supplies, in particular, a healer. They find [[Annika]] and offer to help make a medical stockpile for them. Elm, Ash, Beech (brothers), Birch, Maple, Cedar (sisters) are the Forester Siblings. They are working with [[Theo'din Corvis]]’din to prep the firewood, and we notice that [[Theo'din Corvis]]’din doesn’t have much strength. Maple has a thing for [[WISC]], and the Siblings excitedly welcome us over. [[Uthal]] is quite popular, everyone knows and likes him.
Our group walks up to [[Theo'din Corvis]]’din without [[Riffle]]. Immediately [[Deanira]] says “the kenkku died.” [[Theo'din Corvis]]’din falls for it. He has a raven named Nilas. He says that he heard we knew something about Necromantic activity in the area. We tell him about Kristoff. [[Theo'din Corvis]]’din heard that someone had a prophecy of the ancient city of [[Axeholm]] and the undead horrors within. As a result, there is an effort to track Necromancy up north, which is what sent him here. [[Theo'din Corvis]]’din reveals that his patron is the Raven [[Queen]], who is very anti-necromancy.
At dinner time, we all reconvene. We meet in one of the longhouses for a bit of privacy. Inside, we see pillars with names carved into them, the mirror of the ones at the gate. These list all the deaths of [[Venberg]]. We sit around and [[Riffle]] saunters up. “[[Riffle]], you respawned!” says [[Deanira]]. We introduce [[Theo'din Corvis]] and [[Riffle]]. [[Riffle]] just repeats everything [[Theo'din Corvis]] says. He does not like [[Theo'din Corvis]], very suspicious.
We learn that when [[Theo'din Corvis]]’s mother was pregnant with him and his twin, she had a near death experience. She made a deal with the Raven [[Queen]] to save her life. For the price, on their first birthday a higher up serving the Raven [[Queen]] came to collect: the mom had to give up one of the twins. That twin that was taken was [[Theo'din Corvis]]’din.
For the festival, each year the people make a large effigy of Melora, the goddess of nature and the harvest. They set it alight and celebrate among the community in honour of the harvest.
In the longhouse, [[Theo'din Corvis]] pulls out a dire [[Wolf]] from the gray bag of tricks, and everyone in the longhouse freaks out. A bunch of people including [[Oma]] run in to see what’s going on, “WISCCHENZO WHAT HAS [[Furgus]] GOTTEN YOU INTO THIS TIME?” A family debate
ensues. [[Theo'din Corvis]] casts Zone of Truth and asks if anyone knows anything about necromancers in the area. He doesn’t get much participation.
[[Furgus]] blurts out, “[[Oma]], why do you hate me?!” She responds, “I SWEAR THIS WITH EVERY BONE IN MY BODY, I CAN’T
REMEMBER! But I’m sure it was for a good reason”. [[Deanira]], wanting to mess with [[WISC]], draws a photo of [[WISC]] and her together and gives it to [[Oma]]. “So you can remember what we look like”. [[Oma]] adores it.
Meanwhile, [[Riffle]] has snuck off to meet with [[Jane Pedimore]]. They crack open a bottle of white lightning, which is so strong that it can burn the warts off a mule. They discuss the golden eyed bugbear, but [[Riffle]] doesn’t learn anything new or significant.
