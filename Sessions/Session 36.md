# Session 36 — 31 August 2026

We all buy [[Sending Stones]] and then follow [[Riffle]] to the Library.
The Mage’s District surrounds the Library and the Academy. We are introduced to [[Ester]], the Chief Archivist, who is sitting at a check out desk. After exchanging (somewhat) pleasantries we head to see [[Riffle]]’s pseudo-father, [[Talir Rengavi]], Director of the Library. As we walk, [[Riffle]] chatters and gives us a tour of the regions we are passing. We have never heard them talk so much. We arrive at [[Talir Rengavi]]’s office and enter. It’s a nice office filled with clutter of maps, texts, papers, scrolls, a random pastry and the like. We reintroduce ourselves and he welcomes us. [[Riffle]] attempts to look through the top few papers of his inbox. [[Talir Rengavi]] stops him, apparently this is a normal thing. Due to the Festival, the city is inundated with people and we all will be staying at different locations:
[[Deanira]]: [[The Two Tits]] (Western side of market square)
[[Furgus]] and [[Philip]]: [[Cock and Pig]] ([[Merchant District]] close to docks) [[Theo'din Corvis]] and [[WISC]]: [[The Three Candles]] (located in the [[Mine District]])
We make our way to the market square, and to [[Deanira]]’s great delight, they are selling fish sticks! She buys two battered fish on skewers and then gets settled in her Inn on the Western side. We continue to walk around and head to the [[Mine District]]. This region is filled with buildings that are so close together and each story is built progressively off centre, to the point that it gives the feeling of being in a tunnel or underground. The Inn is very dimly lit, lit only by three candles, which is where it gets its name. [[WISC]] and [[Theo'din Corvis]] drop off their things and we continue on. Making our way to the [[Merchant District]], we see a sort of Boardwalk leading up to the docks. Merchant vessels and fishing ships fill the sea. Inside the [[Cock and Pig]], it is FULL of people. It is crowded with sailors and the bar is completely filled. [[Furgus]] and [[Philip]] check in with the Innkeeper. A group of sailors surrounds a thirteen year old and calls out, “Hey everyone! The young pup just got
his anchor!” The crowd cheers and the young teen reveals an anchor tattoo on his upper arm. Someone erupts, “to the Pelican!” Everyone cheers and toasts. The bartender/Innkeeper is [[Evelyn Keep]]. She says that the sailors have been stuck in port due to some inability to unload their ship. A sailor named [[Alex]] explains that they just got back from [[Savehaven]] and the owner of the ship died so they can’t unload the ship into his warehouse until everything is sorted out. The owner’s name is (the late) Lord Richard Owens. [[Riffle]] has heard of him, a wealthy merchant family. Nothing too significant. [[Riffle]] heard that his son [[Peter Owens]] was studying at the School of Conjuration at the Academy. They’ve been stuck here for a week.
Looking around, we see a very large Goaliath sailor covered in tattoos. In particular he is one of the few sailors who has a dragon skull tattoo. He also has a tattoo on the other arm of a [[Skeletal Hand]]. [[Deanira]] finds an older man with a dragon skull tattoo and asks him of its meaning. She gets him talking about his sea stories. It signifies his fight in the war between the Ambercrown kingdom and the [[Dragon League]], located across the sea on a continent to the East. The [[Dragon League]] were worshipers of Tiamat and through her blessing they were able to control dragons and use them (think dragon riders from Eragon). 100 years or so ago, there was a battle amongst the deities themselves, and Tiamat was put down. Their ability to control dragons vanished. Since then, they had been trying to regain power in various ways, including going to war with the Ambercrown Kingdom. Around 15-20 years ago the war was fought, shortly after King Xavier had ascended to the throne. The [[Dragon League]] lost, it lasted only a few months.
The three swallows on his chest signify how far he has sailed - each swallow is 5,000 nautical miles. He tells us that the Anchor tattoo means that they had been to [[Safehaven]] or [[Evermeet]]. [[Deanira]] continues talking to the sailor for another hour, hearing his stories. Meanwhile, [[WISC]] and [[Furgus]] head to one of the tables where there’s some gambling going on. [[Furgus]] convinces [[WISC]] to take on the Goliath in an arm wrestling match. The Goliath seems to be the only one of the sailors who
Has a [[Skeletal Hand]] tattoo. [[WISC]] wins the arm wrestling match and chats with the Goliath [[Cam Jergins]]. He learns that he saw the [[Skeletal Hand]] tattoo on a [[Blond-Haired Youth|tiny blonde young “kid”]] and thought it looked cool, so he got one. That’s all.
[[Riffle]] reminds us that we have dinner with [[Bianca Onyxcrest]] in her dining room at 6:30, and then makes their way to their favourite wine shop The Vine’s Bounty. To their shock, it is boarded up and closed. There are slight signs of broken glass. They head to another wine shop [[Decanter 3 Table]] and buy a medium grade bottle of wine. There is a sort of bouncer or guard hanging out at the front with a sword at his side. [[Riffle]] nods to him and leaves with their purchase. They go to [[Bianca Onyxcrest]]’s office with the wine. The office itself is lovely and bigger than [[Talir Rengavi]]’s. It’s filled with shelves of books and notably a jewelled silver goblet on her desk.
[[Bianca Onyxcrest]] says hello and [[Riffle]] pours the mid-range wine for both of them. They take a seat and [[Riffle]] asks how things have been. They discuss the gauntlet, Gavin Gosling’s passing and [[Bianca Onyxcrest]]’s transition to Headmistress (a month or so ago when [[Hana Goldfirm]] and [[Talir Rengavi]] visited), [[Talir Rengavi]] has given the Academy access to the stone from the belly of the ram.
[[Riffle]] shares that we went to [[Venberg]] and then we got attacked by an encampment of Stone Giant Werebears. [[Riffle]] ASKS [[Bianca Onyxcrest]] FOR HELP (and ONLY mentions [[WISC]] and [[Furgus]], not [[Philip]]). She is more than happy to help and sends a message to the Head Cleric of Mistra to prepare the spells to reverse the condition tomorrow. She names no price, it is complementary. [[Riffle]] then MAKES SMALL TALK. [[Bianca Onyxcrest]] mentions that [[Talir Rengavi]] said that [[Riffle]] is looking for [[Axeholm]] and she asks how it’s going. [[Riffle]] honestly shares that there isn’t much of any progress. ([[Bianca Onyxcrest]] is not acting suspicious, at least to my knowledge). [[Riffle]] asks [[Bianca Onyxcrest]] not to tell [[WISC]] or [[Furgus]] that they came to her for help. They don’t want them to know just how important they are to them. ([[Deanira]] is also subsequently unaware)
We meet for dinner and the hall is lovely. [[Riffle]] is stuttering around [[Hana Goldfirm]] like crazy, obviously smitten.
[[Bianca Onyxcrest]] takes time to explain the Gauntlet to us. It will begin in a week and a half.
Rules of the Gauntlet:
There will be 5 “bouts” where we fight creatures (not other teams. Each team will be competing independently). A prize will be awarded at the end of each. There will be about 10-15 minutes between each bout to reset the arenas. We can use one short rest during the 5 bouts (they will be using the Cat Nap spell). Clerics will be on hand to revive any who fall, though if one of these Clerics needs to do so, the party member will be out for the rest of the bouts. If a fellow party member revives them, the party member may continue to fight.
In a few weeks there will be a Gala to show off the stone from the belly of the ram (which is drawing in many alumni donations and great scholarly interest), and also to kick off the Gauntlet celebration. We have been asked to represent both the Academy and the Library ([[Riffle]] doesn’t like this and just breaths deeply). We toast to that.
After dinner, [[Riffle]] shows us their room. Many years ago the bell in the tower broke and [[Riffle]] took the opportunity to immediately move into the bell tower. We walk up a long spiral staircase and emerge into a space with a large bell in the centre. There is a pile of blankets and pillows, a desk purely to hold items, several stacks of books, etc. [[Riffle]] doesn’t have a lot of stuff, it is very cozy. There is no furniture for guests, so [[Deanira]] flops onto [[Riffle]]’s bed pile. Afterwards, we return to our respective lodgings and [[Hana Goldfirm]] and [[Riffle]] go to share a drink at her apartment. On their way, they sea a haggard looking man with his hat pulled down. He asks, “are you [[Hana Goldfirm]]? Oh thank god, they told me I might find you here,” He goes to pull out a message/package and spots [[Riffle]] and becomes distraught. This is the messenger that [[Riffle]] sent from [[Truemetal Halls]] to give [[Hana Goldfirm]] a copy of the the pages that they transcribed from the journal. The poor guy lost his horse to a troll, and
has been through hell to get here. This was [[Riffle]]’s insurance policy and it has paid off. The original journal was stolen when [[Daddy Dildo Daggins]] was murdered. Then [[Riffle]]’s original copying got burned along with the rest of their belongings in the fight with the flame skull. But this version, sent to [[Hana Goldfirm]] as a “just in case” is the only surviving copy we can locate.
At [[Hana Goldfirm]]’s apartment, [[Riffle]] fills [[Hana Goldfirm]] in on all the details of the story and the documents relating to [[Axeholm]]. They tell them all about the adventures we’ve been up to and how it could all possibly connect. [[Riffle]] is worried that whatever is going on is possibly coming from [[Amberhold]], perhaps even the Academy or the Library itself. [[Hana Goldfirm]] had told [[Riffle]] that her office was broken into, and some things were stolen, but those items pale in comparison to what we have found. There have been no further developments with the investigation of the break in, and nothing else weird has been happening. [[Riffle]] shares that they had to TALK with [[Bianca Onyxcrest]]. “I noticed that she lacked any stab wounds, so I commend your self restraint” [[Hana Goldfirm]] says.
In the middle of the night, [[Riffle]] SCREECHES into the [[Sending Stones]] “TESTING! TESTING!” [[Riffle]] sends us a message telling us that we need to go see the Head Cleric of Mistra tomorrow (no explanation).
