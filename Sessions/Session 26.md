# Session 26 — Missed Session

Day 1:
On the way to the tomb, [[WISC]] stops to take a piss in the forest. While pissing, [[WISC]] looks up and sees a skeleton entangled in the bush. There is movement within the skeleton. [[WISC]] slowly backs up to the rest of the party, expecting the worst. Suddenly, a chipmunk bursts out of the brush and runs off into the woods.
[[Riffle]] goes to inspect the skeleton. The skeleton appears to have belonged to an elderly individual. [[Furgus]] and [[WISC]] recall that things like this have happened in their ‘berg before, called an “Elder’s Gift”, when an elderly person willingly leaves an encampment to go die in the woods in order to lessen the burden on the ‘berg during harsh winters. After failing to find any identifying items on the body, [[WISC]] asks [[Riffle]] to perform funeral rites on the body.
The group continues to travel. Suddenly, we hear commotion in the woods that sounds like people talking loudly and hurriedly. [[Furgus]] goes to investigate, attempting to stealth into the woods. [[Furgus]] sees three individuals: a dwarf lying on the ground, clutching his stomach, and two others–a human and a goliath–trying to stop the bleeding.
There is a large dead boar nearby. [[Furgus]] accidentally steps on a twig and alerts the hunting party to his presence. He successfully convinces them that he is not trying to hurt them and offers the injured man a few good berries to help stabilize him. The hunting party gives him a slice of boar tenderloin as thanks. The party puts the boar tenderloin into the Yeti cooler.
Day 2:
The party travels further out into some valleys. A fog starts rolling in and the horses start to spook, but the party is able to calm them down. Two towering hill giants suddenly stand up from the ground and engage the party in combat.
[[Furgus]] speaks Giant. He tried to tell the giants that the party comes in peace, but it fails. During the battle, one of the giants cried out, “THIS WAS SUPPOSED TO BE OUR HONEYMOON”. [[Furgus]] is the only one who speaks Giant so only he understood it. He convinces the party that the giants are yelling about killing them.
The party defeats the giants. The giants had 17 gold, ten silver, a picnic setup (covered in lube), and a human-sized cooking pot and scythe. The party takes the money and bounces.
Day 3:
A primeval owlbear attacks the party and instantly downs [[Furgus]]. The party barely manages to defeat the creature. [[WISC]] and [[Riffle]] harvest the owlbear pelt and [[Furgus]] harvests 15 owlbear claws. [[WISC]] cooks Bolstering Treats for the party. [[Riffle]] uses Sending to speak with [[Hana Goldfirm]] about the murder. First message: Let her know about Dildo being murdered, possibly framed by someone with access to library robes, and asked for updates. [[Riffle]] got back the following:
No updates
The Director of Mystra’s Academy is dead
[[Bianca Onyxcrest]] might be the new Director/Head of Academy [[Hana Goldfirm]] reminded [[Riffle]] that they can make shivs
All red library robes were discontinued/destroyed 20 years ago. The party has four more days on the road.
