# Session 3 — 29 February 2025

The slam of the doors echoes through the vast chambers of the cave. Somewhere in the distance we hear damp drips bathed in the silence. [[Deanira]] lights the lantern and Wisk leads the way. We make our way down the first hall and at the end we notice something shifting on the far wall. It appears to be moving shadows of two Dwarves. [[Deanira]] calls out a Dwarvish greeting tentatively. They attack. Once we dispose of the shadows, we continue on. After another few hours, we go down an offshoot and enter a large rounded open area. In the middle, we find a pile of what seems like gelatin. Upon inspection, [[Deanira]] and [[Furgus]] get the impression that they are like a mix of amphibian eggs and reptile eggs, encased in jelly to preserve their moisture. As [[Deanira]] looks around the room,
she sees 5 different holes that could hold a medium creature each. We feel a rumble. 2 gricks pop out of the burrows and instantly down [[WISC]]. One of the the gricks brings [[Deanira]] down to her last hit point with one critical strike. She falls to her knees. As it moves to slither back into its burrow, she whips out her hand and grabs it by the neck and chokes it. [[Furgus]] shoots the last one down the throat. We stomp the remaining eggs. We go back to a more familiar part of the mine and make camp. When we turn down the lamps, we are immersed in the greatest darkness we’ve ever experienced. While keeping watch, [[WISC]] hears the faintest sound of picks hitting ore, though he can’t be sure. In the morning, we make our way further into the cave. Eventually a large chasm blocks our path through the tunnel. [[WISC]] drops a torch down into it to judge the depth, but the flame sputters out before he even lets go of it. He remembers the warning we received before the doors closed, that if our flames go out, we’re entering an area devoid of oxygen. We look down into it with our lanterns and barely make out two Halfling bodies at the bottom.
They are both wearing goggles, and that’s when we remember that Halflings don’t have darkvision. We lower [[WISC]] down the 15 ft necessary and retrieve the goggles. We know the mineshaft was closed 20 years ago, but the bodies do not show significant signs of decomposition. We attribute this to the lack of oxygen down in the chasm. One of the Halfling miners has a tattoo that reads “I like to get dirty in dark holes”. On the miners we find lamp oil and a bullseye lantern, a hooded lantern, and two pairs of goggles of night. We make our way on for a little while and find a Barracks.
We camp there for the night, and [[Deanira]] spends the evening deconstructing the goggles of night and inserting them into her ritual deer skull mask. Several days later as we’re exploring a chamber, a big gelatinous cube drops from the ceiling and barely misses us. We fight it to clear the mine. We make our way to the area where the massive cave-in happened. Eerily, we can tell that there are mining sounds again…on the other side. According to the map, there shouldn’t be any space beyond this. [[WISC]] and
[[Furgus]] notice a hole that has been tunnelled through the cave-in. [[WISC]] peaks in and sees that it continues on for a long time. This tunnel is messily carved, nothing close to the level of the Dwarven. This was someone else. Someone else who has moved in to resume mining operations down here. We sneak into the tunnel and make our way quietly towards the noise. Eventually it opens up into a larger chamber, the ceiling reinforced with timber. We see eight [[Tiny]] creatures working away with pick axes and wheelbarrows. They empty the wheelbarrows down the mining shaft. We walk into the room and fan out. We talk to them and learn that they’re the Knockers. They are mining the mithril to make weapons so they can expand and take over all of the mine on behalf of The Great One.
The priests of the Great One are apparently at the bottom of the chute. They tell us to go down to the chute to meet the Great One. We jump down the chute.
