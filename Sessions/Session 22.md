# Session 22 — 28 Jan 2026

It’s the following morning. [[Furgus]] has stayed up all night to scout out and watch the Basilisks. Now that it’s daytime they come out and lay on rocks to soak up the sun. [[Riffle]] casts invisibility on [[Furgus]] who then sneaks over to the nest. He’s able to collect 3 basilisk egg undetected. He makes his way back to land and we decide to also harvest some basilisk blood. We will be doing this by conjuring a 15 lb country ham, dividing it into 3, and attaching it to strings to lure the basilisks to a choke point where 3 hunting traps are hidden in the grass. We are slipping the [[Immovable Rod]] through the end of the hunting trap chains and sticking it vertically so the basilisks are stuck in space despite their strength.
It works. One gets 3 of its 6 feet trapped in the hunting traps. She throws her mithril dagger and together we kill the basilisk. [[Deanira]] collects 3 vials of basilisk blood to sell along with the 3 eggs. We head back to the town where we left our horse Dante and our donkey. The alchemist suppliers are gathering the goods for us to take back to [[Truemetal Halls]]. A day away from the town, we see this sign. We think it may be due to attempting to traffic a baby, harassing an old woman and trying to steal her stuff, and then disappearing. [[Riffle]] is considering setting fire to the town and then blaming it on the raiding party from the other town whose banners we stole from the ravaged goblin camp. [[Riffle]] wants to sit on one of the eggs to see if it hatches.
