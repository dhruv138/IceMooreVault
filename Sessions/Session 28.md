# Session 28 — 8 April 2026

[[Riffle]] casts sending to send a message to that bugbear who shares [[WISC]]’s eyes. We’re calling him [[WISC]]’s secret cousin brother lover uncle father. They ask for his name and if he is from [[Venburg]], but he refuses.
We have arrived at the tomb and a giant circular stone closes the entrance. It is overgrown with no signs of disturbance or having been entered in a very, very long time. As [[WISC]] is exploring, he almost steps in a pile of horseshit. There are hoof tracks which appear to be out of and leading away from the tomb down the mountain, a though they approached the entrance and then went back down the way they came.
[[Riffle]] and [[Furgus]] are inspecting the large stone concealing the entrance when [[Riffle]] sees [[Furgus]]’s ears blowing in a breeze that is not disturbing the land. They put their hand on the rock and it goes through. It is an illusion. Immediately, an axe comes down upon [[Riffle]]. [[Furgus]] goes inside quickly and sees 6 Minotaur skeletons, animated. We recall that they were placed here within to protect the adventurer in death. Each of the minotaurs has shackles on their wrists glowing with a different coloured rune on each. These correspond to glowing runes on the walls. [[Riffle]] turns undead and they run away but [[Furgus]] is blocking the path, so they do a panic dance rave hopping around [[Furgus]]. [[Riffle]] is looking at the glyphs and doesn’t recognise them but can tell they are old magic. We destroy the runes and as each one stops glowing the corresponding minotaur falls apart into a pile of bones. We collect all of the manacles and put them into our bag of holding. We go through the door and enter an empty chamber with a flagstone floor, stalactites, magical torches lining the walls, and a door at the end.
Using the [[Grey Bag of Tricks]] [[Deanira]] summons a giant badger and we send it through the chamber to set off any booby traps. As it steps on a pressure plate, a metal spike comes down from the ceiling and strikes the giant badger, impaling it. [[Deanira]] then pulls the giant elk. The area is 10’x9’, and the giant elk is 15’x15’. It’s stuck again in this confined space. We once again slowly attack it until it dies and vanishes.
[[Deanira]] pulls a third and final animal from the bag. It’s the fucking giant elk again. We have to slowly kill it again. We hear voices from the other side of the door saying “what are you guys doing out there?!” We ask if we can come in and they say “come on through!” [[Deanira]] enters first and immediately gets hit with a magic missile. On the other side are 4 humanoids (a mix of humans and halflings) who attack us. We roll initiative. As [[Deanira]] pummels the final enemy, [[Riffle]] sees the man has a tattoo of a skeleton hand on his hand. [[Riffle]] has heard of a secret society of necromancers at the Library called the [[Skeletal Hand]] who pushed the boundaries of necromancy and got banned. This was before [[Riffle]]’s time, before even [[Talir Rengavi]]’s time. All four of these people have the tattoo. We see two cells inside the room and [[Riffle]] looks inside warily. Two Dwarves lie in one, wearing lots of cloth and goggles lying nearby. There is a strong sulfuric smell coming from this cell in particular. They are alive but unconscious, near death. In the other cell are three people including a bugbear and a goblin. [[WISC]] recognises the goblin mercenary as someone he gambled with in [[Venburg]] ([[Zeke]]). [[Ambria]] is a Dragonborn that would occasionally give [[Furgus]] haircuts. [[Furgus]] recognizes the bugbear (named [[Wolf]]) as a long hunter from [[Venburg]].
