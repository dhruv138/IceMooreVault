# Session 2

**Session:** 2 · Not dated in the source notes · **Previous:** [[Session 01]] · **Next:** [[Session 03]]
%% card:end %%

> [!summary] TL;DR
> The party arrives at [[Truemetal Halls]] to sell their sheep, meets [[The Bastard's Band]], finds [[Hambrick Onyxcrest]] away in the Feywild, and takes [[Gunther Rockhammer]]'s job clearing and mapping a mine beneath the city.

Luther Trollstumper is directing people who are cued up at the gates of [[Truemetal Halls]], which lies within the mountain itself. We have come to sell our remaining sheep after the loss of our ram. A group of people on giant goats approach and announce themselves as [[The Bastard's Band|The Bastard’s Band]]. We sell our sheep and make our way to the city. We follow a winding path up the side of the mountain until we reach large grand double doors with adamantine hinges. Flanking the doors are statues of the city founders, Astrid and Lothar Darkhammer. Two sentries with mithril armour and mithril weapons stand guard. Past the doors, we pass through a portcullis into a grand chamber called the [[Hall of the Four Clans]]. On the floor is a mosaic of the Great Cosmic Wheel, and in each corner we see a statue of a dwarf, each representing one of the four major clans of the Icemoor mountains. They include the [[Iron Dwarves]], the [[Ice Dwarves]], the [[Fire Dwarves]], and the [[Truemetal Dwarves]]. We look up and hanging from the ceiling is a mithril statue of Moradin, the god of the Dwarves, watching from above. We enter a wealthy area where shops and boutiques reside and the upper class live. We make our way to an alchemist to ask about the black stone. The alchemist hasn’t heard of or seen anything like this before, and doesn’t know of anyone who would. He mentions the [[The Warhammer|Warhammer Inn]] and the owner [[Gunther Rockhammer]]. He said that there we can ask for more niche adventurous jobs.

We decide to find a magic item shop to ask about the black stone. We find a shop run by a wizard named [[Hambrick Onyxcrest]]. We walk into the shop and learn that he’s out of town for several weeks attending a luncheon in the Feywild. After leaving our names, we go to the Warhammer Inn to look for work and a place to stay. We each book a room and get some lunch. We’re directed to Gunther, the owner of the Inn, for work. We take a job clearing out and mapping a mine beneath the city. We head over to a table where the Bastard’s Band is seated. They introduce themselves as [[The Bastard|Bastard]], [[The Poet]], [[Glasses]], and [[Tiny]]. They recommend [[Ree Tealeaf]]’s shop [[Junk in the Trunk]] for asking around about the mysterious stone. He’s never seen anything like it. We buy a hooded lantern to light our way through the dungeon. We make our way from the [[Great Chamber]] through tunnels that lead to different levels and areas like The Paddock, The Farm, and The Singing Hall level where extensive blacksmith shops and the Foundry lie. We approach the doors to the mines and are told a secret knock to be let out. We enter the caves and the heavy doors shut behind us.
