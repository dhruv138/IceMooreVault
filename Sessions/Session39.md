# Session 39
**Date:** 14 October 2026

## Session Notes

### The Mausoleum
With Riffle and Furgus being invisible, Deanira and the rest of the party sneak to the door to see if it is locked behind Owen's. She opens it easily and carefully and sees that it is lit within. Within the stone mausoleum are 4 sarcophagi.

Riffle somehow sneaks in front of Deanira without being seen, and as they both enter they feel a magical hook pull them behind the navel and vanish into darkness. Furgus, WISC, and Theo see them disappear. Furgus feels the same tug but doesn't move, as if something is preventing him from being transported.

Deanira and Riffle take a breath in and choke on the poison gas that begins to fill the dark space. They do see the roughness of stone around them but can't make out anything else. WISC lifts off the lid of the first sarcophagus and Furgus readies an attack. Riffle yells and pounds on the stone; we are stuck in the sarcophagi. Deanira screams, rolls onto her back, and begins kicking the lid with her hooves. WISC rescues Furgus and Deanira.

### The Hidden Crypt
WISC removes the lid of another sarcophagus and finds that it's wood disguised as stone. Inside the tomb is a ladder leading down. Furgus goes down first and the room is similarly lit. There are 6 stately coffins and a crest painted on the wall. The crest has a ship with a mermaid holding an orb in her outstretched hand as the figurehead.

The coffins are labeled:
- **Harold Owens** & **Margaret Owens**
- **Nicholas Brightvine** & **Elizabeth Brightvine**
- **Lord Richard Owens** & **Lady Gezibel Owens** (Peter's parents)

Furgus notices that there is no smell of putrefaction and it's strange... We open Richard and Gezibel's coffins and they are both empty. Furgus walks over to the crest on the wall. He touches the orb and vanishes.

### The Warehouse Labyrinth
We follow suit and find ourselves in another dark room, this time with a group of crates stacked high. We initially wonder if we're on a ship, but the ground is still and made of stone, and the ceiling is the underside of a wooden floor. The crates are filled with various cloths and we find a note of delivery to the warehouse of **Lord Richard Owens**.

Riffle sneaks to the top of the crates and realizes that the crates are laid out intentionally like a maze. We make our way through the labyrinth. 5 glowing spheres drop invisibility and shoot at Deanira, Theo, and WISC and then float back into the boxes. We get through the labyrinth, dealing with the orbs several more times.

### The Laboratory
We enter a new room with hanging lights, cages built into the walls, and two objects that seem like tables covered with sheets. If we didn't know better, they'd look like operating tables with sheets drawn over bodies. Two of the four cages have a slumped-over body in them, possibly still alive... they are breathing at least. Their eyes are open and vacant and they have soiled themselves.

Riffle immediately beelines for the desk across the room and finds it full of scrap paper with arcane equations. Anatomy books and physiology papers about various animals fill the drawers.
- A paper is blacked out with only the words **"brass shield and the sapphire of the plains"** remaining.
- **"Need female phase"** underlined 3 times.
- Several names are crossed out before **"Deanira? Coda's Landing"** is written.

Deanira heads over to one of the tables, lifts the corner, and sees part of an incredibly muscular arm with large nails. She feels a tug behind her navel but resists it this time.

### Confrontation with Peter
Peter emerges and calls on his mother and father to take care of us. Furgus charms Peter into stupour and Deanira uses the immovable rod to impale Richard and fix him to the spot. We finish off the greater zombies and tie up Peter. We find a spell book on Peter and it includes **polymorph** but not **disguise self** or **alter self**.

### Aftermath
We step outside and find ourselves at a riverfront on the docks. Interestingly, the door to the warehouse appears as a wall from the outside. We aren't completely sure we're in Amberhold. Deanira looks around behind the warehouse and sees the lights of a city, getting the feeling that they are still in the Amberhold.

Riffle goes to check on the people in the cages and gets the sense that this is not medical or necromantic but supernatural. Theo offers to send his raven to deliver a letter from Riffle to Talir. For the moment, Peter is tied and restrained, gagged with WISC's super smelly socks.
