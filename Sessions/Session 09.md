# Session 9 — 17 July 2025 Anita: Riﬄe

Lyra: [[Deanira]] Taylor: [[WISC]] Dhruv: [[Furgus]]
We decide to spend the day exploring the city, as the monthly market isnt for two days. As we’re walking through, Dwarves are hanging banners, one of which is on a ladder. He falls and cracks his head open. As we run over, a luxuriously robed kenku runs over as well. They are exquisitely decorated in golden rings and other jewellery. [[Deanira]] and the kenku examine the man, he is not breathing and portions of brain have come out, very clearly dead.
The foreman tells us his name was Daggen. The undertaker arrives and takes care of the body. The kenku turns and waddles over to [[Junk in the Trunk]]. We follow them to try to recruit them to our party. They are about to buy two books when [[WISC]] walks up and puts his paw on the books and offers to pay for them. We begin to talk. They are looking for historical books and secrets. They also like things that are shiny and things that are soft. They are wearing a clerical pendant of a lit candle dripping onto a stylized eye. They tell us that their deity is Deneir. We offer them to read the journal that we found on the dead Halfling suspended from a rope in the cave. They’re able to pick out of the cryptics “[[Axeholm]]”. I metagame and randomly say the name “[[Daddy Dildo Daggins]]”. The shopkeep replies, “oh you know [[Daddy Dildo Daggins]]?” He tells us that [[Daddy Dildo Daggins]] stayed a dragon and then became incredibly wealthy. He then became a collector. The last time the shopkeep saw him he gave him a package that he ordered. It was a little while ago, and he wouldn’t disclose what it was. The kenku vanishes and pickpockets the journal, unbeknownst to us. We go on a hunt for fish sticks (it’s unsuccessful). Riﬄe goes to a coffee shop and copies several pages of the journal (the page before, the [[Axeholm]] page, and the following page.)
The rest of us finish shopping and head back to [[The Warhammer]] for the evening. Meanwhile, Riﬄe asks the Barista if they know where Mr. [[Daddy Dildo Daggins]] lives. She says he lives on this (upper middle class) ring of the city, but she’s not sure quite where. He used to adventure with others, so they may know.
Back at [[The Warhammer]], the three of us are chatting about a job that the Alchemist offered to us via [[Furgus]]. He is offering an advance of healing potions, and 100 gold each upon return of ingredients. We are to pick up ingredients and return them to the apothecary.
Apparently the road has bandits. Soon after, Riﬄe comes up to us and hands us back the journal, implying that we dropped it, but we are wise to it. We pretend it was a test. They introduce themself, their name is the sound of rifling paper from books in a library. We translate this to Riﬄe. We discuss [[Axeholm]] and Riﬄe suggests we sneak into [[Daddy Dildo Daggins]]’s home and rifle through his things to find a lead to [[Axeholm]]. They know that it’s an ancient Dwarven city, very, very deep underground, though they’re not sure where. [[WISC]] asks the waitress where he lives and she gives us directions. “It’s an oddball house, but you can’t miss it.” We make our way towards the house and come upon a hobbit hole into the outer wall, complete with a garden of grass and flowers and a Pickett fence. [[WISC]] and [[Deanira]] go up to knock on the door to talk to [[Daddy Dildo Daggins]], while Riﬄe and [[Furgus]] will sneak into the house and look around. We mention our interest in his landscaping but he sees right through it. We sigh and mention [[Junk in the Trunk]] and he mentions that he just received a package from them. He says it’s for his “personal collection”. He invites us in to see. We enter a lavishly furnished study filled with dildos. He is convinced they are all male fertility statues from various cultures. [[Furgus]] and Riﬄe “go to the bathroom” while we remain engaged in conversation with [[Daddy Dildo Daggins]]. [[Deanira]] expresses interest in cartography and exploring ancient Dwarven cities. He said that the crowing jewel of his collection would be the solid gold jewel encrusted statuette of [[Axeholm]]. It’s NW of [[Truemetal Halls]], ruled by the Axeforge family.
The town grew very wealthy because of the gold and jewels mined from there. [[Queen]] Sylvie commissioned this golden jewel-ribbed dildo to be created. One day a supernatural greed set upon the city. One day the royal family discovered a large portion of the treasure was missing, along with a number of the guard and royal [[Court]]. They sealed off the city until the culprit was found. At that time, a legendary blizzard came upon the city and buried the entrance. We show him the journal and he says that he wants to work on it for a few weeks to crack the cipher. [[Deanira]] offers to seek out [[Axeholm]] to find the golden dildo for him. He said that he would provide any research and leads he’s collected over the years. He would pay very handsomely. [[Deanira]] asks if Dwarven worship of the god of greed Abathor could have contributed, he says it’s possible. We leave the journal with him for the next few weeks and [[Deanira]] takes the silver dildo as collateral. After we leave, [[Deanira]] hears a voice in her head, one she hasn’t heard since her first visit to the city. “This is Kilston Whistlebelt, assistant to [[Hambrick Onyxcrest]], my Master (the wizard) is home and is willing to meet with you tomorrow to look at the mysterious magical object you inquired about.” We agree to meet tomorrow about the stone.
