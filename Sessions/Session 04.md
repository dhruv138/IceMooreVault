# Session 4 — 9 March 2025

At the base is a pile of ore. 3 paths lead from the cavern, one forward, one left, one right. The rock around us is covered in slightly bioluminescent lichen, providing dim light. We hear the sounds of wheels and voices approaching. The three of us dash partway into a dark tunnel to hide. Four Dwarvish looking individuals clad heavily in armour emerge with a Bugbear pulling the cart. [[Furgus]] recognises them as Duergar. They are permanently underground dwelling, and were not represented by the clan statues in the city, as they are not considered true Dwarves. There has been conflict between the Dwarves and Duergar for millennia. They pull up to the pile of ore and the Bugbear begins loading it into the cart. When they enter the tunnel we’re hiding in, we attack them. The Bugbear dies at the hands of one of the Duergar. On them we find that each has a lead pendant of a dagger painted to look like gold. Perhaps a clan or holy symbol. We decide to sneak down the tunnel they came from to see what their operation is. After 1-2 miles of tunnel, it opens into
a larger cavern space. We see about 20 Duergar and an array of other humanoids that are their enslaved. It appears to be some sort of compound where they are smelting the ore. We decide to go back and check out the other tunnels to get more of an idea of what’s around here and how it’s all connected. We hear footsteps behind us and fight another group of 4 when we get back to the first chamber. It’s very close, but we win the battle. We go to rest in the less travelled tunnel and rest while [[Furgus]] scouts ahead. He comes across an underground lake with a gravel beach on the far side with a rowboat and a stone door. He comes back and tells [[WISC]] and [[Deanira]] about the lake and the stone door. After a little rest, we make our way to the lake to check it out. We get to the lake and swim to the other side unimpeded. We have a feeling that the reason we got across just fine is because we picked up the Duergar pendants. We land on the beach and approach the stone door, with the same stone dagger symbol as the amulets. [[Furgus]] nicks his finger with an arrow and offers his blood to the door. The door absorbs it and opens. There is a small foyer with braziers and a hallway leading to an altar displaying a large jewelled dagger made of actual gold and gems. On the altar itself is the body of a Knocker. We put on our medallions and walk in. We go down to the altar and see that the Knocker’s heart is carved out, but there is no blood on the altar itself. [[WISC]] recognises this symbol as that of Abbathor, the Dwarven god of greed. He is seen to most Dwarves as a curse more so than a blessing; he has also been known to induce madness. A voice comes from the darkness, “You three will make great sacrifices to him.” We fight an evil priest of Abbathor. After we kill her, we long rest in the chamber with her body.
