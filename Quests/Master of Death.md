# Master of Death

**Status:** active

## Objective

Identify the hooded necromancer who summoned [[Christolf Nightfall]] to an extraction point and who appears to direct the [[Skeletal Hand]].

## Clues & findings

An unidentified male humanoid / human-Elf figure connected to the [[Skeletal Hand]].

### Observed description
- Late 40s to early 50s.
- Slight greying at temples.
- Clean shaven with slight sideburns.
- Green eyes.
- Appeared extremely annoyed when the party interfered with [[Christolf Nightfall]]'s plans.
- Uses advanced magic including Sending and portals/rifts through reality.

### Known statement
He appeared at an extraction rendezvous expecting Christolf and complained that the party had interfered. He then opened a rift and disappeared.

## Recent developments
- [[Session 37]]: Possible lead on Skeletal Hand recruitment in Amberhold: [[Blond Skeletal Hand Recruiter]].
- [[Session 40]]: [[Peter Owens]] confirmed under Zone of Truth that he worked for the Master, who is **male**, recruiting and experimenting for him. As far as Peter knew, the Master has told **no one his true name**.
- [[Session 40]]: Peter's tattoo glowed green and cast Disintegrate on him just as he was about to talk. [[Furgus]] thinks it held a glyph like the mausoleum's teleport trap, triggered by something specific, possibly speaking the Master's name.
- [[Session 40]]: [[Riffle]] worries about the timing: animating [[Corthos]], recruiting to the [[Skeletal Hand]] and building stronger minions, all centred on [[Amberhold]] just before the [[Gauntlet]].
