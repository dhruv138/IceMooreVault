# Hilda's Smelting Project

**Status:** upcoming

## Objective

Protect [[Hilda the Blacksmith]] while she smelts a new kind of metal at [[Hilda's Hammer]], in return for a discount on custom armour.

## Details ([[Session 41]])
- She has been studying the theory of a metal that must be smelted in a special way.
- The process may cause elemental beings to emerge, and she needs them kept off her while she works.
- The party agreed enthusiastically, to do it **after the [[Gauntlet]]**.
