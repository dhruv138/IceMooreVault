# Daggins Murder

## Session 38 lead

[[Atheela]] confirmed that Daggins was a member of the [[Union of Many]], where he was known as Brother Daggins. His fertility-statue collection reflected the order's many love and fertility deities. This explains the gathering beneath [[The Two Tits]] but does not identify his killer.

**Status:** active

Determine who murdered [[Daddy Dildo Daggins]], stole his Axeholm research, and attempted to frame [[Riffle]].
