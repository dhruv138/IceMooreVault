# Missing Sanitation Workers

**Status:** partially resolved

Find three sanitation workers who vanished in the sewers of [[Amberhold]]. Four people sent to look for them have also disappeared. Posted on the city job board for 200 gp ([[Session 36]]).

- [[Session 37]]: A city representative gave the party the job and they accepted.

## Recent developments
- [[Session 39]]: Six of the seven found, chained in old tunnels reached by a hidden stair in the [[Owens Family Mausoleum]]. Four were alive: [[Gertrude Mossbottom]], [[Pim Lark]], and searchers Odo and Fenwick. Two dead workers had been raised as ghouls and were destroyed. A [[Skeletal Hand Acolyte|Skeletal Hand acolyte]] was captured.
- [[Session 39]]: Still missing: the searcher [[Hollis]], taken to the [[Man in the Grey Mask]]. [[Imelda Thorne|Captain Thorne]] will pay the 200 gp reward, plus extra for the searchers, once the foreman confirms events.
