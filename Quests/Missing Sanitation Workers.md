# Missing Sanitation Workers

**Status:** active  
**Location:** Sewers beneath [[Amberhold]]  
**Reward:** 200 gp

An Amberhold city representative asked the party to find three sanitation workers who vanished in the sewers several days before [[Session 37]]. Four additional people sent to search for them also disappeared.

## Objectives

- Enter the sewers.
- Locate the three sanitation workers.
- Locate the four missing searchers.
- Determine what caused the disappearances.
