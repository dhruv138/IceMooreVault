# Gauntlet

## Summary

An upcoming series of magically staged combat encounters at [[Mistra's Academy]]. The party will compete on behalf of the [[Library of Deneir]] and represent both institutions at the associated Gala.

## Known Rules

- Four teams will participate independently rather than fighting one another.
- Five bouts are themed around Beasts, Celestials, Fiends, Elementals, and Fey.
- Each team receives about 10–15 minutes between bouts to reset.
- The party may use one short rest through *catnap* during the five bouts.
- Academy clerics can revive fallen participants, but anyone revived by them is removed from the remaining bouts. A participant revived by a party member may continue.
- A prize is awarded after each bout.
