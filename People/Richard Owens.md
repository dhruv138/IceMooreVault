# Richard Owens

**Lord Richard Owens**, a wealthy [[Amberhold]] merchant and head of the [[Owens Family]]. Father of [[Peter Owens]], husband of [[Gezibel Owens]].

- [[Session 36]]: His recent death has left his ship stuck in port, because its cargo can't be unloaded into his warehouse.
- [[Session 38]]: Rumours say the family cook poisoned him and his wife.
- [[Session 39]]: His coffin in the hidden family crypt was empty, with no smell of decay. Peter called on his "mother and father" to attack the party; Richard was one of the greater zombies. [[Deanira]] pinned him in place with the [[Immovable Rod]], and the party destroyed him.

**Status:** dead; his reanimated body was destroyed ([[Session 39]]). See [[Owens Family Deaths]].
