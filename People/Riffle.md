# Riffle

**Role:** Player character  
**Species:** Kenku  
**Class:** Cleric of [[Deneir]]

## Affiliations
- [[Library of Deneir]]
- [[Department of Acquisitions]]
- Connected to [[Mistra's Academy]]
- [[Talir Rengavi]] is a father-figure.
- [[Hana Goldfirm]] is Riffle's close friend/researcher.

## Backstory
Riffle serves as a clandestine collector of knowledge, artifacts, and secrets for the Library. During a past journey with Talir, a fire injured Riffle while they rescued Talir. Riffle describes the burn as the price paid to become a cleric.

## Major threads
- [[Axeholm]]
- [[Skeletal Hand]]
- [[Master of Death]]
- [[Daddy Dildo Daggins]]
- [[Mystery Stone]]
- Suspicion that the conspiracy may involve [[Amberhold]], [[Mistra's Academy]], or the [[Library of Deneir]] itself.

## Notes
Riffle is highly inquisitive, prone to theft and mischief, and has accumulated a substantial number of secrets.

- In [[Session 37]], learned from [[Ester]] that the Library's robes changed from red to purple 50–60 years ago and that old red robes may have survived, creating a lead in the [[Daggins Murder]].
- Discovered that a sect claiming to worship [[Torm]] has been attacking Amberhold businesses that sell alcohol.
- In [[Session 38]], helped [[Karyn]] with Gala preparations and learned the five themes of the [[Gauntlet]]. Later, [[Talir Rengavi]] accused Riffle of stealing documents from his safe after [[Archivist Siobhan]] claimed to have seen Riffle enter his office.
