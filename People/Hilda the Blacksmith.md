# Hilda the Blacksmith

Dwarven blacksmith who runs [[Hilda's Hammer]] in [[Amberhold]]. Massive arms, burn scars covering her forearms; she trains apprentices. Wife of [[Varis]].

Not to be confused with [[Hilda]], the halfling who runs the [[Black Dragon Inn]].

- [[Session 41]]: [[Theo'din Corvis|Theo]] talked her into **sponsoring the party for the [[Gauntlet]]**. [[Riffle]] asked for 1,500 caltrops; she can supply 100 in time. She offered a discount on custom armour in return for help with [[Hilda's Smelting Project]], which the party agreed to do after the Gauntlet.
