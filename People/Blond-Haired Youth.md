# Blond-Haired Youth

Young man in [[Amberhold]] who appears to recruit people for the [[Skeletal Hand]]. A dead member identified him as **Peter**, though whether that is his true name remains unknown. His true appearance is also uncertain.

## Description

- Appeared to be a young man with blond hair.
- Concealed a Skeletal Hand tattoo beneath his sleeve.
- May use *disguise self*, so the observed appearance may be false.

## Known encounters and reports

- In [[Session 30]], a dead Skeletal Hand member questioned after the confrontation with [[Christolf Nightfall]] said that a blond young man named Peter recruited him at an inn in Amberhold.
- [[Cam Jergins]], the Goliath sailor whom [[WISC]] defeated in an arm-wrestling match, said he copied his Skeletal Hand tattoo from a small blond youth.
- In [[Session 37]], the party followed instructions on a magically concealed Academy scholarship flyer. The youth met them after they ordered a beer, an ale, and wine simultaneously. He said Academy admission was not guaranteed and offered quest work to applicants who were rejected.
- [[Furgus]] glimpsed the tattoo beneath his sleeve.
- He appeared to change his appearance with *disguise self* when leaving the tavern.
- An unidentified black-haired woman followed him outside.
- In [[Session 38]], [[Sabrina]] reported that a young blond man calling himself **Andrew** visited a worker named Angela at the [[Velvet Glove Brothel]]. The party wondered whether this was the same person, but no confirming evidence was found.

## Open questions

- What is his true name and appearance?
- Is he officially connected to [[Mistra's Academy]], or merely using it as a recruitment lure?
- Who was the black-haired woman following him?
- What work does he assign to unsuccessful applicants?
- Is “Andrew” another alias, or an unrelated blond man?
- Is there any connection to Academy graduate student [[Peter Owens]]? They remain unconfirmed as separate identities.
