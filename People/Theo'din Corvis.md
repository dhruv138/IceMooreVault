# Theo'din Corvis

Shadar-kai associated with the [[Raven Queen]]. Joined the party later and investigated northern necromancy. His twin was taken as the price of a deal his mother made with the Raven Queen.

In [[Session 37]], Theo communed with the Raven Queen in a magically dark and silent chamber at her Amberhold temple. He recalled the tenets of the [[Order of Black Feathers]], reaffirmed the party's shared purpose in opposing violations of death, and left one of the [[Cornhusk Dolls]] in the temple.
