# Daddy Dildo Daggins

Collector and researcher in [[Truemetal Halls]]. Investigated [[Axeholm]] and the journal cipher. Murdered in his home; his Axeholm research disappeared.

In [[Session 38]], [[Atheela]] confirmed that Daggins belonged to the [[Union of Many]], where he was known as Brother Daggins. His collection of fertility statues from different cultures reflected the order's devotion to many love and fertility deities.
