# Skeletal Hand Acolyte

Young dark-haired man with a [[Skeletal Hand]] tattoo on his wrist, explicitly **not** the [[Blond Skeletal Hand Recruiter|blond recruiter]]. He was guarding the captives beneath the [[Owens Family Mausoleum]] and raising the dead workers as ghouls. Furgus shot him and WISC brought him down with the [[Javelin of Lightning]] ([[Session 39]]).

He carried the [[Black Iron Key]], a vial of grave dust, 14 gold and the [[Red Robes Ledger Page]].

**Status:** in the custody of [[Imelda Thorne]]'s city watch. The party wants to question him.
