# Aldric Venn

Leader of a group of the [[Sect of Torm]], in white tabards. [[WISC]] and [[Theo'din Corvis|Theo]] watched his men smash a wine shop's bottles. He said drink "dulls the discipline the gods require" and that the city will be "clean before the Gala". Asked why the [[Academy Gala]] matters to them, he only smiled ([[Session 39]]).
