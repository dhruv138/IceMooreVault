# Sabrina

Tiefling bartender at the [[Velvet Glove Brothel]] in [[Amberhold]] and acquaintance of [[Riffle]].

In [[Session 38]], Sabrina met Riffle outside the brothel and reported that a young blond man calling himself **Andrew** had visited a worker named Angela. The party suspects a possible connection to the [[Blond-Haired Youth]], but the identification is unconfirmed.
