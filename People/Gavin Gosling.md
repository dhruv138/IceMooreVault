# Gavin Gosling

Former head of [[Mistra's Academy]]. His death was reported in [[Session 26]], and [[Bianca Onyxcrest]] succeeded him as Headmistress ([[Session 36]]).

## Recent developments
- [[Session 40]]: Called "Gavin Goslin" in the notes. Per [[Talir Rengavi]], the [[Gauntlet]] was his project to show off the Academy's power; it went ahead after his death. [[Hana Goldfirm]] says he was old and his death wasn't unexpected, and **there was no investigation**. [[Riffle]] wonders if it was natural.
