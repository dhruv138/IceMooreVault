# Peter Owens

Graduate student at [[Mistra's Academy]] (School of Conjuration, per [[Session 36]]). Son of the late Lord Richard Owens, the merchant whose death has left a ship stuck in port because its cargo can't be unloaded into his warehouse.

The notes spell the family name both **Owens** ([[Session 36]]) and **Owen** ([[Session 38]]).

- [[Session 36]]: [[Riffle]] remembered the son's name only as "something with a P".
- [[Session 38]]: Both his parents recently died, and he has taken a leave of absence. Students say he has been visiting the [[Owens Family Mausoleum]] more often, which they find strange because he wasn't close to his parents. They describe him as strange, with "icky weird vibes". He lives in an apartment off campus. See [[Owens Family Deaths]].

**Possible link (still unconfirmed after his capture):** the [[Skeletal Hand]] recruiter in [[Session 30]] was described as a blond youth named Peter. See [[Blond Skeletal Hand Recruiter]].

## Recent developments
- [[Session 39]]: **Captured.** He had raised his parents, [[Richard Owens]] and [[Gezibel Owens]], as greater zombies and called on them to attack the party in his laboratory under the [[Owens Warehouse]]. [[Furgus]] shot him with an [[Arrow of the Harpy]], charming him into a stupor; the party destroyed the zombies, and they tied Peter up and gagged him with WISC's socks.
- [[Session 39]]: His notes name [[Deanira]] and [[Coda's Landing]] as a target, and say he "needs a female phase". See [[Peter's Experiments]].
- [[Session 39]]: His spellbook includes *polymorph* but not *disguise self* or *alter self* ([[Peter's Spellbook]]).
- [[Session 39]]: [suspicion] The party called the mausoleum "Owen's" when checking whether the door was locked behind someone, so they took the hooded figure from [[Session 38]] to be Peter.
- [[Session 40]]: Under [[Theo'din Corvis|Theo]]'s Zone of Truth he named himself **Lord Peter Owens** and admitted he **works for the [[Master of Death]]**, finding recruits and running experiments; the captives are test subjects for making bigger and better minions. He suggested his parents created the lab and started the experiments. He denied robbing [[Talir Rengavi]]'s office or ever framing [[Riffle]] or a kenku.
- [[Session 40]]: **Dead.** As [[Furgus]] intimidated him into talking, his tattoo glowed green and a Disintegrate spell reduced him to ash. Theo kept some of the ashes. The party took a [[Ring of Waterwalking]] and a [[Pearl of Power]] from him.
- [[Session 40]]: Talir's cover story: he died resisting a royal arrest (see [[Royal Guard]]). As the Owens heir, his death leaves the family inheritance open.
