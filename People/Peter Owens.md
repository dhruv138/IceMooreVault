# Peter Owens

## Summary

Graduate student at [[Mistra's Academy]] and son of the late merchant Lord Richard Owens, whose death left a ship unable to unload into the family warehouse.

## Current Status

Peter is taking a leave of absence after the recent deaths of both parents. He lives in an apartment rather than on campus.

## Party Knowledge

- Other students describe him as strange and unsettling.
- Rumors suggest the family cook may have poisoned his parents.
- Although reportedly not close to his parents, he has recently visited the [[Owen Family Mausoleum]] more often.
- A hooded figure entered the mausoleum shortly before the party arrived in [[Session 38]].
- Peter is not confirmed to be the [[Blond-Haired Youth]] or the man calling himself Andrew.
