# Zelda

## Summary

Former worker at the [[Velvet Glove Brothel]] who moved to [[Heaven's Touch]] a few weeks before [[Session 38]].

## Party Knowledge

Zelda told [[Riffle]] she wanted a change of scenery and that Heaven's Touch felt nice. [[Theo'din Corvis|Theo]] perceived that she appeared to be affected by the same enchantment aura present in the establishment. His attempt to cast *dispel magic* led staff to remove him before the result could be established.
