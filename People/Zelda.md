# Zelda

Brothel worker who moved from the [[Velvet Glove Brothel]] to [[Heaven's Touch]] a few weeks before [[Session 38]].

- [[Session 38]]: [[Riffle]] recognised her at Heaven's Touch. She said she needed a change of scenery and that it "just felt so nice here". She appeared to be enchanted; [[Theo'din Corvis]] tried to cast Dispel Magic on her. See [[Heaven's Touch Enchantment]].
