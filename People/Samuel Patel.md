# Samuel Patel

Head Cleric at the [[Temple of Mistra]] in [[Amberhold]]. [[Bianca Onyxcrest]] asked him to prepare the spells that cured [[WISC]] and [[Furgus]] of the [[Werebear Curse]].

- [[Session 37]]: Told [[Deanira]] she had a minor curse on her and sent her, with a letter, to the Student Health Clinic, where the [[Mask of the Green Dragon]] was removed.
