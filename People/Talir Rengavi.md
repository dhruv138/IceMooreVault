# Talir Rengavi

Director of the [[Library of Deneir]] and a father figure to [[Riffle]]. He can arrange magical transportation and has access to major institutional resources.

## Recent developments
- [[Session 37]]: Misread Riffle asking him to read a flyer as an admission Riffle can't read, and apologised for failing as a father.
- [[Session 38]]: Very stressed and overworked preparing the [[Academy Gala]] and [[Gauntlet]].
- [[Session 38]]: Sent Riffle an angry Sending demanding the return of documents stolen from his safe, citing Archivist [[Siobhan]] as a witness. See [[Talir's Stolen Documents]].
- [[Session 39]]: Riffle sent him a letter by Theo's raven after Peter's capture. Its contents aren't recorded.
- [[Session 40]]: Replied by [[Conji]], warning of kingdom-shaking stakes, then arrived invisibly at the [[Owens Warehouse]]. Explained the politics of a dead Lord (see [[Ambercrown Kingdom]]) and arranged a cover story through a contact in the [[Royal Guard]]. Revealed what was taken from his secret safe ([[Talir's Stolen Documents]]) and that the [[Gauntlet]] was [[Gavin Gosling]]'s project, which he opposed.
- [[Session 40]]: That night someone left Riffle a note asking what secrets Talir is keeping. See [[Pillow Note]].
- [[Session 41]]: When [[Royal Guard|royal guards]] came for the party, he answered Riffle's Sending with "proceed."
