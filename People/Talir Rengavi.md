# Talir Rengavi

Director of the [[Library of Deneir]] and a father figure to [[Riffle]]. He can arrange magical transportation and has access to major institutional resources.

In [[Session 37]], a misunderstanding led Talir to believe Riffle could not read; he apologized for having failed them as a father before the confusion was resolved.

In [[Session 38]], Talir sent Riffle an unusually angry message demanding the return of documents allegedly stolen from his safe that afternoon. He said [[Archivist Siobhan]] had seen Riffle enter his office.
