# Peter's Spellbook

Spellbook the party found on [[Peter Owens]] after capturing him ([[Session 39]]). It includes **polymorph**, but not **disguise self** or **alter self**.

[inference] Those are the spells someone might use to impersonate people. That matters because of the impersonations the party has run into: the fake [[Hector]] ([[Session 25]]), the figure dressed as [[Riffle]] in the [[Daggins Murder]], and the recruiter believed to have used Disguise Self ([[Session 37]]). The session doesn't say what the party concluded.
