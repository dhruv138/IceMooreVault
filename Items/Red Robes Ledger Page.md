# Red Robes Ledger Page

Folded page of [[Library of Deneir]] letterhead from the Library's storeroom ledger, found on the [[Skeletal Hand Acolyte]] ([[Session 39]]). It lists "Ceremonial robes (red, old pattern), 12, Storeroom B, sub-basement". Someone circled the line and wrote "11" beside it. [[Storeroom B]] did hold only 11. See [[Missing Red Robe]].
