# Mask of the Green Dragon

Ancient green dragon-shaped wooden mask found in the flooded Dragon League fortress. Transmutation magic.

In [[Session 37]], [[Samuel Patel]] identified a minor curse affecting [[Deanira]]. When a healer at the [[Student Health Clinic]] removed the mask, Deanira immediately felt much better about [[WISC]], revealing that the curse had been influencing her attitude toward him.
