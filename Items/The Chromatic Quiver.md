# The Chromatic Quiver

A quiver made of a quilt of chromatic dragon skins, embedded with magical properties. The Emberstone family gave it to [[Furgus]] for rescuing [[Elzock Emberstone]] ([[Session 08]]).

**Carried by:** [[Furgus]], attuned ([[Furgus Character Sheet]]). Fired with a longbow, his attacks are +13 to hit for 1d8+6 piercing, one better than an ordinary longbow.
