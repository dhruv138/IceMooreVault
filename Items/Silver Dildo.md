# Silver Dildo

## Summary

A silver fertility object that [[Daddy Dildo Daggins]] gave [[Deanira]] as collateral while he worked on deciphering the journal connected to [[Axeholm]]. Deanira continues to safeguard it after his death.

## Session History

### [[Session 38]]

Deanira showed the item to [[Atheela]], linking it to Daggins's membership in the [[Union of Many]] and his interest in fertility objects from different cultures.
