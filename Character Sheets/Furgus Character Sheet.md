### What the notes add to the vault

- **Party classes:** [[WISC]] is a bugbear **barbarian** played by Taylor; [[Deanira]] is a satyr **monk** played by Lyra.
- **Where the party came from:** "an alpine valley two days' trip from Venragh", i.e. [[Venburg]]. They bought 20 sheep and ended up with a flock of 44, then 48, and sold them in [[Truemetal Halls]] at half the purchase price.
- **[[Truemetal Halls]]:** stands at Darkhammer Peak; founded by Lothar and Astrid Darkhammer, whose statues stand by the gate.
- **[[Hambrick Onyxcrest]]'s shop** is run by a bored gnome.
- **[[Junk in the Trunk]]:** its stock changes, and you can visit once a day to see what's new.
- **The half-elf of the [[Shredding Dude]] quest** was wearing a hi-vis green jacket and carried an "anti-knockout" item and warm boots. His body should be near the [[MurderDrum]], north of Truemetal Halls.
- **The oil of slipperiness and the [[Ring of Jumping]]** were bought "during festival". The 22 July 2025 game night is [[Session 10]], where the party visits the monthly market. Oil of slipperiness no longer appears in the inventory.
- **[[Session 03]]'s date:** the notes date it **28 February 2025**. The vault's session title says 29 February 2025, which isn't a real date, so the 28th is probably right.
- **The Kohlemine dwarves:** the notes call them "kholmine dwarves", i.e. the [[Black Hand Dwarves]] of [[Kohlemine]].
- **"1 day of poison vulnerability left"** isn't dated or explained. It may relate to the poisonings in [[Session 14]] or [[Session 24]]; that's a guess.
